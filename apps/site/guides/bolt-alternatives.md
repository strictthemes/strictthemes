Bolt.new builds an app from a prompt in the browser. It prices that work in tokens: the free plan gives 1 million a month, at most 300,000 a day, and Pro is $25 a month with a custom domain. Reasons to look elsewhere include the daily limit ending a session, wanting to pay by credits or a flat plan, and wanting to own the code in an editor.

This guide covers six alternatives: three browser builders like Bolt (Lovable, Base44 and v0), two coding agents for your editor (Cursor and Devin Desktop), and one visual website editor (Siter.io). We read every price below on each tool's own pricing page on 19 and 20 November 2026.

![Bolt.new's pricing page, with Free at $0 and Pro at $25 a month billed monthly.](/guides/bolt-alternatives/bolt.jpg "Bolt.new's pricing page, 19 November 2026")

## TL;DR

- **A free plan with a database and sign-in:** Base44. Free has 25 message credits a month and up to 5 apps; paid plans start at $20 a month.
- **Best for a team building an app together:** Lovable. Free allows unlimited collaborators; Pro is $25 a month for 100 credits.
- **Best if you deploy to Vercel:** v0. Free has $5 of one-time credits; Plus is $30 per user a month.
- **Best if you want to own and edit the code:** Cursor, from $8 a month, or Devin Desktop, formerly Windsurf, from $20.
- **Best for a marketing website you edit by hand:** Siter.io, from $7 a month billed yearly.
- **To host the result somewhere else:** a static site can go on a flat-price host such as Static.app, or a free one such as Cloudflare Pages.

## Browser app builders

### 1. Lovable: best for a team building an app together

[Lovable](https://lovable.dev/) builds apps from prompts and hosts them. Even the free plan allows unlimited collaborators, and every plan lists Lovable Cloud.

![Lovable's pricing page, with Free, Pro at 25 and Business at 50 a month, shown in euros for our location.](/guides/bolt-alternatives/lovable.jpg "Lovable's pricing page, shown in euros, 19 November 2026")

Lovable's docs list prices in US dollars. Pro is $25 a month for 100 credits, or $250 a year, and Business is $50 a month for 100 credits, adding SSO and a team workspace. Credit tiers go up to 3,000 a month. The free plan gives 5 build credits a day, capped at 30 a month. Free apps run on lovable.app addresses with Lovable's badge; Pro adds custom domains, removes the badge and lets unused credits roll over. Its pricing page showed us euros, so check the currency for where you are.

What it does not do: credits are the whole budget, so check your balance as you go. The free plan's 30 build credits a month is enough to try it, not to build much.

**Good for:** a team that wants to build and host an app in one tool.

### 2. Base44: best for a free plan with a backend

[Base44](https://base44.com/) is an AI app builder with a backend, database, sign-in and analytics included on every plan. Its FAQ says you own your code and that two-way GitHub sync exports the full source to your own repository.

![Base44's pricing page, with Elite at $160, Pro at $80 and Builder at $40 a month billed annually.](/guides/bolt-alternatives/base44.jpg "Base44's pricing page, 20 November 2026")

Free is $0 with 25 message credits a month and up to 5 apps. Starter is $20 a month, or $16 billed annually, for 100 message credits and a custom domain. The annual cards also list a free domain for 1 year. Builder is $50 or $40 for 250 credits. Pro is $100 or $80 for 500 credits, and Elite is $200 or $160 for 1,200. Annual billing saves 20%. Each plan also has integration credits.

What it does not do: the cards list GitHub integration only from Builder up, though the FAQ describes the sync in general, so check before you rely on it. Third-party services you connect bill you separately. It is a hosted platform, so your app runs there unless you export it.

**Good for:** a first app with a database and sign-in, where you want a free plan to start on.

### 3. v0: best if you already deploy to Vercel

[v0](https://v0.app/) is Vercel's AI builder. It writes React and Next.js code and deploys to Vercel.

![v0's pricing page, with Free at $0 and Plus at $30 per user a month.](/guides/bolt-alternatives/v0.jpg "v0's pricing page, 19 November 2026")

Free is $0 with $5 of one-time credits for new users and 7 messages a day. Plus is $30 per user a month with $30 of monthly credits per user and $2 of free daily credits on login. Business is $100 per user a month. Models are billed by tokens, from $0.10 per million input tokens on v0 Mini to $8 on v0 Max Fast.

What it does not do: the free daily message limit is small, and the app deploys to Vercel, whose free Hobby plan is for personal, non-commercial use. See [Vercel pricing in 2026](/guides/vercel-pricing/) and [v0 alternatives in 2026](/guides/v0-alternatives/).

**Good for:** developers who want React code on a host they know.

## Coding agents in an editor

These work on code on your computer. You own the project and host it anywhere, but they ask more of you than Bolt does.

### 4. Cursor: best if you want to own and edit the code

[Cursor](https://cursor.com/) is an editor with a coding agent, now sold as part of The X Subscription. Free has limited usage, Plus is $8 a month with Cursor's own models only, and Premium is $30 a month with third-party models billed at API list price.

![Cursor's pricing page, with Free at $0, Plus at $8, Premium at $30 and Super at $100 a month.](/guides/bolt-alternatives/cursor.jpg "Cursor's pricing page, 19 November 2026")

What it does not do: it does not host anything, and you need to know enough to run and deploy a project. Usage resets weekly. See [Cursor pricing in 2026](/guides/cursor-pricing/).

**Good for:** developers who want Bolt's speed on a codebase they control.

### 5. Devin Desktop (Windsurf): best for frontier models in a $20 plan

[Devin Desktop](https://devin.ai/pricing) is the new name for Windsurf, from Cognition. Free has a light quota. Pro is $20 a month with frontier models and cloud agents. Max is $200 a month. Teams is a flat $80 a month for up to 100 members, plus usage billed as shared on-demand credits.

![Devin's pricing page for individuals, with Free at $0, Pro at $20 and Max at $200 a month.](/guides/bolt-alternatives/devin.jpg "Devin pricing for individuals, 19 November 2026")

What it does not do: it does not host, and the page does not say how large each plan's usage allowance is. See [Windsurf pricing in 2026](/guides/windsurf-pricing/).

**Good for:** developers who want frontier models included in one plan.

## An AI website editor

### 6. Siter.io: best for a marketing website you edit by hand afterwards

[Siter.io](https://siter.io/) is a visual website editor. You place elements anywhere on a free-form canvas, or describe a page and its AI builds it there as editable elements rather than code.

![Siter.io's pricing page, with Solo at $7, Plus at $12 and Pro at $24 a month billed yearly.](/guides/bolt-alternatives/siter.jpg "Siter.io's pricing page, shown with yearly billing, 19 November 2026")

Solo is $7 a month billed yearly, or $8 month to month, for 1 website, 600 AI credits a month and 25 GB of traffic. Plus is $12, or $14 monthly, for 3 websites and 800 AI credits. Pro is $24, or $28 monthly, for 5 websites, 2,000 AI credits and HTML download. Its pricing page showed a Black Friday sale of 35% off.

What it does not do: it builds websites, not apps, so there is no database or app logic like Bolt's. There is no free plan: every plan starts with a 3-day trial that needs a card.

**Good for:** a landing page or company site that a non-developer will keep editing.

## Where to host what you build

Lovable, Base44, Bolt.new and Siter.io host for you. If what you built is a static site, HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no server, you can move it to any static host and stop paying for the builder:

| Host | Price | Custom domain | Metered | Git needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Static.app | From $6 a month, or $60 a year | Yes | No, unlimited traffic | No |
| Cloudflare Pages | Free plan | Yes | No bandwidth limit listed | No, direct upload works |
| GitHub Pages | Free with public repositories | Yes | Soft limit of 100 GB a month | Yes |

[Static.app](https://static.app/) takes a ZIP or a folder and serves it with SSL, a custom domain, forms and analytics at a flat price from $6 a month. It has [a page on hosting sites built with Bolt.new](https://static.app/bolt-new-hosting). It runs no server code, so an app that needs its builder's database or functions will not work there, and it has no free plan, only a 7-day trial. Until 1 December, its [Black Friday sale](/guides/black-friday-2026-deals/) takes 35% off the first payment. [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/) is free for static files. [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) is free for public repositories.

For more on hosting AI-generated sites, see [hosting an AI-generated website](https://bootstrap.build/articles/host-ai-generated-website/) on bootstrap.build. Before you share the link, [check the live page against your design](/guides/publish-and-check-live-page/).

## FAQ

### What is the best free alternative to Bolt.new?

The browser builders count differently, so they are hard to rank. Bolt.new's free plan gives 1 million tokens a month, at most 300,000 a day. Base44's gives 25 message credits a month, and Lovable's gives 5 build credits a day, up to 30 a month. Cursor and Devin Desktop also have free plans for coding in an editor.

### Is Lovable better than Bolt.new?

They do the same job with different pricing. Bolt.new meters tokens and Lovable meters credits. Both have a Pro plan from $25 a month with a custom domain.

### Is there a cheaper alternative to Bolt.new Pro?

Base44's Starter is $20 a month, or $16 billed annually, and Siter.io's Solo is $7 a month billed yearly, but they differ: Base44 builds apps and Siter.io builds websites. Compare what each plan includes, not only the headline price.

### Can I move a Bolt.new site to another host?

If your project is a static site, yes: export the built files and upload them to a static host such as Static.app, Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages. An app that needs Bolt's database or server functions needs a host that runs them.

### Is there a Bolt.new alternative that is not for developers?

Siter.io is a visual website editor with AI, where the result stays editable on a canvas instead of as code. It builds websites rather than apps.

### Are Bolt.new and v0 the same?

No. Both build from prompts, but Bolt hosts the result itself and meters tokens, while v0 deploys to Vercel and bills credits for model usage.
