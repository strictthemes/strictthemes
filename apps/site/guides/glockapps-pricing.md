GlockApps sells email deliverability tests. Its main test, Inbox Insight, sends your email to a seed list and reports where it landed at each mailbox provider: inbox, tabs, spam or missing. Each test uses one spam test credit. The paid plans bundle credits with DMARC reports, uptime monitors and IP reputation monitors.

The number people notice is 360. That is how many credits the Essential plan includes, but only when you pay for a year. It works out to 30 tests a month. This guide explains what each plan costs, what one credit buys, and what the same testing costs with other tools. We read every number on GlockApps' pricing page on 25 November 2026.

![GlockApps' pricing page with annual billing, showing Free at $0, Essential at $59, Growth at $99 and Enterprise at $129 a month.](/guides/glockapps-pricing/glockapps-annual.jpg "GlockApps' pricing page, billed annually, 25 November 2026")

## TL;DR

- **Essential is $59 a month billed yearly ($708 a year) or $85 a month billed monthly.** Yearly gives 360 credits for the year. Monthly gives 30 credits a month. Both work out to 30 tests a month.
- **One credit is one test.** You send one email to the full seed list, or a shorter one, and get one report.
- **Per test, Essential costs about $1.97 billed yearly and $2.83 billed monthly.** Larger plans cost less per test, down to about $0.86 on Enterprise billed yearly.
- **The free plan has 2 credits.** GlockApps' free checker page calls them "your first 2 tests".
- **Credit packs cost $16.99 for 3, $47.99 for 10 and $75.99 for 20,** and they expire after 1 to 3 months.
- **Automatic tests use credits too,** one per run.

## What 360 credits really buys

**One credit is one Inbox Insight test.** GlockApps' FAQ says a test is one email sent to the full seed list or a shorter one, followed by one delivery report. However many seed addresses the test reaches, it counts as one test.

So the 360 credits on yearly Essential are 360 tests a year. The monthly plan gives the same 30 a month. The yearly totals on the page are twelve months of the monthly amounts, for DMARC messages too: 600,000 a year against 50,000 a month.

**What one test costs, by plan:**

| Plan | Billed yearly | Billed monthly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | $708 ÷ 360 = about $1.97 | $85 ÷ 30 = about $2.83 |
| Growth | $1,188 ÷ 1,080 = about $1.10 | $142 ÷ 90 = about $1.58 |
| Enterprise | $1,548 ÷ 1,800 = about $0.86 | $185 ÷ 150 = about $1.23 |

These figures count the whole plan price against tests. Each plan also includes other tools, below, so if you use them, a test costs you less than this.

**Automatic tests are not free.** The FAQ says every run of an automatic test takes one credit. A daily automatic test uses about 30 credits a month, which is all of Essential.

## Hidden costs and limits

- **The 360 is a yearly number.** Essential gives 30 tests a month, whether you pay yearly or monthly.
- **Monthly billing costs about 44% more than yearly.** $85 against $59 a month on Essential.
- **Essential has one user.** A second person needs Growth, at $99 a month billed yearly.
- **Automatic tests draw from the same credits** as manual ones.
- **Pack credits expire** after 1, 2 or 3 months.
- **Help fixing a spam problem is a separate service.** The FAQ points to GlockApps' deliverability consultation for that.

## A worked example

A small team sends one newsletter a week and tests each send twice, before and after a fix. That is about 8 tests a month.

- **GlockApps Essential:** $59 a month billed yearly, or $85 monthly. They use 8 of 30 credits.
- **GlockApps credit packs:** a 20-credit pack at $75.99 lasts about 2.5 months at 8 tests a month, inside its 3-month validity. That is about $30 a month.
- **Unspam:** 3 inbox placements a month are free, then $0.50 each on the Custom plan, from a $5 monthly minimum. 8 placements a month cost $5, the minimum.

Now an agency running 90 placement tests a month:

- **GlockApps Growth:** $99 a month billed yearly, or $142 monthly, with 90 credits a month.
- **Unspam:** 90 placements cost $45 a month in its pricing calculator. 150 placements cost $72.50, because rates drop with volume.

The two tests are not identical. GlockApps doesn't publish its seed list size on its pricing page. Unspam sends each test to 10 seed mailboxes at 7 providers. Compare the providers each one covers before you choose.

## GlockApps alternatives on price

### Unspam

[Unspam](https://unspam.email/) runs inbox placement tests and spam tests, priced per test. A placement test sends your email to 10 seed mailboxes at 7 providers and reports where each copy landed. A spam test checks SPF, DKIM, DMARC, content, blacklists and HTML.

![Unspam's pricing page, with the No account and Free plans, the Free plan including 21 tests a month.](/guides/glockapps-pricing/unspam.jpg "Unspam's pricing page, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** the free plan includes 21 tests a month: 5 spam tests, 3 inbox placements, 3 device previews, 5 screenshots and 5 heatmaps. The Custom plan charges $0.50 per inbox placement and $0.18 per spam test, from a $5 monthly minimum, with rates that drop as volume grows. Every paid plan has a 14-day refund.

**Limitations:** no uptime monitors. DMARC monitoring, the API and the MCP server need the Custom plan. Check that its 7 providers include the ones your list uses.

**Good for:** teams that test a few campaigns a month and don't want a yearly commitment.

### mail-tester.com

[mail-tester.com](https://www.mail-tester.com/) scores one message. You send an email to the address it gives you and get a spam score with notes on what to fix. It does not test placement at mailbox providers.

![mail-tester.com's price list, with 50 tests a month for $10, 500 for $25 and 2,000 for $80.](/guides/glockapps-pricing/mail-tester.jpg "mail-tester.com's prices, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** manual tests on the website need no account. Monthly plans are $10 for 50 tests, $25 for 500, $80 for 2,000 and $250 for 7,500, before VAT. They add an API, white-labelling and test history.

**Good for:** a quick check of authentication and content before a send, or building scores into your own app.

## Which GlockApps plan should you choose?

- **You test now and then:** start with the 2 free credits, then a credit pack. Or try a pay-per-test tool first.
- **You test a few times a week:** Essential. Pay yearly if you are sure, because monthly costs about 44% more.
- **Two or more people need access:** Growth. Essential has one user.
- **You run automatic tests for many clients:** Enterprise, or a custom plan. Daily tests use credits fast.
- **You only need a spam score, not placement:** mail-tester.com is cheaper.

## FAQ

### How much does GlockApps cost?

Essential is $59 a month billed yearly ($708 a year) or $85 billed monthly. Growth is $99 or $142. Enterprise is $129 or $185. There is a free plan with 2 spam test credits.

### What are GlockApps credits?

Spam test credits. One credit runs one Inbox Insight test: one email sent to the seed list and one report. Automatic tests also take one credit per run.

### Is 360 credits a month?

No. Essential billed yearly includes 360 credits for the year. Billed monthly, it includes 30 credits a month. Both are 30 tests a month.

### Does GlockApps have a free plan?

Yes. It includes 2 spam test credits, 10,000 DMARC messages, 5 uptime monitors and 1 IP reputation monitor, for one user.

### Can I buy GlockApps credits without a subscription?

Yes. Packs cost $16.99 for 3 credits, $47.99 for 10 and $75.99 for 20. They are valid for 1, 2 and 3 months.

### Is there a cheaper alternative to GlockApps?

For a few tests a month, a pay-per-test tool costs less. Unspam includes 3 inbox placements a month free, then charges $0.50 each from a $5 monthly minimum. For a spam score alone, mail-tester.com starts at $10 for 50 tests a month.
