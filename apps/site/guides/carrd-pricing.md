Carrd builds one-page websites, and it is priced by the year, not the month. The free plan publishes up to 3 sites on Carrd's own addresses, with Carrd branding. Pro Lite is $9 a year, Pro Standard is $19 a year and Pro Plus is $49 a year. Each Pro tier costs more as you add sites: Pro Standard is $19 for 10 sites and $599 for 1,000.

This guide is for anyone building a landing page, link page or small site with Carrd. We read every number below on Carrd's Pro page on 19 November 2026.

![Carrd's Compare Plans table, with Pro Lite at $9, Pro Standard at $19 and Pro Plus at $49 a year.](/guides/carrd-pricing/carrd.jpg "Carrd's Pro plans, 19 November 2026")

## TL;DR

- **Free publishes up to 3 sites** on Carrd's addresses, with "Made with Carrd" branding.
- **Pro Lite is $9 a year** for 3 sites with no branding, but no custom domain.
- **Pro Standard is $19 a year for 10 sites,** with a custom domain, forms, widgets, embeds and analytics.
- **Pro Plus is $49 a year for 25 sites,** adding advanced forms, site downloads, redirects and password protection.
- **More sites cost more.** Pro Standard runs up to $599 a year for 1,000 sites, Pro Plus to $999.
- **Pro has a 7-day free trial,** with no card.

All Pro tiers add high-quality and larger images and videos, no element limit, premium templates, site icons, share images and QR codes. Pro Lite's "premium URLs" are unbranded Carrd addresses, such as a .crd.co address, not your own domain.

You can renew yearly or pay as you go, with PayPal or a card.

## What the free plan includes

Carrd's free plan lets you build and publish up to 3 sites on Carrd's addresses. Its Pro page lists custom domains, forms, embeds, analytics, removing the "Made with Carrd" branding and more than three sites as Pro features. So a free Carrd site lives at a Carrd address and shows Carrd's branding.

The 7-day Pro trial lets you try custom domains and advanced forms without a card.

## Is Carrd cheap?

Very, for what it does. Pro Standard at $19 a year is less than two dollars a month for a one-page site on your own domain with forms. The trade-off is that Carrd builds single pages in its editor. If you need several pages, or you have a site built elsewhere, such as with a coding agent, you need a host instead.

### Static.app

[Static.app](https://static.app/) hosts static sites at a flat price. You upload a ZIP or a folder with as many pages as you need, and the site is live with SSL. Starter is $6 a month, or $60 a year, for 2 sites and 500 MB of storage. Medium is $12 a month for 7 sites and 3 GB. Large is $18 a month for 30 sites and 10 GB. Every paid plan lists unlimited traffic, a custom domain, forms and analytics. It has an API and an MCP server, so a coding agent can publish to it.

![Static.app's pricing page, with a 7-day free trial, and the Starter plan at $5 a month when billed yearly.](/guides/carrd-pricing/static-app.jpg "Static.app's pricing page, shown with yearly billing, 18 November 2026")

What it does not do: it has no page builder like Carrd's, so you bring a finished site. It costs more than Carrd: $60 a year against $19 for Pro Standard. There is no free plan, only a 7-day trial. Until 1 December, its [Black Friday sale](/guides/black-friday-2026-deals/) takes 35% off the first payment.

**Good for:** a multi-page site, or one built outside Carrd, that needs forms and a domain without a server.

### Cloudflare Pages

[Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/) has a free plan with 500 builds a month, 20,000 files per site and 25 MiB per file. Its docs say requests for static files are free and unlimited.

![The Cloudflare Pages home page, headed Build and deploy full-stack applications instantly.](/guides/carrd-pricing/cloudflare-pages.jpg "Cloudflare Pages, 18 November 2026")

**Good for:** developers who want free hosting and are at home in Cloudflare's dashboard. See [Cloudflare Pages and Workers pricing in 2026](/guides/cloudflare-pages-pricing/).

### GitHub Pages

[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) serves a site from a GitHub repository, free for public repositories, with a soft limit of 100 GB of bandwidth a month. GitHub says it is not for sites mainly for commercial transactions or software as a service.

![The GitHub Pages documentation home page.](/guides/carrd-pricing/github-pages.jpg "GitHub Pages documentation, 18 November 2026")

**Good for:** personal sites and project pages already on GitHub.

## Which Carrd plan should you choose?

- **A link page or a test:** Free, on a Carrd address.
- **No branding, but a Carrd address is fine:** Pro Lite at $9 a year.
- **A landing page on your own domain with a signup form:** Pro Standard at $19 a year.
- **Many sites, password-protected pages or redirects:** Pro Plus, sized to your number of sites.
- **A multi-page site:** a static host, since Carrd builds single pages.

## FAQ

### Is Carrd free?

Yes. The free plan lets you build and publish up to 3 sites on Carrd's addresses, with Carrd branding. Custom domains, forms and analytics need Pro.

### How much does Carrd Pro cost?

Pro Lite starts at $9 a year, Pro Standard at $19 a year and Pro Plus at $49 a year. Prices rise with the number of sites, up to $599 a year for 1,000 sites on Pro Standard and $999 on Pro Plus.

### Which Carrd plan includes a custom domain?

Pro Standard and Pro Plus. Pro Lite removes the branding and gives unbranded Carrd addresses, but not your own domain.

### Is Carrd billed monthly?

No. Carrd's Pro plans are priced per year, and you can renew automatically each year or pay as you go.

### Does Carrd have a free trial of Pro?

Yes. Carrd offers a 7-day Pro trial, with features such as custom domains and advanced forms, and no payment or card needed.

### What does Carrd Pro Plus add over Pro Standard?

Advanced forms, site downloads, redirects, password protection and more advanced settings, such as variables, site files and canonical URLs. It starts at 25 sites.
