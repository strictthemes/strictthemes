Warmy sells email warm-up as part of a larger deliverability platform. Plans start at $49 a month and are priced by the volume of warm-up emails you need, not by mailbox. That works for some teams. Others leave because they want a price per mailbox they can work out before a sales call, or because they only need warm-up and not the rest of the platform.

This guide is for people moving off Warmy. It compares five alternatives on what you actually pay for 1, 3 and 10 mailboxes, and on what each plan includes. We read every price on the vendor's own pricing page on 25 November 2026, leaving out Black Friday and first-month discounts. For a broader look at warm-up tools and how they differ from placement tests, see [email warm-up tools in 2026](/guides/email-warm-up-tools/).

![Warmy's pricing page, listing what every plan includes: email verification, DMARC tracking, a deliverability tester, a reputation tracker and Google Postmaster data.](/guides/warmy-alternatives/warmy.jpg "Warmy's pricing page, 25 November 2026")

## TL;DR

- **Cheapest per mailbox:** Warmup Inbox, from $15 per inbox a month for 75 warm-up emails a day, with a volume discount from 6 inboxes.
- **Best for warming a few mailboxes well:** MailReach, at €25 per mailbox a month with 20 spam test credits included.
- **Best for a small team that wants expert calls:** Mailwarm, from $69 a month billed yearly for 1 mailbox, with a monthly call with a deliverability expert.
- **Best if you also send cold email:** Instantly, from $47 a month with unlimited email accounts and unlimited warm-up.
- **Best if you already use lemlist:** its Email plan includes warm-up, from 55€ a month billed yearly.
- **Test before and after:** warm-up does not tell you where your mail lands. Run a placement test, for example with Unspam's free plan, before you switch and again a few weeks later.

## What Warmy costs in 2026

Warmy's pricing page shows three plan types: B2B senders, B2C senders and a custom plan. All three say "plans starting from $49 / month", with a 7-day free trial and no card. Pricing is by the volume of warm-up emails Warmy generates for you.

Every plan lists the same features:

- warm-up through a network Warmy puts at more than 1 million mailboxes, plus more than 500,000 US seed mailboxes;
- shared placement test credits;
- an AI assistant called Adeline;
- email verification, DMARC monitoring, a reputation tracker and Google Postmaster data;
- full API access and a dedicated deliverability expert with 24/7 support.

Warmy's FAQ says you can invite as many users as you need, and that they share the plan's mailboxes and monthly quotas.

## Why people look for an alternative

- **No price per mailbox.** The page gives a starting price and says pricing depends on warm-up volume. You find your real price on the trial or with Warmy's team.
- **The placement test allowance is not published.** The page tells you to confirm your monthly test credits with Warmy's team.
- **They only need warm-up.** If you already have verification, DMARC monitoring and placement tests elsewhere, you pay for features you don't use.

## 1. MailReach: best for warming a few mailboxes well

[MailReach](https://www.mailreach.co/pricing) prices warm-up per mailbox. Its All-In-One plan combines the warmer with a spam tester.

![MailReach's pricing page, with a slider for the number of mailboxes and a price of €25 per mailbox a month.](/guides/warmy-alternatives/mailreach.jpg "MailReach's pricing page, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** €25 per mailbox a month, excluding tax. Yearly billing saves 20%. The plan includes 20 spam test credits, warm-up of up to 100 emails a day per mailbox, a reputation dashboard, and health checks for blacklists, SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Agencies can ask for custom pricing.

**Limitations:** the per-mailbox price adds up past a handful of mailboxes. The page shows the price for 1 mailbox, and you see your total by moving its slider. It works with Google, Outlook and any provider that supports SMTP.

**Good for:** founders and small sales teams warming 1 to 5 mailboxes who want a spam test in the same tool.

## 2. Warmup Inbox: cheapest per mailbox

[Warmup Inbox](https://warmupinbox.com/pricing) is a warm-up tool priced per inbox, with three tiers by daily volume.

![Warmup Inbox's pricing page, with Basic at $15, Pro at $49 and Max at $79 per inbox a month.](/guides/warmy-alternatives/warmupinbox.jpg "Warmup Inbox's pricing page, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** per inbox a month, list price:

| Plan | Per inbox | Warm-up emails a day | Billed yearly, 1 inbox |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $15 | 75 | $180 |
| Pro | $49 | 250 | $588 |
| Max | $79 | 1,000 | $948 |

The page says a volume discount starts at 6 inboxes, and yearly billing saves 20%. Every plan has a 7-day trial with no card. Basic includes spam and category monitoring, a reputation check and a developer API. Pro adds your own templates, warm-up in other languages and per-provider warm-up.

**Limitations:** Basic sends 75 warm-up emails a day, so faster ramps need Pro or Max. It has no placement test credits or DMARC monitoring of the kind Warmy bundles.

**Good for:** teams with many mailboxes who want the lowest price per mailbox.

## 3. Mailwarm: best for a small team that wants expert calls

[Mailwarm](https://www.mailwarm.com/pricing) sells plans that include a set number of mailboxes, users and calls with a deliverability expert.

![Mailwarm's pricing page, with Starter at $69, Growth at $159 and Scale at $479 a month, billed yearly.](/guides/warmy-alternatives/mailwarm.jpg "Mailwarm's pricing page, billed yearly, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:**

| Plan | Monthly | Billed yearly, per month | Mailboxes | Users | Warm-up emails a day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $79 | $69 | 1 | 1 | 50 |
| Growth | $189 | $159 | 3 | 2 | 200 |
| Scale | $549 | $479 | 10 | 5 | 500 |

Starter includes 1 expert call a month and Growth includes 4. Scale has calls on demand. Every plan covers B2B and B2C warm-up, spam score monitoring, warm-up by provider and tools to fix authentication. A Tailored plan with unlimited mailboxes is priced on request.

**Limitations:** the most expensive per mailbox here. Users are capped by plan.

**Good for:** a small team that wants a person to review its setup every month, not just an automated warmer.

## 4. Instantly: best if you also send cold email

[Instantly](https://instantly.ai/pricing) is a cold email platform. Every Outreach plan includes unlimited email accounts and unlimited warm-up.

![Instantly's pricing page, with Growth at $47, Hypergrowth at $107 and Light Speed at $358 a month.](/guides/warmy-alternatives/instantly.jpg "Instantly's pricing page, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** Growth is $47 a month, with 1,000 uploaded contacts and 5,000 emails a month. Hypergrowth is $107 a month, with 25,000 contacts and 125,000 emails. Light Speed is $358 a month. All include unlimited email accounts and unlimited warm-up. Enterprise is custom.

**Limitations:** you pay for a sending platform. If you send from another tool, you are buying features you won't use.

**Good for:** teams that run cold email and want warm-up and sending in one place, for any number of mailboxes.

## 5. lemlist: best if you already use lemlist

[lemlist](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing) is a sales outreach platform. Its warm-up, lemwarm, is part of the Email plan's warm-up and deliverability hub.

![lemlist's pricing page, with the Email plan at 55€ and Multichannel at 87€ a month, billed yearly.](/guides/warmy-alternatives/lemlist.jpg "lemlist's pricing page, billed yearly, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** the Email plan is 69€ a month, or 55€ billed yearly, with unlimited users and email senders and 50,000 emails a month. Multichannel is 109€ a month, or 87€ billed yearly, and adds LinkedIn, SMS and calling. Quarterly billing saves 10%. There is a free trial.

**Limitations:** like Instantly, it is a full outreach platform. Prices are in euros.

**Good for:** teams already sending from lemlist, who can drop a separate warm-up bill.

## Test before and after you switch

Warm-up builds a sending history. It does not tell you where your campaigns land. Before you cancel Warmy, run an inbox placement test on a real campaign, and run the same test a few weeks after you move. If placement drops, you know the change had an effect.

[Unspam](https://unspam.email/) does this without a warm-up subscription. It sends your email to test mailboxes at Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo and other providers and reports whether each copy landed in the inbox, a tab or spam. Its free plan includes 3 placement tests a month. Beyond that, placement tests are $0.50 each, from a $5 monthly minimum. Unspam does not warm up mailboxes.

## Which Warmy alternative should you choose?

- **You warm many mailboxes and want the lowest price:** Warmup Inbox, with its volume discount from 6 inboxes.
- **You warm 1 to 5 mailboxes and want a spam test included:** MailReach.
- **You want an expert to check your setup each month:** Mailwarm.
- **You send cold email anyway:** Instantly or lemlist, with warm-up included.
- **You use Warmy's verification, DMARC and placement tests:** stay, or price each of those separately before you switch. None of the five above includes all of them.

## FAQ

### How much does Warmy cost?

Warmy's plans start at $49 a month. The price depends on the volume of warm-up emails you need, and the page doesn't list prices by mailbox. There is a 7-day free trial with no card.

### What is the cheapest Warmy alternative?

Per mailbox, Warmup Inbox, from $15 per inbox a month on its Basic plan. If you send cold email from Instantly, its $47 Growth plan includes warm-up for unlimited email accounts.

### Can several people share one Warmy plan?

Yes. Warmy's FAQ says you can invite as many users as you need, and they share the plan's mailboxes and quotas. Mailwarm caps users by plan: 1, 2 or 5. lemlist's Email plan has unlimited users.

### Do I need placement tests if I use warm-up?

Yes, if you want to know where your mail lands. Warm-up builds reputation over time. A placement test shows the result at each provider. Warmy includes test credits. MailReach includes 20 spam test credits. Unspam sells placement tests on their own.

### Will my reputation drop if I stop Warmy?

Warm-up activity stops when you cancel, so start the new tool before you do. Keep sending volume steady during the switch, and test placement before and after.
