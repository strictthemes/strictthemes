MailReach sells two things. Its All-In-One plan warms up a mailbox and includes spam test credits, which send your email to a seed list and show where it lands. Its Spam Tester sells those test credits on their own. Most people looking for an alternative want one of three things: cheaper warm-up, cheaper testing, or a plan that does both with more support.

This guide covers each case. For a full guide to warm-up tools, see [email warm-up tools in 2026](/guides/email-warm-up-tools/). If you are comparing on cost per mailbox, [Warmy alternatives](/guides/warmy-alternatives/) has a table for 1, 3 and 10 mailboxes. We read every price on each vendor's own page on 25 November 2026, leaving out seasonal sales and first-month discounts.

![MailReach's pricing page with the All-In-One plan selected, at €25 per mailbox a month.](/guides/mailreach-alternatives/mailreach.jpg "MailReach's pricing page, billed monthly, 25 November 2026")

## TL;DR

- **Cheapest warm-up per mailbox:** Warmup Inbox, from $15 per inbox a month. It has no test credits, so pair it with a placement test.
- **Cheapest placement tests:** Unspam. 3 inbox placements a month free, then $0.50 each, from a $5 monthly minimum. It does not do warm-up.
- **Warm-up with expert calls:** Mailwarm, from $69 a month billed yearly for 1 mailbox.
- **Warm-up and placement tests in one plan:** Warmy, from $49 a month, priced by warm-up volume.
- **More automated testing:** GlockApps, from $59 a month billed yearly.
- **Stay on MailReach** if you want warm-up and about 20 tests a month for one mailbox on one bill. €25 a month is hard to beat for that.

More credits raise the bill: the calculator adds €30 a month for 50 credits, €55 for 100 and €135 for 300.

MailReach's FAQ says the first warm-up phase should last at least 14 days, with no campaigns sent during it, and that you should keep warming as long as you send.

## Why people look for an alternative

- **They only need one half.** Warm-up and testing are separate jobs. If you already test elsewhere, or don't need warm-up, the bundle costs more than the part you use.
- **Cost at scale.** €25 per mailbox adds up for a team with many sending addresses.
- **They want more help.** MailReach's page offers agencies custom pricing, but its plans don't list calls with a deliverability expert.

## 1. Warmup Inbox: cheapest warm-up per mailbox

[Warmup Inbox](https://warmupinbox.com/pricing) warms up mailboxes by exchanging email with what its page calls more than 30,000 real inboxes.

![Warmup Inbox's pricing page, with Basic at $15, Pro at $49 and Max at $79 per inbox a month.](/guides/mailreach-alternatives/warmupinbox.jpg "Warmup Inbox's pricing page, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** Basic is $15 per inbox a month for 75 warm-up messages a day. Pro is $49 for 250 a day. Max is $79 for 1,000 a day. Billed yearly, they are $180, $588 and $948 per inbox. A volume discount starts at 6 inboxes. There is a 7-day trial with no card.

**Limitations:** no test credits on the pricing page. It lists spam and category monitoring and a reputation check, but not a seed placement test. Pro and Max cost more per inbox than MailReach.

**Good for:** teams that want the lowest price for warm-up and will test placement somewhere else.

## 2. Unspam: cheapest placement tests

[Unspam](https://unspam.email/pricing) replaces the testing half of MailReach. It sends your email to a seed list of 10 mailboxes at 7 providers, including Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo, and reports where each copy landed: inbox, a tab, spam or missing. Its spam test checks SPF, DKIM and DMARC, content, blacklists and HTML. It does not warm up mailboxes.

![Unspam's pricing page, with the No account and Free plans, the Free plan including 21 tests a month.](/guides/mailreach-alternatives/unspam.jpg "Unspam's pricing page, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** the free plan includes 21 tests a month: 5 spam tests, 3 inbox placements, 3 device previews, 5 screenshots and 5 heatmaps. The Custom plan is pay per test from a $5 monthly minimum: $0.50 per inbox placement and $0.18 per spam test. Rates drop as volume grows, and every paid plan has a 14-day refund. Scheduled tests, the API and the MCP server need the Custom plan.

**Limitations:** no warm-up. Tests are one at a time or scheduled, not continuous monitoring of every send.

**Good for:** anyone who warms up with another tool, or doesn't need warm-up, and wants to check placement before a campaign. 20 placements a month cost $10.

## 3. Mailwarm: warm-up with expert calls

[Mailwarm](https://www.mailwarm.com/pricing) warms up mailboxes and includes a monthly call with a deliverability expert. Every plan lists spam score monitoring and inbox placement insights.

![Mailwarm's pricing page, with Starter at $69, Growth at $159 and Scale at $479 a month, billed yearly.](/guides/mailreach-alternatives/mailwarm.jpg "Mailwarm's pricing page, billed yearly, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** Starter is $79 a month, or $69 billed yearly, for 1 mailbox and up to 50 warm-up emails a day. Growth is $189, or $159 billed yearly, for 3 mailboxes. Scale is $549, or $479 billed yearly, for 10 mailboxes.

**Limitations:** the most expensive option here for one mailbox. Starter allows 50 warm-up emails a day, half of MailReach's 100.

**Good for:** a small team that wants a person to look at its deliverability each month.

## 4. Warmy: warm-up and placement tests in one plan

[Warmy](https://www.warmy.io/pricing) is the closest match to MailReach's bundle. Every plan lists warm-up, shared placement test credits, email verification, DMARC monitoring and API access.

![Warmy's pricing page, showing plans starting from $49 a month and a feature list that includes placement test credits.](/guides/mailreach-alternatives/warmy.jpg "Warmy's pricing page, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** plans start from $49 a month and are priced by warm-up volume. There is a 7-day trial with no card.

**Limitations:** the page doesn't publish a price per mailbox or how many test credits you get. It tells you to confirm the allowance with Warmy's team.

**Good for:** teams that want one vendor for warm-up, testing and the tools around them, and are fine agreeing the details on a call.

## 5. GlockApps: more automated testing

[GlockApps](https://glockapps.com/pricing/) is a testing and monitoring platform. Plans add DMARC analytics and scheduled tests.

![GlockApps' pricing page, with Free, Essential at $59, Growth at $99 and Enterprise at $129 a month, billed yearly.](/guides/mailreach-alternatives/glockapps.jpg "GlockApps' pricing page, billed yearly, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** there is a free plan. Essential is $59 a month billed yearly ($708 a year), Growth is $99 ($1,188) and Enterprise is $129 ($1,548). [GlockApps pricing in 2026](/guides/glockapps-pricing/) explains what the credits buy.

**Limitations:** no warm-up. A yearly plan is a bigger commitment than MailReach's Spam Tester.

**Good for:** teams that test often and want DMARC reports in the same tool.

## Which MailReach alternative should you choose?

- **You only want warm-up, cheaply:** Warmup Inbox, from $15 per inbox.
- **You only want placement tests:** Unspam, free for 3 a month, then $0.50 each.
- **You want both, on one bill, for one or two mailboxes:** stay on MailReach.
- **You want both, plus a person to call:** Mailwarm.
- **You want both, plus verification and DMARC monitoring:** Warmy.
- **You test often and want monitoring and DMARC:** GlockApps.
- **You also send cold email:** a sending platform with warm-up included may cost less. [Warmy alternatives](/guides/warmy-alternatives/) compares Instantly and lemlist.

## FAQ

### How much does MailReach cost?

The All-In-One plan is €25 per mailbox a month with 20 spam test credits, before tax. Billed yearly it is 20% less. The Spam Tester alone starts at €15 a month for 20 credits, or €19.50 for 20 credits pay as you go.

### What is a MailReach spam test credit?

One credit is one spam test. MailReach sends your email to its seed list and shows where it landed by provider, with checks for blacklists and authentication.

### Is there a cheaper alternative to MailReach?

For warm-up alone, Warmup Inbox Basic is $15 per inbox a month. For tests alone, Unspam's free plan includes 3 placement tests a month, and its Custom plan charges $0.50 per placement from a $5 minimum.

### Can I use one tool for warm-up and another for testing?

Yes. They are separate jobs. Warm-up builds a sending history. A placement test shows where a real email lands. Many teams use a warm-up tool plus a separate placement test.

### How long does email warm-up take?

MailReach's FAQ recommends at least 14 days before you send campaigns, and keeping warm-up running while you send. Other vendors give similar advice.
