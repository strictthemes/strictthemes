## What to check before you buy a UI kit on Black Friday 2026

A discount makes a kit cheaper. It does not make it fit your project. Four things to check first:

1. **One payment or a subscription.** Most kits on this list are paid once, so the Black Friday price is the price for good. A subscription at 50% off for the first year costs full price from the second.
2. **What the licence covers.** Read whether you can use it in work for clients, in a product you sell, or in an open source project. A team licence is often cheaper than two personal ones.
3. **Whether it uses your theme.** Blocks that read shadcn's theme variables take on your colors and corners when you add them. Blocks with colors written into each class keep their own look, and you end up with two designs on one page.
4. **Whether your coding agent can use it.** A kit served as a shadcn registry installs with one command, which an agent can run. A zip file of pages needs copying by hand.

To check point 3 on a kit you already bought, add a few of its blocks to a project and run [Strict Check](/guides/strict-check-in-ci/) against your theme. It lists every color, size and corner on the page that your theme does not declare.
