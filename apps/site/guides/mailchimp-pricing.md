Mailchimp prices its plans by the number of contacts you have. Up to 500 contacts, Essentials is $13 a month, Standard is $20 and Premium is $350. The price climbs with every contact band: at 5,000 contacts, Essentials is $75 and Standard is $100. A free plan covers up to 250 contacts and 500 emails a month.

This guide is for anyone who sends newsletters or marketing email with Mailchimp. We read every number below on Mailchimp's pricing page on 19 November 2026, in US dollars. Mailchimp showed us its Spanish page in euros at first, based on where we were, so check the currency and language when you compare.

![Mailchimp's pricing page in US dollars, with Premium at $297.50 a month for 12 months, and Standard and Essentials free for 14 days.](/guides/mailchimp-pricing/mailchimp.jpg "Mailchimp's pricing page, 19 November 2026")

## TL;DR

- **Free covers 250 contacts and 500 emails a month,** at most 250 a day, with 1 seat and 1 audience. Sending pauses if you go over.
- **Essentials starts at $13 a month and Standard at $20,** for up to 500 contacts. Both have a 14-day free trial.
- **The price rises with contacts.** At 5,000 contacts, Essentials is $75 and Standard $100. At 50,000, they are $385 and $450.
- **Premium starts at $350 a month,** with a first-year offer of $297.50 a month for 12 months.
- **Each plan's monthly email limit is a multiple of your contacts:** 10 times on Essentials, 12 on Standard, 15 on Premium.
- **Paid plans bill overages automatically** if you go over your contacts or sends.

## The free plan, and what happens at the limits

Mailchimp's free plan includes 1 seat, 1 audience of up to 250 contacts, and up to 500 email sends a month, with at most 250 a day. Mailchimp says sending is paused if you go over the contact or send limit. The free plan can only use some of Mailchimp's pre-built templates; paid plans get all of them.

On paid plans, Mailchimp's FAQ says that after your trial, if you reach your contact or send limit, it bills the overage automatically. As long as you pay on time, it does not pause your campaigns.

Since the price follows your contact band, check how many contacts your audience holds before a band boundary, such as 5,000 or 10,000, and remove ones you no longer mail.

## Before you send: check where your email lands

Mailchimp's reports show opens, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes. A delivered email was accepted by the receiving server, which does not tell you whether Gmail put it in the inbox, the promotions tab or spam. For that you need an inbox placement test.

[Unspam](https://unspam.email/) runs that test. You send your real campaign from Mailchimp to a seed list of inboxes at Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo and others, and it reports where each copy landed. It also scores the message against spam signals, including your domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, and renders it in 60+ email clients and devices.

![Unspam's pricing page, headed Plans that scale with your sending, with the No account and Free plans.](/guides/mailchimp-pricing/unspam.jpg "Unspam's pricing page, 18 November 2026")

The free plan gives 21 tests a month, including 3 inbox placements and 5 spam tests. After that, the Custom plan is pay per test from a $5 monthly minimum: $0.50 an inbox placement and $0.18 a spam test. Its API and MCP server, so a test can run from CI or a coding agent, need the Custom plan.

What it does not do: it does not send your campaigns, warm up a domain or fix a blocklisting. It finds the problem; you fix it.

**Good for:** a check before a big campaign, and after any change to your domain's DNS or your template.

## Which Mailchimp plan should you choose?

- **A new list under 250 people:** Free. Watch the 250-a-day limit.
- **A regular newsletter with a few thousand subscribers:** Essentials, if simple automation is enough.
- **Welcome series, abandoned carts, coded templates or A/B testing at scale:** Standard.
- **A team of more than 5 people, or phone support:** Premium.

## FAQ

### How much does Mailchimp cost per month?

It depends on your contacts. Up to 500 contacts, Essentials is $13 a month, Standard $20 and Premium $350. At 5,000 contacts, Essentials is $75 and Standard $100. At 50,000, they are $385 and $450.

### Is Mailchimp free?

Yes, for small lists. The free plan includes 250 contacts, 1 audience, 1 seat and 500 email sends a month, at most 250 a day. Sending pauses if you go over.

### What happens if I go over my Mailchimp contact limit?

On paid plans, Mailchimp bills the overage automatically after your trial, and does not pause your campaigns if you pay on time. On the free plan, sending is paused.

### How many emails can I send on Mailchimp?

On paid plans, your monthly send limit is a multiple of your contact limit: 10 times on Essentials, 12 times on Standard and 15 times on Premium. The free plan allows 500 a month.

### Does Mailchimp have a free trial?

Standard and Essentials have a 14-day free trial. Mailchimp says a 1-month Premium trial is available to eligible new customers through its sales team.

### Is there a Mailchimp discount?

The pricing page offered 15% off for lists of 10,000 contacts or more, 15% off Standard for 12 months, and Premium at $297.50 a month for the first 12 months. Annual plans for large lists are available through sales.
