lemwarm is lemlist's email warm-up. It exchanges real emails with real inboxes to build your sender reputation, and lemlist's pricing FAQ says it is included in all plans. We found no standalone lemwarm plan on lemlist's pricing page, so lemwarm costs what a lemlist plan costs.

That matters if you are leaving lemlist, or if you only wanted warm-up and a way to check where your email lands. This guide splits the job in two: testing deliverability, and replacing the warm-up. We read every price on each vendor's own page on 25 November 2026, leaving out seasonal sales and first-month discounts.

## Testing deliverability

A placement test sends your real email to a seed list of mailboxes and reports where each copy landed. It is the check that tells you whether warm-up, or anything else, is working.

### 1. Unspam: best for placement tests without a platform

[Unspam](https://unspam.email/pricing) sends your email to a seed list of 10 mailboxes at 7 providers, including Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo, and reports where each copy landed: inbox, a tab, spam or missing. Its spam test checks SPF, DKIM and DMARC, content, blacklists and HTML. You can paste your email in, or add the seed address to a campaign in any sending tool. Unspam does not warm up mailboxes.

![Unspam's pricing page, with the No account and Free plans, the Free plan including 21 tests a month.](/guides/lemwarm-alternatives/unspam.jpg "Unspam's pricing page, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** the free plan includes 21 tests a month, among them 3 inbox placements and 5 spam tests. The Custom plan is pay per test from a $5 monthly minimum: $0.50 per inbox placement and $0.18 per spam test. Rates drop as volume grows. Every paid plan has a 14-day refund. Scheduled tests, the API and the MCP server need the Custom plan.

**Limitations:** no warm-up, and no sending. You need another tool for both.

**Good for:** teams that left lemlist, or never used it, and want to test placement before each campaign without a subscription.

### 2. MailReach Spam Tester: best for tests on a subscription, with alerts

[MailReach](https://www.mailreach.co/pricing) sells its spam tester on its own. Each credit is one test against its seed list.

![MailReach's pricing page with the Spam Tester tab open, showing 300 credits pay as you go at €175.50.](/guides/lemwarm-alternatives/mailreach.jpg "MailReach's Spam Tester pricing, pay as you go, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** prices showed in euros for us, before tax.

| 20 | €15 | €19.50 |
| 100 | €55 | €71.50 |

The Spam Tester includes automatic tests with alerts to Slack or webhooks, a placement view by provider, seed list rotation, a blacklist checker and test history.

**Limitations:** no free plan. Per test, it costs more than Unspam at low volume: 20 tests are €15 a month, against $10 for 20 Unspam placements.

**Good for:** teams that want tests to run on a schedule and post results to Slack.

### 3. GlockApps: best for testing with DMARC reports

[GlockApps](https://glockapps.com/pricing/) combines placement tests with DMARC analytics and monitoring.

![GlockApps' pricing page, with Free, Essential at $59, Growth at $99 and Enterprise at $129 a month, billed yearly.](/guides/lemwarm-alternatives/glockapps.jpg "GlockApps' pricing page, billed yearly, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** there is a free plan. Essential is $59 a month billed yearly ($708 a year), Growth is $99 ($1,188) and Enterprise is $129 ($1,548). [GlockApps pricing in 2026](/guides/glockapps-pricing/) explains the credits.

**Limitations:** no warm-up. The best prices need a yearly plan.

**Good for:** teams that want placement tests and DMARC reports in one tool.

## Replacing the warm-up

### 4. MailReach All-In-One: warm-up and tests from one vendor

The same MailReach account can warm up your mailboxes too. The All-In-One plan is €25 per mailbox a month, with 20 spam test credits for one mailbox, up to 100 warm-up emails a day, and checks for blacklists, SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Billed yearly, it is 20% less. MailReach works with any provider that supports SMTP.

**Good for:** one or two mailboxes, where one bill for warm-up and tests is simplest. [MailReach alternatives](/guides/mailreach-alternatives/) compares it with the rest.

### 5. Warmup Inbox: cheapest warm-up on its own

[Warmup Inbox](https://warmupinbox.com/pricing) is a warm-up tool and nothing else. Basic is $15 per inbox a month for 75 warm-up messages a day, Pro is $49 for 250 and Max is $79 for 1,000. A volume discount starts at 6 inboxes, and there is a 7-day trial with no card.

**Good for:** teams with several mailboxes that want low-cost warm-up and will test placement with Unspam or GlockApps.

## Which lemwarm alternative should you choose?

- **You are staying on lemlist:** keep lemwarm. It is already in your plan. Add an independent placement test, such as Unspam's free plan, to check its effect.
- **You are leaving lemlist and need both warm-up and tests:** MailReach All-In-One for one or two mailboxes. Warmup Inbox plus Unspam for more, or to keep costs down.
- **You only need to test where email lands:** Unspam for pay per test, MailReach Spam Tester for scheduled tests with Slack alerts, GlockApps if you also want DMARC reports.
- **You are moving to another outreach platform:** check whether it includes warm-up. [Warmy alternatives](/guides/warmy-alternatives/) covers Instantly, which includes unlimited warm-up.

## FAQ

### Can I buy lemwarm without lemlist?

We found no standalone lemwarm plan on lemlist's pricing page. Its FAQ says lemwarm is included in all lemlist plans, which start at 55€ a month billed yearly.

### How much does lemwarm cost?

It costs what your lemlist plan costs: 69€ a month for Email, or 55€ a month billed yearly. Multichannel is 109€ a month, or 87€ billed yearly.

### What is the best way to test deliverability?

Run a seed placement test. It sends your real email to test mailboxes at the main providers and shows whether each copy reached the inbox, a tab or spam. Unspam, MailReach and GlockApps all do this. Unspam's free plan includes 3 placement tests a month.

### Does Unspam warm up mailboxes?

No. Unspam tests deliverability: placement, spam signals and authentication. For warm-up, use a tool such as MailReach or Warmup Inbox, or keep lemwarm if you stay on lemlist.

### Do I still need warm-up after I leave lemlist?

You do if your mailboxes are new or you plan to send cold email at volume. If your mailboxes have a long sending history and placement tests look good, you may not.
