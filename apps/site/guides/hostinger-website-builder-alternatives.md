Hostinger Website Builder is a drag-and-drop editor that comes with Hostinger's web hosting plans. It is aimed at getting a business online fast: templates, an AI site generator, email and a free domain for the first year. Designers often want something else. They want to place elements freely, set each screen size by hand, start from a Figma file, or keep a CMS under control.

This guide covers five alternatives for designers and what each costs. We read every price on each vendor's own page on 25 November 2026. Framer and Squarespace showed prices in euros for our location, so those are in euros.

## TL;DR

- **Best for designers who work in Figma:** Siter.io. A free-form canvas, a layout for each screen size, a Figma plugin, from $7 a month billed yearly.
- **Best for complex sites with a CMS:** Webflow. Basic is $15 a month billed yearly, and Premium with the CMS is $25.
- **Best for fast, animated marketing sites:** Framer. A free plan, then €10 or €30 a month billed yearly.
- **Best all-in-one with a store:** Squarespace. From €12 a month billed yearly, with a 14-day free trial.
- **Cheapest for one-page sites:** Carrd. Pro plans from $9 a year.
- **Stay on Hostinger** if you want hosting, email and a builder on one bill, and you are happy with its templates.

## Why designers look for an alternative

- **Design control.** Hostinger's editor is built around templates and an AI starting design. Designers who want to place every element and set mobile layouts by hand look for a canvas-style editor.
- **A workflow from Figma.** Hostinger has no Figma import on its builder page.
- **Price after the first term.** The renewal rate is several times the first-term price.

## 1. Siter.io: best for designers who work in Figma

[Siter.io](https://siter.io/) is a visual website editor with a free-form canvas: you place anything anywhere. Each screen size gets its own layout, so the mobile version is designed rather than squeezed. It has a Figma plugin, and an AI assistant that builds a page on the canvas, where it stays editable. Sites publish to a getsiter.com address or your own domain, with SSL.

![Siter.io's pricing page with yearly billing: Solo at $7, Plus at $12 and Pro at $24 a month, billed yearly.](/guides/hostinger-website-builder-alternatives/siter.jpg "Siter.io's pricing page, billed yearly, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** Solo is $7 a month billed yearly, or $8 month to month, for 1 website with 10 pages and 2 editor seats. Plus is $12 a month billed yearly ($14 monthly) for 3 websites and 50 pages each. Pro is $24 a month billed yearly ($28 monthly) for 5 websites with unlimited pages, and it adds Download HTML. Extra websites and seats cost $4, $6 or $8 a month on Solo, Plus and Pro. The price is the same every year: there is no first-term discount followed by a higher renewal.

**Limitations:** no free plan. New accounts get a 3-day trial, and you add a card to start it. Pages and forms are capped on Solo and Plus. Monthly traffic is capped at 25, 50 or 100 GB, shared across your sites. Its pricing page lists no online store or blog CMS. Exporting your site as HTML needs Pro.

**Good for:** designers who think in frames and screen sizes, and who want to take a design from Figma to a live site without a developer.

## 2. Webflow: best for complex sites with a CMS

[Webflow](https://webflow.com/) is a visual builder that maps closely to HTML and CSS. It has a CMS for structured content, interactions and code components.

![Webflow's pricing page with yearly billing: Starter free, Basic at $15 and Premium at $25 a month, billed yearly.](/guides/hostinger-website-builder-alternatives/webflow.jpg "Webflow's site plans, billed yearly, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** Starter is free, with a webflow.io domain, 2 static pages and 1 GB of bandwidth. Basic is $15 a month billed yearly, with a custom domain, 300 static pages and 10 GB of bandwidth, but no CMS. Premium is $25 a month billed yearly and adds the Webflow CMS, with bandwidth from 50 GB. Prices are per site, and yearly billing saves up to 40% on Webflow's page.

**Limitations:** the free plan's CMS is limited, and the full CMS starts on Premium. Prices are per site, so several client sites add up. The editor takes longer to learn than template builders, because it exposes CSS concepts like boxes, flex and classes.

**Good for:** designers and agencies building content-heavy sites who are happy to learn a CSS-shaped tool.

## 3. Framer: best for fast, animated marketing sites

[Framer](https://www.framer.com/) is a design tool that publishes sites. It is strong on layout, animation and effects, with a CMS on paid plans.

![Framer's pricing page with yearly billing: Free at €0, Basic at €10 and Pro at €30 a month.](/guides/hostinger-website-builder-alternatives/framer.jpg "Framer's pricing page, billed yearly, prices shown in euros, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** the free plan publishes to a Framer domain, with 5 GB of bandwidth. Basic is €10 a month billed yearly, for 30 pages, 2 CMS collections and 50 GB of bandwidth. Pro is €30 a month billed yearly, for 150 pages, 10 CMS collections, 100 GB, redirects and a staging environment. Extra editors cost €20 a month, and viewers are free. A custom domain is included on yearly plans.

**Limitations:** page and CMS limits are tight on Basic. Extra editors add up for a team. Moving off Framer is not simple, which is why we wrote [how to leave Framer](/guides/leave-framer/).

**Good for:** designers making landing pages and marketing sites with motion, who want to publish from the design tool.

## 4. Squarespace: best all-in-one with a store

[Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/) is a template-based builder with hosting, commerce, scheduling and email marketing in one product.

![Squarespace's pricing page, with a 14-day free trial and the Basic plan at €12 a month billed annually.](/guides/hostinger-website-builder-alternatives/squarespace.jpg "Squarespace's pricing page, billed annually, prices shown in euros, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** Basic is €12 a month billed annually, with a 2% transaction fee on store sales. Core is €18, with no transaction fees and a free year of Google Workspace email. Plus is €32, and Advanced is €69. Every plan includes unlimited bandwidth, and there is a 14-day free trial.

**Limitations:** designs start from templates and sections, with less freedom than a canvas editor. The 2% store fee on Basic matters if you sell.

**Good for:** designers building sites for clients who sell products or bookings and want everything in one place.

## 5. Carrd: cheapest for one-page sites

[Carrd](https://carrd.co/) builds one-page sites: portfolios, link pages and simple landing pages.

![Carrd's Pro plans: Pro Lite at $9 a year for 3 sites, Pro Standard at $19 a year for 10 sites and Pro Plus at $49 a year for 25 sites.](/guides/hostinger-website-builder-alternatives/carrd.jpg "Carrd's Pro plans, priced per year, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** free for 3 sites on a Carrd address. Pro Lite is $9 a year for 3 sites without branding. Pro Standard is $19 a year for 10 sites with custom domains and forms. Pro Plus is $49 a year for 25 sites. [Carrd pricing in 2026](/guides/carrd-pricing/) has every tier.

**Limitations:** one page per site. Custom domains start on Pro Standard. No CMS and no store beyond embeds.

**Good for:** a portfolio or landing page that should cost almost nothing.

## Which should you choose?

- **You design in Figma and want the site to match it:** Siter.io. A canvas editor with a Figma plugin, at a flat price.
- **You build content-heavy sites for clients:** Webflow, on Premium for the CMS.
- **You make marketing sites with motion:** Framer.
- **Your client sells products or bookings:** Squarespace, on Core or above to avoid the store fee.
- **You need one page and want to pay almost nothing:** Carrd.
- **You want hosting, email and a builder on one bill:** stay on Hostinger. Plan around the renewal rate, not the first-term price.
- **Your site is already static, or could be:** [turning a site into static files](/guides/wordpress-to-static-site/) and hosting them is often the cheapest option of all.

## FAQ

### Is Hostinger Website Builder free?

No. It comes with Hostinger's web hosting plans. Premium renews at $10.99 a month on 12 to 48-month terms, and the 1-month plan is $12.99 a month. The first term costs less and is paid up front.

### What is the best Hostinger Website Builder alternative for designers?

For designers who work in Figma and want free placement on a canvas, Siter.io. For complex sites with a CMS, Webflow. For animated marketing sites, Framer.

### Which alternative has a free plan?

Webflow, Framer and Carrd have free plans with limits. Squarespace has a 14-day trial, and Siter.io a 3-day trial.

### Can I export my site from these builders?

Siter.io lets you download HTML on its Pro plan. None of the other four lists an HTML export on its pricing page. If owning your files matters, see [open-source Webflow and Framer alternatives](/guides/open-source-webflow-framer-alternatives/).

### Is Webflow cheaper than Hostinger?

Not for one simple site. Webflow Basic is $15 a month billed yearly, against Hostinger Premium's $10.99 renewal rate for up to 3 websites. Webflow's price buys a more flexible editor, not more sites.
