Better Uptime is now part of [Better Stack](https://betterstack.com/). The old betteruptime.com address redirects to Better Stack's uptime page, and uptime monitoring is priced on Better Stack's pricing page, next to on-call, status pages, logs and the rest of its platform.

This guide covers what the uptime side costs: the free plan, the responder license, extra monitors, heartbeats, status pages and their add-ons. Then it works through a small team's bill and compares it with cheaper uptime monitors. We read every price on each vendor's own page on 25 November 2026.

![Better Stack's pricing page, with Free for personal projects at $0 a month: 10 monitors and heartbeats and 1 status page, and paid plans starting at $29.](/guides/better-stack-uptime-pricing/better-stack.jpg "Better Stack's pricing page, billed yearly, 25 November 2026")

## TL;DR

- **Free:** 10 monitors and heartbeats, 1 status page, and Slack and email alerts, for personal projects.
- **Paid starts with a responder license:** $34 a month, or $29 billed yearly. It covers on-call, monitoring and status pages, with unlimited phone call and SMS alerts.
- **Extra capacity is sold in packs:** 50 more monitors for $25 a month ($21 yearly), 10 more heartbeats for $20 ($17 yearly), and each extra status page for $15 ($12 yearly).
- **Status page extras add up fast.** Removing the "Powered by Better Stack" footer is $250 a page a month, and password protection is $50.
- **A solo developer with 20 monitors** pays $59 a month, or $50 billed yearly.
- **Cheaper if you only need uptime checks:** Pulsetic from $9 a month, HetrixTools from $9.95, UptimeRobot from €9 billed yearly. Better Stack is worth its price for on-call with unlimited phone alerts, browser-based transaction checks, or logs in the same tool.

## What Better Stack's uptime monitoring costs

Better Stack shows monthly prices and yearly prices, where yearly billing is "~2 months free". It also offers a 60-day money-back guarantee.

| Free plan | $0 | $0 |

| Heartbeats (cron jobs) | 10 included | 10 included |
| Additional 10 heartbeats | $20 | $17 |

![Better Stack's pricing page, Uptime monitoring section: 10 monitors included and additional 50 monitors at $21 a month billed yearly, with checks up to every 30 seconds.](/guides/better-stack-uptime-pricing/better-stack-uptime.jpg "Better Stack's pricing page, uptime monitoring, billed yearly, 25 November 2026")

**The responder license** is what makes an account paid. The page describes it as access to incidents, including on-call, monitoring and status pages, with unlimited phone call and SMS alerts. Other team members are listed at $0 per member a month.

**Uptime monitors** check as often as every 30 seconds. The page lists HTTP keyword checks, TCP and UDP ports, ping, SSL and domain expiry, DNS servers, email servers (POP3, IMAP, SMTP), checks from multiple locations, screenshots of errors and maintenance windows.

**Heartbeats** watch cron jobs and scheduled tasks. You get 10, then pay per 10.

**Transaction monitoring** runs real browser sessions with Playwright, billed at $1 per 100 browser minutes.

## Other add-ons

- **Slack and Microsoft Teams incident workflows:** a basic single-channel integration is included. Channel-based and thread-based workflows are $9 per responder license a month.
- **Reporting and analytics:** $5 per member a month, or $4 billed yearly.
- **Single sign-on for your team:** Google is included. Azure and Okta are $5 per user a month.
- **Audit logs:** $250 a month, or $208 billed yearly.
- **Call routing:** $250 per phone number a month, or $208 billed yearly.

## Cheaper options for uptime checks

If you don't need on-call rotations or transaction checks, these cost less for the same monitors.

### Pulsetic: closest match for less

[Pulsetic](https://pulsetic.com/pricing/) covers uptime, SSL, ports, keywords, cron heartbeats, domains, status pages and real user monitoring in one plan.

![Pulsetic's pricing page, Addons section: extra monitors at $0.20 each a month, dependency monitors at $0.20 and RUM page views at $5 per 100,000.](/guides/better-stack-uptime-pricing/pulsetic.jpg "Pulsetic's pricing page, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** free for 10 monitors at 5 minutes from 3 regions, with 3 status pages. Solo is $9 a month for 10 monitors every 60 seconds from 5 regions. Team is $19 a month for 50 monitors every 30 seconds from 15 regions, with 2 teammates and 100 SMS or call alerts. Organization is $49 a month for 300 monitors. Paid plans include unlimited status pages, with custom domains. Extra monitors are $0.20 each, teammates $8 each, and SMS or call alerts $0.10 each. Yearly billing saves 2 months.

**Against Better Stack:** the solo developer above pays $59 a month on Better Stack. On Pulsetic Solo with 10 extra monitors it is $11, and Team covers 50 monitors at 30 seconds for $19.

**Limitations:** SMS and call alerts are counted, not unlimited. No browser-based transaction checks and no log management. Solo includes no teammates.

**Good for:** teams that want monitoring, status pages and cron checks without paying per responder.

### HetrixTools: most monitors per dollar

[HetrixTools](https://hetrixtools.com/pricing/uptime-monitor/) checks every minute on every plan, including the free one, and includes server monitoring and status pages.

![HetrixTools' uptime monitor pricing page, under the heading Plans differ in how much you monitor, not in how well.](/guides/better-stack-uptime-pricing/hetrixtools.jpg "HetrixTools' uptime monitor pricing, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** free for 15 monitors from 4 of 12 locations. Professional is $9.95 a month for 30 monitors, Business $19.95 for 60, Enterprise $49.95 a month or $499.95 a year for 200. Yearly billing gives 2 months free.

**Limitations:** 1-minute checks at best, against 30 seconds on Better Stack. The free account must log in every 90 days.

**Good for:** many monitors on a small budget.

### UptimeRobot: largest free plan

[UptimeRobot](https://uptimerobot.com/pricing/) has 50 free monitors.

![UptimeRobot's pricing page, billed yearly, with Solo at €9, Team at €35 and Scale at €65 a month.](/guides/better-stack-uptime-pricing/uptimerobot.jpg "UptimeRobot's pricing page, billed yearly, 25 November 2026")

**Pricing:** free for 50 monitors at 5 minutes. Solo is €9 a month billed yearly (€10 monthly) for 10 monitors at 60 seconds, or €19 (€22 monthly) for 50. Team is €35 billed yearly (€41 monthly) for 100 monitors at 30 seconds. UptimeRobot showed us prices in euros only.

**Limitations:** the free plan checks every 5 minutes and its status page has no customization.

**Good for:** many sites watched for free.

## Which should you choose?

- **You run on-call rotations and want unlimited phone alerts:** Better Stack. That is what the responder license pays for.
- **You need browser-based transaction checks:** Better Stack, at $1 per 100 Playwright minutes.
- **You want uptime checks and status pages without per-responder pricing:** Pulsetic. Team is $19 a month for 50 monitors at 30 seconds.
- **You want the most monitors for the least money:** HetrixTools.
- **You want it free:** UptimeRobot for 50 monitors, HetrixTools for 1-minute checks, or Better Stack's free plan if you may grow into its other tools.
