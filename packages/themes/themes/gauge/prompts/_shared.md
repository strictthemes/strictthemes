### How Gauge pages are laid out

- **Page:** steel white, dense on purpose. The outermost element is a `<div>` with `flex min-h-svh flex-col bg-background text-body text-foreground`, holding `<header>`, `<main>` (`flex-1`) and `<footer>`.
- **Container:** the header's row, the content of `<main>` and the footer's line each sit in a centered container `<div>`, `mx-auto w-full max-w-[1280px] px-4`: a 16px gutter at every width. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed. The `<main>` element itself has `py-6`; the container inside it carries the rhythm below.
- **Today:** the sample's today is Thursday 12 November 2026, a fixed date in code, never the device clock.
- **Dates and times:** a date that stands on its own (a table cell, a value, a small figure) is `12 Nov 2026`, in a `<time>` with `dateTime="2026-11-12"`. Inside a sentence a date is written out, `13 November 2026`, and is not a `<time>`, in small print too. A time is on the 24-hour clock, `15:00`, and is a `<time>` only where it stands on its own. Dates and times take `tabular-nums` only where they are `font-mono`.
- **Sample organisation:** "Torvale Bearings Ltd", an invented supplier of industrial bearings with its works and stores in Brigholt. Prices are in pounds and exclude VAT, dimensions are in millimetres, loads in kilonewtons. The buyer filling in the forms is "Pellam Pumps Ltd". The same parts, figures and prices appear on every page; this brief gives the ones it needs.
- **Links:** the product's name links to `/catalogue`. The nav links, in this order, are Catalogue (`/catalogue`), Compare (`/compare`), Downloads (`/downloads`) and Quote (`/quote`), the same in both navs; this brief says which is current. Any other link without a named destination is `#`. A text link is `text-foreground underline underline-offset-4 hover:decoration-2` and inherits its size: links are the text color, so the hover thickens the underline and changes no color. Add `transition-colors duration-150`.
- **Vertical rhythm:** every vertical distance between siblings comes from a `gap-*` on their parent, a `flex flex-col` or a grid. Never use `space-y-*`. Rows and cells use padding. The only margins are the Confirmations note's. The container inside `<main>` is `flex flex-col gap-6`: the page header, then each row of panels. On a page this brief divides into sections (each a `<section>` named by its `<h2>` with `aria-labelledby`), the container is `gap-12`, the spec's 48px between sections, and each section is `flex flex-col gap-4`. The other steps: `gap-1` between a label and the value under it; `gap-2` between a field and its helper text and error, between the options of a group, and between buttons in a row; `gap-3` between a label and its field or button group; `gap-4` inside a panel (between its heading row and its content, between groups, between the fields of a form), between panels and between grid columns. Where a page's own section gives another value, the page wins.
- **Grid:** where this brief puts panels side by side, they are items of a 12-column grid, `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 lg:grid-cols-12`, a single column below `lg`. "Three columns" means `lg:col-span-3`, and so on. A column of several panels is `flex flex-col gap-4`, and its last panel has `flex-1`, so the columns of a row end level.
- **Header:** a full-width `<header>` with `border-b border-border bg-card`. Its container is the row, `flex items-center gap-6 py-3`: the product's name, "Torvale", in `font-heading text-h2 text-foreground`, no underline; then a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Main" and `hidden md:block`, holding a `<ul>` with `flex gap-6` of the nav links; then, at the right end (`ml-auto`), the sales line as a small figure, `Sales 01632 960 418`, with `hidden md:block`; and the "Menu" button of the note on menus below. A nav link is a label: the current page's link is `text-foreground underline underline-offset-4` with `aria-current="page"`, the others `text-muted-foreground hover:text-foreground`.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` with `border-t border-border` across the full width. Its container has `py-6` and holds one small figure: "Torvale Bearings Ltd · Brigholt works · Prices exclude VAT · A demo: nothing is sold or sent."
- **Page header:** a row that is not a panel: `flex flex-wrap items-end justify-between gap-4`. On the left a stack with `gap-1`: the breadcrumb or label this brief gives, then the `<h1>` in `text-h1`. On the right the page's actions, `flex flex-wrap gap-2`.
- **Breadcrumb:** where this brief asks for one, it is a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Breadcrumb" holding an `<ol>` with `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-2`, in `font-mono text-label`: each step but the last a link in `text-muted-foreground underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-foreground`, a `/` in `text-muted-foreground` with `aria-hidden="true"` between steps, and the last step in `text-foreground` with `aria-current="page"`, not a link. Because the breadcrumb then says `page`, the header's nav link for the section it belongs to has `aria-current="true"` in place of `page`, with the same look.
- **Headings:** Chivo, sentence case, at the weight its type class sets: do not add `font-bold`, `font-semibold` or `font-medium` to a heading. A panel's or a section's `<h2>` is `text-h2`. A smaller heading inside a panel is an `<h3>` in `font-sans text-body font-bold`: body text in bold, not the heading font.
- **Panels:** a panel is a plain element with `rounded-xl border border-border bg-card p-4`, white on steel white, not the shadcn `Card`, which ships its own padding and a ring. It is `flex flex-col gap-4` when it holds a heading row and content. Never put a panel inside a panel, and never a bordered box with rounded corners inside a panel: divide a panel's parts with 1px rules (`border-t border-border`). No tinted panel: every panel is `bg-card`.
- **Panel heading:** a panel opens with a heading row, unless this brief says it has none: `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-3`. It holds the panel's `<h2>` in `text-h2` and, at its right end, anything that belongs to the whole panel: a count, a link, a filter, a badge.
- **Panels with rows:** a panel that holds a table or a list of rows has no padding of its own, no gap, and `overflow-hidden`. Its heading row is one element: the heading row's classes plus `border-b border-border px-4 py-3`, with no wrapper around it. Each row of a list has `px-4 py-3` and a 1px top border, except the first. Anything under the table inside the panel (a note, a total) is a row with `border-t border-border px-4 py-3`.
- **Labels:** table headers, field labels, the names of groups, nav links, breadcrumbs and button and badge text are labels: `font-mono text-label`, in sentence case, never `uppercase`, a short fragment and never a sentence. They are `text-muted-foreground` unless a note or this brief gives another color; button and badge text keep their component's color.
- **Small figures:** a part number under a name, a date, a count, a file size, a reference, helper text and small print are small figures: `font-mono text-label tabular-nums text-muted-foreground`, the look of a label used for a value or a sentence. A `text-foreground` in this brief replaces the muted color.
- **Numbers and units:** every number that stands on its own (a table cell, a value, a price) is `font-mono tabular-nums`; inside a sentence it stays in the body font. A quantity, a dimension, a load, a price and a file size are right-aligned in a table, and so is their column's header. A unit is given once, where the eye finds it: in a table it is in the column header, "Bore (mm)", and the cells hold the bare figure, `20`; beside a single value it follows the figure with a space, `20 mm`, `13.40 kN`. Prices have two decimals and a thousands comma (`1,137.80`); outside a priced column they carry the pound sign, `£2.94`. A minus sign is U+2212 (`−30 °C`). Never round a figure the buyer relies on: write the decimals this brief gives.
- **Part numbers:** a part number is `font-mono` and written in full everywhere (`TVL-2047-RS`), never shortened or hidden behind a click. In a table's first column it is a text link in `font-mono text-body`; under a name it is a small figure.
- **Text color:** headings, body text and values are `text-foreground`. Labels, small figures and helper text are `text-muted-foreground`, and only on the page or a panel, never on a fill.
- **Measure:** a paragraph of body text is `text-foreground`, never muted, and at most `max-w-lg` wide.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="px-4 font-mono text-label"`, its stock height and corner, in sentence case; add it with `npx shadcn@latest add button` if missing. There are two looks and no others. The default variant (graphite) is for the one main action of a view, "Add to quote" or "Request a quote": at most one enabled default button on screen. Every other button is `variant="secondary"` (pale steel with graphite text): no `outline`, `ghost` or `destructive` buttons on these pages. In `components/ui/button.tsx`, replace the `secondary` variant's stock hover class, `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]`, with `hover:bg-secondary/80`, once, on every page (each page has a secondary button, the "Menu" button at least), and add no hover class beside it or in a `className`. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. A button that is disabled before anyone clicks it, or whose click moves focus elsewhere, uses the plain `disabled` prop. This brief says whether any button gets `focusableWhenDisabled`; on a page where none does, `button.tsx` needs no `data-disabled:` classes. The stock `Button` keeps its `transition-all` and its nudge when pressed. A link styled as a button is an `<a>` with `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "px-4 font-mono text-label")`: `buttonVariants` alone does not merge classes.
- **Button groups:** a set of buttons where one is chosen (a filter) is a `role="group"` with `flex flex-wrap gap-1` and an `aria-label`. Every button in it is `variant="secondary"` with `aria-pressed` and the `className` `aria-pressed:border-foreground aria-pressed:font-bold` beside the Buttons note's classes: the chosen one, `aria-pressed="true"`, is marked by a 1px graphite border and bold text, never by a fill of its own. The stock button's `border-transparent` is what this border replaces.
- **Badges:** a badge marks a part: "New", "Made to order". It is the shadcn `Badge` (`npx shadcn@latest add badge`) in the `outline` variant with `className="h-auto rounded-sm border-transparent bg-tertiary px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label text-tertiary-foreground"`: mid steel with graphite text. The corner must be in this `className`, or the stock pill wins. One or two words, no icon. Steel is a fill only: never write `text-tertiary`.
- **Stock states:** a stock state is a status dot and a word, `inline-flex items-center gap-2 whitespace-nowrap`. The dot is `size-2 shrink-0 rounded-full` with `aria-hidden="true"`: `bg-success` for "In stock", `bg-muted-foreground` for "Low stock" and "Made to order", `bg-destructive` for "Discontinued". The word is `text-body text-foreground`. The dot is the only circle on these pages, and the green and the red appear nowhere but beside their word: never show a state by color alone, never color a row, a cell or a panel.
- **Inputs:** the shadcn `Input` (`npx shadcn@latest add input`) with `className="text-body md:text-body"`, its stock height and corner. In `components/ui/input.tsx`, replace the class `bg-transparent` and the class `dark:bg-input/30` with `bg-card`, so a field is white on the white panel and matches the selects; `file:bg-transparent` and the `disabled:` fills stay. A quantity field is also `font-mono tabular-nums text-right` with `inputMode="numeric"`. A field is a `flex flex-col gap-3`: a visible `<label>` with `block`, styled as a label and tied to the field with `htmlFor`; then a stack with `gap-2` holding the field, any helper text (a small figure) and the error line when there is one. Name the helper text in the field's `aria-describedby`. An optional field says so in its label, "Phone (optional)"; a required one has no mark.
- **Selects:** a native `<select>` with `h-8 w-full rounded-lg border border-input bg-card px-2 text-body text-foreground aria-invalid:border-destructive` and the focus outline from the rules (`focus-visible:outline-solid focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-ring`). Set a width on an element around it, not on the `<select>`. Same label and stack as any field.
- **Checkboxes:** the shadcn `Checkbox` (`npx shadcn@latest add checkbox`) with `className="rounded-sm"`, the theme's 2px corner. Each option is a `<label>` with `flex items-center gap-2 text-body text-foreground` wrapping the `Checkbox` and a `<span>` with its text, so the whole line toggles it; the `Checkbox` also gets `aria-labelledby` naming that `<span>`'s `id`. A set of options is a `<div>` with `role="group"`, `aria-labelledby` naming its label (a `<p>` styled as a label), and `flex flex-col gap-2`, the label its first item.
- **Errors:** an error line is `flex items-start gap-2 font-mono text-label text-destructive`, not a label (a sentence), holding the `CircleAlert` icon (`shrink-0`) and a sentence that says what to do. The field gets `aria-invalid` and names the line in its `aria-describedby`. Fields are checked in code on submit, with `noValidate` on the `<form>`; focus then moves to the first field with an error. Render the error lines first and move focus after: set the errors in state and focus the field in an effect that runs when they change, so the field's `aria-describedby` already points at a line that exists. An error line is removed when its own field changes (on `input` or `change`); every other error stays until the next submit.
- **Confirmations:** a message after an action goes into a `role="status"` element that is on the page from the start, empty, or it may not be announced. It is never hidden, has no classes that draw or take space, and is never an item of a `gap-*` stack or row: put it in a plain `<div>` with the element above it. The message inside it, rendered only when there is one, carries the look and the space, such as `mt-3`. Where a message replaces a form, the form and the status element share that `<div>`. Where the message replaces the control that had focus, give its heading or first element `tabIndex={-1}` and move focus to it.
- **Description lists:** a list of properties is a `<dl>`. Each row is `flex items-baseline justify-between gap-3 py-2` with a 1px top border except the first: the `<dt>` a label in `text-muted-foreground`, the `<dd>` in `text-right`, `font-mono tabular-nums` when it is a figure and `text-body` when it is a phrase.
- **Tables:** the shadcn `Table` (`npx shadcn@latest add table`) with `text-body`. The header row is mono: header cells are labels in `text-muted-foreground`, keep their stock `h-10`, `font-medium` and `whitespace-nowrap` (so a header is one short line) and get `px-4`. Body and footer cells get `px-4 py-2`. Rows are divided by the stock 1px borders and keep the stock `TableRow` hover fill, also where a row does nothing; no zebra stripes and no colored rows. Cells that hold a sentence get `whitespace-normal`. No `<caption>`, which would scroll away: the table's visible name is the heading above its box, and the `label` prop below repeats its words. A wide table scrolls sideways in its own box, which is fine on a phone: edit `components/ui/table.tsx` to give its scroll box `tabIndex={0}`, `role="region"`, an `aria-label` from a required `label` prop, and the focus outline drawn inside (`focus-visible:-outline-offset-2`, since a table here fills a panel that clips). A total row sits in a `<tfoot>`: the stock `TableFooter` ships a fill and `font-medium`, so give it `bg-transparent`, and each of its `TableRow`s `hover:bg-transparent`.
- **Drawings:** a part is drawn in outline with plain elements, never an image file, an SVG or an icon: 1px lines in the text color (`border-foreground`) on the panel's white, with no fill of their own, and its dimensions in labels in `text-foreground`. Every bordered box of a drawing has square corners: no `rounded-*` class, since a bordered box with rounded corners inside a panel reads as a card inside a card. No circles, no gradients, no hatching and no shadows. Sizes that come from the part's millimetres are inline `style` widths and heights at the scale this brief gives. The drawing has `aria-hidden="true"`; the figure's caption gives every dimension in words.
- **Icons:** from `lucide-react`, with `size-4` and `strokeWidth={1.5}`, only where this brief or these notes name one, with `aria-hidden="true"` beside a word. Never emoji.
- **Corners:** `rounded-sm` (2px) for badges and checkboxes, `rounded-lg` (4px, what they ship with) for buttons, inputs and selects, `rounded-xl` (8px) for panels, `rounded-full` only for the status dot, which is `size-2`, under the theme's 12px limit. No avatars and no pills.
- **Surfaces:** flat. No shadows, blur or gradients in the code you write. A stock component that floats over the page keeps the one short shadow it ships with.
- **Menus on small screens:** below `md` the nav links go behind a "Menu" button (`variant="secondary"`, `md:hidden`) with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, at the right end of the header's row (`ml-auto`). It opens a second `<nav>` under the row, inside the `<header>`, also named "Main" because only one of the two is ever shown: it has `md:hidden`, the `hidden` attribute while closed, the `id` that `aria-controls` names and a full-width 1px top border; in its container, a `<ul>` with `py-2`, each link a block with `py-2`. Escape closes it only while focus is inside the menu or on its button, and focus then goes to the button. Do not use a `Sheet` or a `Dialog`. Whether the menu is open is page state, and a page that has no other state still has this one.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, only colors transition. Nothing moves on scroll. Stock components keep the motion they ship with.
