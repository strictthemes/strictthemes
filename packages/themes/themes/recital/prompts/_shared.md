### How Recital pages are laid out

- **Container:** one `<div>` wraps the whole page and is the container: `mx-auto max-w-3xl` (768px) with `px-4`, and `px-6` from `md` up, plus `text-body`, which every paragraph, list and card inherits: they need no size class of their own. Inside it sit the `<header>`, a `<main>` holding everything between the two, and the `<footer>`. The header, the footer and whatever this brief calls wide (a figure, a table, a row of numbers) use its full width.
- **Reading column:** all other content sits in a reading column inside the container: `mx-auto max-w-lg` (512px, 60 to 70 characters a line in this font). Headings, paragraphs, lists, block quotes, cards and forms are in it. One column only: no sidebar, and no text set in two columns.
- **Wide blocks between text:** the page is a stack of blocks inside the container, each one either a reading column or a wide block. Where a wide block comes in the middle of the text, end the reading column before it and start a new one after it. The wide block carries the `my-12`; the column after it has no margin of its own, and its first element keeps its usual top margin, which collapses into the block's `mb-12`. Where this brief starts a new part after the block, that part's `mt-24` collapses with the `mb-12` in the same way. Do not pull an element out of the column with a negative margin.
- **Alignment:** text is left-aligned. Nothing is centered or justified, headings and short lines included. Only the two columns themselves are centered on the page.
- **Vertical rhythm:** written as a top margin on each element, never as `space-y-*` on the column: `space-y-*` also puts its margin under every heading. A paragraph or a list that follows text has `mt-6`. An `<h2>` has `mt-12`, and the text under a heading has `mt-4`. A block quote, a figure, a card, a table or a block of code has `my-12`. A label and the thing it labels are `mt-2` apart, where this brief gives no other margin. A new part of the page starts with `mt-24`, where this brief says so. Where a part begins "In the reading column, with `mt-…`" (or "Wide, with `mt-…`"), that margin goes on the part's own reading column `<div>` (or wide block), and the part's first element takes no top margin of its own: an `<h2>` that opens it has no `mt-12`, and a card that opens it has `mb-12` in place of `my-12`. Where this brief gives a block its own top margin, it replaces the top half of that block's `my-12`. A link or a button that stands on a line of its own sits in a block element (a `<p>` for a link, a `<div>` for a button), and the margin this brief gives it goes on that element. These margins rely on collapsing: keep the columns and the parts in them in normal block flow, not flex or grid. A row inside a part that this brief lays out with `flex` or `grid` (the header, a list item set as one row) is the exception, and the margins of its children do not collapse.
- **Headings:** the `<h1>` is `text-h1`, and `text-display` only where this brief says so. An `<h2>` is `text-h2`. An `<h3>` is `text-body font-semibold`, in the heading font it gets on its own, unless this brief gives it another size. Sentence case, no full stop. A label heading, where this brief names one, is an `<h2>` set as a label: `font-mono text-label text-muted-foreground`, with no top margin when it opens its part or its card.
- **Standfirst:** the sentence or two under a title that sum the piece up: a `<p>` in `font-heading text-h2 font-normal italic`.
- **Labels:** dates, bylines, reading times, captions, table headers, form labels, and button and badge text are labels: `font-mono text-label`. Write them in sentence case. Nothing is uppercased, in the text or with a class. A label on its own line is a block element such as a `<p>`. Parts of a label line are joined with ` · `, and each part is in sentence case on its own, as in `Free · Every other Sunday`.
- **Dates:** written day, month in full, year: `12 March 2026`, and `12 March` where the year stands in a heading above the entry. Every date in a label, a heading or a list item of its own is a `<time>` with a `dateTime` (`2026-03-12`, or `2019` for a year alone), unless this brief says it is plain text. A date or a year inside a sentence of running text, and a copyright year, are plain text.
- **Text color:** headings, body text and form labels are `text-foreground`. Other labels are `text-muted-foreground`. A button's text color comes from its variant. Paragraphs are never muted.
- **Emphasis:** `<em>` for emphasis, and nothing else: no bold in running text, no color, no underline. No small caps either. The theme loads real italics for the heading and the body font.
- **Links:** a link in running text is `text-primary underline decoration-1 underline-offset-4 hover:text-foreground` and inherits its size. A link in the header, the footer or a list of titles is `text-foreground decoration-1 underline-offset-4 hover:underline`. The link for the current page has `aria-current="page"` and `underline`; mark one only where this brief names the current page. A link you give one of these two class lists also gets `transition-colors duration-150`.
- **Header:** the `<header>` is itself the row, with `py-6` and no border: `flex flex-col items-start gap-y-2 md:flex-row md:items-baseline md:justify-between`. On the left the publication's name, a link to `/` in `font-heading text-h2`, styled as a header link and never marked as the current page. On the right a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Main" holding the links in `text-body` with `gap-6` between them. Below `md` the links sit on a second line under the name. No "Menu" button.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` in the container with `mt-24`. Inside it one element with a 1px top border and `py-12` holds the publication's name as plain text in `font-heading text-h2`, one sentence about it with `mt-2`, and a label line with the copyright with `mt-6`.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="h-auto rounded-md px-6 py-2 font-mono text-label"`. The default variant (oxblood) is for the page's one main action. Any other button is `variant="secondary"` (ink). Its stock hover color cannot be told from its resting one in this theme: on a page with a `Button` or a `buttonVariants` link, in `components/ui/button.tsx`, replace the `secondary` variant's `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]` with `hover:bg-secondary/80`, once, and add no hover class on the page. The buttons of a group that are not the chosen one are `variant="outline"`. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. A call to action that goes to another page or jumps within this one is an `<a>` with the same classes: write `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "…")`, because `buttonVariants` alone does not merge classes. Button text is sentence case, at most three words.
- **Inputs:** the shadcn `Input` and `Textarea` with `className="h-auto rounded-md border-muted-foreground px-4 py-2 text-body md:text-body"`. The stock `border-input` is about 1.4:1 against the page, too faint to find the field; `border-muted-foreground` reaches 3:1 in both modes. The stock `focus-visible:border-ring` and `aria-invalid:border-destructive` stay. They keep the fill they ship with, in dark mode too. Every field has a visible `<label>` above it with `block`, tied to it with `htmlFor`, and the field has `mt-2`.
- **Errors:** an error is a line under its field with `mt-2`: `flex items-start gap-2 font-mono text-label text-destructive`, holding the `CircleAlert` icon (`shrink-0`) and a sentence that says what to do. The field gets `aria-invalid` and points to the line with `aria-describedby`. Fields are checked in code when the form is sent, with `noValidate` on the `<form>`, and focus moves to the first field with an error. An error line is removed when its own field changes (in React's `onChange`, which fires on every `input` event); the errors of the other fields stay until the next submit.
- **Confirmations:** a message that replaces a form goes into an element with `role="status"` that is on the page from the start, empty: a status element that arrives together with its text is often not announced. The form and the status element share one wrapper, and the wrapper carries the top margin, so the message stands where the form stood. Never hide the empty status element. Give the message's first element `tabIndex={-1}` and move focus to it, because the button that had focus is gone.
- **Badges:** the shadcn `Badge` with `className="h-auto rounded-sm bg-tertiary px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label text-tertiary-foreground"`. One or two words. At most one badge on a page.
- **Cards:** a card is a plain element (an `<aside>` for a note beside the text) with `rounded-xl border bg-card p-6`. Inside it, a label comes first, with no top margin: a `<p>`, not a heading, unless this brief calls it a label heading. What follows it has `mt-2` unless this brief gives another margin. Use one only where this brief asks for it. Never put a card inside a card, and do not use the shadcn `Card`: it ships its own padding and a ring.
- **Block quotes:** a `<blockquote>` with `border-l-2 border-primary pl-6`. The quote is a `<p>` inside it in `font-heading text-h2 font-normal italic`, with no quotation marks. Who said it is a `<footer>` inside the `<blockquote>` with `mt-4`, a label with `not-italic`.
- **Figures:** a `<figure>` is wide. Its panel is `rounded-xl border bg-card p-6`, drawn from plain elements with `bg-foreground`, `bg-background` and borders: no image files, no primary color, no gradient. The `<figcaption>` is a label under the panel with `mt-2`.
- **Lists:** a list in running text is `list-disc` or `list-decimal` with `pl-6`, and `mt-2` between its items. A list of entries or titles, where this brief gives each item a top border, is a `<ul>` (or the list element this brief names) with no markers, no padding and no space between items, and no line under its last item.
- **Rules:** every line is 1px and `border-border`, except the block quote's rule.
- **Corners:** `rounded-sm` for badges, `rounded-md` for buttons and inputs, `rounded-xl` for cards and figures, `rounded-full` only for avatars.
- **Surfaces:** flat. No shadows, no blur, no gradients in the code you write. A stock component that floats over the page (a menu, a dialog) keeps the one soft shadow it ships with.
- **Icons:** from the project's icon set, with `size-4`, `strokeWidth={1.5}` and `aria-hidden="true"`. Use them only where this brief or these notes name one.
- **Small text:** a bare `<sup>` or `<small>` gets a size that is not in the type scale. Give it `text-label`.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, the only transition is color. Nothing moves on scroll. Stock components keep the motion they ship with.
