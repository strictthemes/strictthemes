### How Dispatch pages are laid out

- **Container:** the `<header>`, the `<main>` and the `<footer>` each hold one `<div>` that is the container: `mx-auto max-w-[1280px]`, with `px-4`, and `px-6` from `md` up. The three landmarks carry no classes of their own; every class these notes give the header, main or footer goes on its container `<div>`. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed. Put `text-body` on the element that wraps the whole page.
- **Fonts:** headings (`<h1>` to `<h6>`) get Big Shoulders, the headline font, on their own. `font-sans` is Newsreader, the body font: in this project it is a serif, and it is the only serif. Do not write `font-serif`. `font-mono` is Chivo Mono, for labels and figures. The theme declares the weights 400, 500, 700 and 800: use only `font-normal`, `font-medium`, `font-bold` and `font-extrabold`.
- **Headlines:** every piece of text in the headline font is `uppercase`: headlines, the masthead name, a pull quote set in it. Never sentence case. A headline is at most two lines at 1280px (three are fine between `md` and `lg` in a row of three), with no word over ten letters so nothing overflows at 375px. Do not rely on word-breaking utilities.
- **Headline sizes:** the page's one main headline is the largest text on the screen, and nothing else comes near it. It is `text-h1` below `md` and `text-display` from `md` up; this brief names which headline that is. Other story headlines are `text-h2`. A short headline in a dense list (a side column, "Most read", the "Live" strip, a related story) is a **small headline**: `font-heading text-body font-bold uppercase`. A headline that is a link holds an `<a>` in `text-foreground hover:underline decoration-2 underline-offset-4`.
- **Masthead:** the paper's name is a link to `/` in `font-heading uppercase text-foreground hover:underline decoration-2 underline-offset-4`, in the header and the footer, never a heading element. It is `text-h1` from `md` up on the front page, and `text-h2` on every other page and below `md` everywhere, so that it never competes with the page's headline.
- **Header:** a `<header>` whose container holds three rows. The top row is `flex items-center justify-between gap-4 py-2`: the date as a label in `text-muted-foreground` at the left (`WEDNESDAY 11 NOVEMBER 2026`, a `<time>` with `dateTime="2026-11-11"`), and at the right the "Subscribe" link to `#` styled as a default button. The second row is `flex items-center justify-between gap-4 border-t-2 border-b py-4 md:border-b-0`, holding the masthead name; on the front page from `md` up it is `md:justify-center` instead, with the name alone in it. Its `border-b` closes the header below `md`, where the third row is hidden; from `md` up the third row's top border does it. Below `md` the name may wrap to two lines beside the "Menu" button; that is fine. The third row is a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Sections" and `border-y py-2`, holding a `<ul>` with `flex flex-wrap gap-x-6 gap-y-2` of the section links as labels. The section links, in this order, each to `#`: News, Politics, City, Sport, Results, Business, Culture, Opinion. Each brief names the current one. The link for the current page is `text-foreground` with `aria-current="page"` and `underline decoration-2 underline-offset-4`; the others are `text-foreground hover:underline`.
- **Menus on small screens:** below `md` the third row is hidden, and a "Menu" button (`variant="outline"`) with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` sits at the right end of the second row. It shows the section links inside the `<header>`, under the second row: a second `<nav>`, placed in the source between the second row and the third, with the same `aria-label` "Sections", shown only below `md`, which carries the `id` that `aria-controls` names and has the `hidden` attribute while closed. It has a 1px bottom border and `py-2`, and holds a `<ul>` with no classes; each link is a block with `py-2`. It closes on Escape and gives focus back to the button. Do not use a `Sheet` or a `Dialog`.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` whose container is `flex flex-col gap-4 border-t-2 py-6`: the masthead name, a link to `/` in `font-heading text-h2 uppercase` with `self-start` so it is only as wide as its text, then a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Footer" holding a `<ul>` with `flex flex-wrap gap-x-6 gap-y-2` of the section links as label links, none of them current: no `aria-current` and no underline at rest. Then a label line in `text-muted-foreground` with the copyright year and the city.
- **Vertical rhythm:** written as gaps on stacks (`flex flex-col gap-*`), never as `space-y-*`, and never as a margin on an element inside a stack unless this brief names one. The container `<div>` inside `<main>` is a stack, `flex flex-col gap-12 pt-6 pb-12`: its items are the page's blocks (the page head, the lead row, each section). Inside a block: `gap-4` between a section head and what follows, between paragraphs and between stories in a list; `gap-2` inside a story unit and between a label and its value; `gap-1` between a line and a label line directly under it that belongs to it (a headline and its time, two lines of one byline) where this brief says so.
- **Grid:** a row of columns is `grid grid-cols-1` with `lg:grid-cols-12`, and a column spans what this brief gives it (`lg:col-span-8`, `lg:col-span-4`). Below `lg` the columns stack in source order.
- **Rules between columns:** columns of stories are divided by a 1px rule, not by space. The grid is `gap-4 lg:gap-0`: a row gap while the columns are stacked, and no column gap. The column after a rule has `lg:border-l lg:pl-6`, and the column before it `lg:pr-6`, so there is 24px of space on each side of the rule. Below `lg`, where the columns stack, the rule turns into a 1px top border on each column after the first, with `pt-4`, and the grid's `gap-4` above it; from `lg` up that is `lg:border-t-0 lg:pt-0`. A stacked column that opens with a section head takes no top border and no `pt-4`: the head's 2px rule divides it. Use the same construction for three stories in a row at `md`: a `<div>` with `grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3`, not a list, holding three `<article>`s that carry these classes. Each story after the first has `border-t pt-4 md:border-t-0 md:pt-0 md:border-l md:pl-6`, each story before the last `md:pr-6`, and the grid has `gap-4 md:gap-0`.
- **Rules between stories:** a list of stories stacked in a column is a `<ul>` (or `<ol>`) with `flex flex-col gap-4`, no markers and no padding. Each item after the first has a 1px top border and `pt-4`. Every line is `border-border` (nearly black on purpose), which the base styles set, so `border` alone draws it. Never use a pale line or `border-muted`.
- **Section head:** a section opens with a row `flex items-baseline justify-between gap-4 border-t-2 pt-2`: the section's name as an `<h2>` label in `font-mono text-label uppercase text-foreground` (it needs `font-mono`, since a heading gets the headline font on its own), and at the right end, where this brief gives one, a label link such as "All politics".
- **Story unit:** a story in a list is an `<article>`, a stack with `gap-2`: an optional kicker, the headline, an optional standfirst in `text-body`, and the label line. A story with a picture puts the figure first, except a page's lead story, whose headline comes before its picture (the brief says so). A story's headline is an `<h3>` inside a section and an `<h2>` where it stands under the `<h1>` with no section head between.
- **Labels:** section names, kickers, bylines, times, captions, table headers, nav links, and button and badge text are labels: `font-mono text-label uppercase`, a short fragment and never a sentence; only a caption may be a short sentence. A label line joins its parts with ` · ` (`BY HANA LINDQVIST · 09:40`). Names in a byline are uppercased like the rest of it, as `DESIGN.md` asks.
- **Text color:** headlines, body text, kickers and section names are `text-foreground`. Bylines, times, captions and helper text are `text-muted-foreground`. Paragraphs are never muted.
- **Red:** `text-primary` and `bg-primary` mean live, new or the main action, and nothing else: the "Live" marker, the "New" label (a `<span>` label `NEW` in `text-primary`, with no dot or badge, beside the time where the brief puts it), links in running text and the default button. Never a headline, a rule or a fill for decoration. The one other red is the focus outline from the rules: `outline-ring` is red in this theme, and stays so. Red text is fine on `bg-background` and `bg-card` in both modes.
- **Live marker:** `inline-flex items-center gap-2 font-mono text-label uppercase text-primary`, holding a dot (`size-2 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary`, `aria-hidden="true"`) and the word "Live". The word is always there: the dot never stands alone. The dot does not pulse or blink.
- **Yellow:** `bg-tertiary` is a fill only, always with `text-tertiary-foreground` on it, which is black in both modes. It marks one phrase (a `<mark>` with `bg-tertiary px-0.5 text-tertiary-foreground`) or the "Exclusive" badge. Never red text on yellow, and never yellow as text.
- **Badges:** the shadcn `Badge` with `className="h-auto rounded-none border-transparent bg-tertiary px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label uppercase text-tertiary-foreground"`, for "Exclusive" only. At most one on a page.
- **Dates:** "today" is always the sample date, Wednesday 11 November 2026, never the clock. Nothing on these pages reads the time. Dates and times take three forms: a time today is `09:40`; another day is `TUE 10 NOV`, with no time; the long form, in the header and a page head, is `WEDNESDAY 11 NOVEMBER 2026`. Every date and time in a label, in a byline or alone, is a `<time>` that wraps only the date or time (`UPDATED <time>09:40</time>`), with `dateTime="2026-11-11T09:40"` for a time and `dateTime="2026-11-10"` for a day or a long date. A time inside a headline or a sentence (`COUNCIL MEETS AT 18:00`) is plain text, and so is the copyright year in the footer.
- **Numbers:** every `<time>`, the long date included, and every score, position and count outside a sentence is `font-mono tabular-nums`, labels or not. In a table, numbers are right-aligned, and so is their column header. A **big figure** (a score in a score box, a number in a fact box, a rank in a numbered list) is `font-mono text-h2 tabular-nums`: this is the one use of a mono font at a heading size, because `DESIGN.md` sets scores in Chivo Mono with tabular figures.
- **Measure:** a paragraph of body text is at most `max-w-xl` wide (576px, about 65 to 70 characters a line in Newsreader at 18px), whatever column it sits in. A standfirst in `font-sans text-h2 font-normal` is capped at `max-w-3xl` instead.
- **Emphasis:** `<em>` is a real italic. Newsreader italic is also used for a pull quote. No bold inside paragraphs.
- **Links:** a link in running text is `text-primary underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-foreground` and inherits its size. A label link (in the nav, the footer, a section head) is `text-foreground hover:underline underline-offset-4`. Links get `transition-colors duration-150`. The masthead name points to `/`; every other link, the current page's own nav link and every story link included, points to `#`, unless this brief gives another `href`.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="h-auto px-4 py-2 font-mono text-label uppercase"`. It keeps its stock corners (`rounded-lg`, 2px here). The default variant (red) is the one main action, and "Subscribe" in the header is it on every page: any other button is `variant="secondary"` (black, cream in dark mode), except the "Menu" button and the unchosen buttons of a button group, which are `variant="outline"`. Its stock hover cannot be told from its resting color: in `components/ui/button.tsx`, replace the `secondary` variant's `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]` with `hover:bg-secondary/80`, once, and add no hover class on the page. A call to action that goes to another page is an `<a>` with the same classes: write `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "…")`, because `buttonVariants` alone does not merge classes. A button that its own click disables, while focus stays on it, gets `focusableWhenDisabled`; on these pages that is only the live blog's "Show new update". Base UI then sets `aria-disabled="true"` and `data-disabled` instead of the `disabled` attribute, so the stock `disabled:` classes do nothing: on a page that uses the prop, and only there, add `data-disabled:pointer-events-none data-disabled:opacity-50` beside them in `components/ui/button.tsx`. A button whose click moves focus elsewhere or removes it does not get the prop. If the project has no `Button` yet, add it with `npx shadcn@latest add button`.
- **Button groups:** a set of buttons where one is chosen (an order, a filter) is a `role="group"` with an `aria-label` and `flex flex-wrap gap-1`. The chosen button has `aria-pressed="true"` and `variant="secondary"`; the rest have `aria-pressed="false"` and `variant="outline"`. Every button in it has the Buttons classes. Pressing the chosen button again changes nothing, and a status element keeps its text.
- **Announcements:** a message that tells a screen reader what changed goes into an element with `role="status"` that is on the page from the start, empty: one that arrives together with its text is often not announced. It is never hidden with `hidden` or `empty:hidden`; where its text is for screen readers only, it is `sr-only`. Each status element here is a `<p>` whose only class is `sr-only`: it adds no gap and has no margin.
- **Pictures:** there are no image files. A picture is a `<figure>`, a stack with `gap-2`: a plain `<div>` with an aspect ratio (`aspect-3/2` unless this brief says otherwise), `w-full bg-muted` (the flat muted block `DESIGN.md` names, used only for this fill), `role="img"` and an `aria-label` that describes the scene; then a `<figcaption>` label in `text-muted-foreground` that says what it shows and ends with the credit after ` · ` (`THE KESSEL STREET BRIDGE ON TUESDAY · PHOTOGRAPH: ODILE MARSH`). Nothing is written on the fill. Pictures have square corners and no border.
- **Boxes:** a box (a fact box, a score box, the "Live" strip) is a plain element with `border bg-card p-6`, `p-4` below `md`, and square corners. Do not use the shadcn `Card`. Never put a box inside a box.
- **Tables:** the shadcn `Table` with `text-body`. Header cells are labels in `text-muted-foreground`; they keep their stock `h-10` and padding, and so do body cells. Rows keep their stock borders and hover color. A table that is wider than a phone scrolls sideways in its own box, reachable with the keyboard: `tabIndex={0}`, `role="region"` and an `aria-label`. Edit `components/ui/table.tsx`: put these on its scroll box, with the focus outline from the rules, and add a required `label` prop for the name. The grid column or flex item that holds the scroll box gets `min-w-0`, or `min-w-lg` widens the column and the page instead of scrolling. Name the table with a visible `<h2>` or `<h3>` above it, not a `<caption>`, which would scroll with the table. Give the `Table` `min-w-lg` where this brief says it scrolls on a phone.
- **Icons:** from `lucide-react`, with `size-4`, `strokeWidth={1.5}` and `aria-hidden="true"`, only where this brief names one. Never emoji.
- **Corners:** square. `rounded-none` for boxes, pictures and badges (boxes and pictures need no class at all). Buttons and the stock `Input` keep `rounded-lg`, which is 2px. `rounded-full` only for the "Live" dot.
- **Surfaces:** flat: ink on paper. No shadows, no blur and no gradients in the code you write. The page is `bg-background`; a box is `bg-card`. A stock component that floats over the page (a menu, a dialog) keeps its border and the one short shadow it ships with.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, the only transition is color. Nothing moves on scroll, and nothing animates in when it is added to the page. The stock `Button` keeps its `transition-all` and its 1px press nudge, as the rules say.
