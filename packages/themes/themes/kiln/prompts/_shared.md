### How Kiln pages are laid out

- **Page:** the page is `bg-background`. Put `bg-background text-body text-foreground` on the outermost element you write.
- **Container:** `mx-auto max-w-[1120px] px-4 md:px-6`. The header's row, the footer's row and `<main>` itself carry it. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed.
- **Grid:** where this brief gives columns, the element it names is a 12-column grid, `grid gap-x-6 md:grid-cols-12`. It sets columns and side gaps only. Below `md` the columns stack; a column that comes under another gets `mt-10 md:mt-0`.
- **Vertical rhythm:** written in one way only. `<main>` also carries `flex flex-col gap-20 pt-10 pb-20`: each section is an item of that stack, and a section never gets a top or bottom margin of its own. Inside a section, every element after the first gets its own top margin: `mt-2` between a label and what it labels, `mt-4` between a heading and its paragraph and between paragraphs, `mt-2` between a title in `text-body font-medium` and the text under it, `mt-6` between a heading and the block it opens and between form fields, `mt-10` between blocks inside a section. Never use `space-y-*`. The one other source of vertical space is the `gap-*` or `gap-y-*` of a list or grid of like items, where this brief gives one; its items never get a top margin as well. In a column of a 12-column grid, the first element has no top margin, except the `mt-10 md:mt-0` above. A list "with no markers" needs no class: Tailwind's base styles remove markers and padding.
- **Section heading:** a section opens with an `<h2>` in `text-h2`. Where a link belongs to the whole section, the heading and the link share a row, `flex flex-wrap items-baseline justify-between gap-x-6 gap-y-2`, and the row is the section's first element.
- **Headings:** a page has one `<h1>`, in `text-h1` unless this brief says `text-display`. Headings are sentence case, with no full stop. A title in `text-body font-medium`, such as a product's name in a tile, also gets `font-sans`.
- **Header:** a `<header>` that runs the full width with a 1px bottom border (`border-b`). Inside it, one `<div>` is the container and the row: `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-x-4 gap-y-2 py-4 md:gap-x-6`. On the left the studio's name, a link to `/` in `font-heading text-h2`. Then a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Main" and `hidden md:block`, holding a `<ul>` with `flex gap-6` of four links, in this order: Shop (`/`), Tableware (`/tableware`), Vases (`/vases`) and Studio (`/studio`). At the right end, a wrapper `ml-auto flex items-center gap-4` holds the "Menu" button (below `md` only) and the basket link, `inline-flex items-center gap-2`: a `ShoppingBag` icon, the word "Basket" (`hidden md:inline`) and the count in brackets in `font-mono tabular-nums` (`Basket (3)`, `Basket (0)`), with an `aria-label` that says it in words ("Basket, 3 pieces", "Basket, empty"). It goes to `/basket`, on the basket page too. The link for the current page keeps its `href` and gets `aria-current="page"` and `underline`; mark one only where this brief names the current page. A link whose destination is not given is `#`.
- **Menus on small screens:** below `md` the nav links go behind a "Menu" button (`variant="outline"`) with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, in the wrapper before the basket link. The button shows the links as a list inside the `<header>`, under its row: a second `<nav>` with `md:hidden`, the `id` that `aria-controls` names, and the `hidden` attribute while closed. It also has the `aria-label` "Main": the two are never shown at once. The second `<nav>` has a 1px top border across the full width; inside it a `<ul>` that is the container, with `py-2`, holds the same links, each a block with `py-2`. Escape closes it and moves focus to the "Menu" button. Do not use a `Sheet` or a `Dialog`.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` that runs the full width with a 1px top border. Inside it, a `<div>` that is the container, with `py-10`, holds a row `flex flex-col gap-6 md:flex-row md:justify-between`. On the left the studio's name as plain text in `font-heading text-h2` and, with `mt-2`, the address as an `<address>` with `not-italic`, two lines split by a `<br />` (the name is not repeated): "Unit 3, Coldharbour Yard" and "Bramwick BW4 2LT". On the right a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Footer" holding a `<ul>` with `flex flex-col gap-2` of four links, styled as header links, in this order: Shop (`/`), Studio (`/studio`), Delivery and returns (`/delivery`) and Care of stoneware (`/care`). Under the row, with `mt-6`, a label line with the copyright, `© 2026` and the studio's name.
- **Labels:** captions, field labels, button and badge text, small print and the line of details under a product's name are labels: `font-mono text-label`. Nothing is uppercased, in the text or with a class. A label is a short fragment; only helper text and small print may be a short sentence. Other sentences are body text. A label on a line of its own is a `<p>`; one inside a line is a `<span>`.
- **Text color:** headings, body text, prices and field labels (also the `<p>` that names a button group or a quantity) are `text-foreground`. Other labels, captions and helper text are `text-muted-foreground`. A paragraph is never muted.
- **Prices and quantities:** a price is `font-mono tabular-nums` with the currency sign against the figure and two decimals (`£34.00`), in `text-foreground`, never in the primary color. A price in a tile, a list or a summary is `text-body`; the one large price on a product page and a basket's total are `text-h2 font-medium` and stay `font-mono`, in place of the rule's `font-heading` for `text-h2`. A quantity, a count and a piece's measurements are `font-mono tabular-nums` as well. Work out money in whole pence and format it with `Intl.NumberFormat("en-GB", { style: "currency", currency: "GBP" })`.
- **Measure:** a paragraph of body text is at most `max-w-lg` wide.
- **Dates and times:** a date is written `6 November 2026` (`24 December` where the year is plain), a time `10:00`. Each is a `<time>` with a `dateTime` (`2026-11-06`, `10:00`), and a range is two; a year alone or "early December" is plain text. A date takes the font of the text around it (body, or mono in a label); a time is `font-mono tabular-nums`.
- **Links:** a link in running text is `text-primary underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-foreground` and inherits its size. Links in the header, the footer, a breadcrumb and a section heading's row are `text-foreground underline-offset-4 hover:underline`. Links get `transition-colors duration-150`.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="h-auto px-4 py-2 font-mono text-label"`. The default variant (terracotta) is for the one thing on a screen the visitor should press; a screen shows at most one, and this brief names it or says the page has none. Quiet actions are `variant="outline"`: the "Menu" button, the minus and plus of a quantity, "Remove", and the unchosen buttons of a group. Every other button is `variant="secondary"`. The stock secondary hover does not show in this theme: in `components/ui/button.tsx`, replace the `secondary` variant's `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]` with `hover:bg-secondary/80`, once, and add nothing on the buttons. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. A `w-full` button keeps the stock `whitespace-nowrap`: its label here fits at 375px. Only the minus and plus of a quantity, which their own click can disable, get `focusableWhenDisabled`, so that keyboard focus stays on them. They then carry `data-disabled` in place of `disabled`, so on a page with a quantity, and only there, add `data-disabled:pointer-events-none data-disabled:opacity-50` beside the stock `disabled:` classes in `components/ui/button.tsx`. A call to action that goes to another page is an `<a>` with the same classes: write `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "…")`. A button that holds only an icon is `size="icon"` with an `aria-label`; it keeps its stock size and padding, and takes none of the classes above.
- **Button groups:** a set of buttons where one is chosen (a category, a glaze, a delivery option) is a `role="group"` with `flex flex-wrap gap-2`. Its name is an `aria-label`; where this brief asks for a visible label above the group, the label is a `<p>` and the group points to it with `aria-labelledby`. The chosen button has `aria-pressed="true"` and `variant="secondary"`; the rest have `aria-pressed="false"` and `variant="outline"`.
- **Quantity:** a `role="group"`, named as this brief says, with `inline-flex items-center gap-2`: an `outline` button with `size="icon"`, the `Minus` icon and an `aria-label` ("One fewer"); the number in an `<output>` with `aria-live="polite"` and `inline-flex w-10 justify-center font-mono tabular-nums`, with a visually hidden "Quantity " (`sr-only`) before the figure, so a change reads "Quantity 3"; and a matching button with the `Plus` icon ("One more"). The minus is disabled at 1, the plus at the limit this brief gives.
- **Badges:** the shadcn `Badge` (add it with `npx shadcn@latest add badge`) with `className="h-auto rounded-sm border-transparent bg-tertiary px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label text-tertiary-foreground"`: one or two words. `text-tertiary-foreground` is the theme's own color for text on denim: keep it. At most one badge on a tile. Denim is a badge fill only.
- **Inputs:** the shadcn `Input` and `Textarea` with `className="h-auto px-4 py-2 text-body md:text-body"`. In `components/ui/input.tsx` and `textarea.tsx`, replace the stock `bg-transparent` and `dark:bg-input/30` with `bg-card`; `file:bg-transparent` in `input.tsx` stays. Every field has a visible `<label>` above it, a label in `text-foreground` with `block`, tied to it with `htmlFor`; the field has `mt-2`. Helper text under a field is a label in `text-muted-foreground` with `mt-2`.
- **Selects:** a choice from a list is a native `<select>` with `h-auto w-full rounded-lg border border-input bg-card px-4 py-2 text-body aria-invalid:border-destructive`. Set its width on a wrapper. It gets the same label, focus outline and error line as any field.
- **Errors:** an error is a line with `mt-2`: `flex items-start gap-2 font-mono text-label text-destructive`, holding the `CircleAlert` icon (`shrink-0`) and a sentence that says what to do. It sits under its field; where a field and a button share a row, it sits under the row. The field gets `aria-invalid` and points to the line with `aria-describedby`. Fields are checked in code when the form is sent, with `noValidate` on the `<form>`, and focus moves to the first field with an error. An error line is removed when its own field changes (on `input` or `change`); otherwise it stays until the next submit.
- **Confirmations:** a message that appears after an action goes into an element with `role="status"` that is on the page from the start, empty. It is never hidden (no `hidden` attribute, no `empty:hidden`), has no classes that draw or take space, and is never an item of a `gap-*` stack or row. The message is an element inside it, rendered only when there is a message, and it carries any margin: a row, `flex items-start gap-2 text-body`, with the `CircleCheck` icon in `text-success` and `mt-1`, and a sentence in `text-foreground`. A control that follows a message sits beside the status element, not in it. Where a message replaces a form, the form and the status element share one wrapper, and the wrapper carries the top margin this brief gives, so the message stands where the form stood; give the message `tabIndex={-1}` and move focus to it.
- **Stock and state:** a state is a mark and a word, side by side: `inline-flex items-center gap-2`. The mark is a `size-2 shrink-0 rounded-sm` square with `aria-hidden="true"`, `bg-success` for in stock and `bg-muted-foreground` for made to order or sold out; the word is a label in `text-foreground`. A state on a line of its own sits in a `<p>`, which carries its top margin. Never show a state by color alone, and never put text on `bg-success`.
- **Cards:** a card is a plain element with `rounded-xl border bg-card p-6`. Do not use the shadcn `Card`. Never put a card in a card; divide its parts with 1px borders.
- **Product tiles:** a grid of tiles is a `<ul>` with `grid grid-cols-2 gap-x-4 gap-y-10 md:grid-cols-3 md:gap-x-6 lg:grid-cols-4`. Each tile is an `<li>` holding one `<a>` to the piece, with `flex h-full flex-col overflow-hidden rounded-xl border bg-card`: the picture at its top with a 1px bottom border and no corners of its own, then the text part with `p-4`. The text part holds the piece's name as an `<h3>` in `font-sans text-body font-medium`, with `mt-1` its glaze as a label in `text-muted-foreground`, and with `mt-2` a row `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-2` holding the price and, where there is one, the badge. The tile's link draws the focus outline inside it (`focus-visible:-outline-offset-2`). A piece's address is `/tableware/` (or `/vases/`) and its name in lower case, each run of other characters than letters and figures made one hyphen, none at the ends: `/tableware/tumbler-set-of-two`.
- **Pictures:** there are no image files. A picture of a piece is a plain element with `role="img"` and an `aria-label` that says what it shows, in a fixed aspect ratio (`aspect-square` or `aspect-4/5`), `bg-muted` (a theme color, as every semantic class is), with `flex items-center justify-center`. Inside it, the piece is drawn as one plain element with `border border-foreground/40` and square corners (no `rounded-*`): `bg-glaze-pale` for a pale glaze (Ash, Oat), `bg-glaze-dark` for a dark one (Iron, Slate). Both keep their color in dark mode. Its size: `h-1/2 w-2/5` for a tall piece (a mug, a cup, a beaker, a tumbler, a jug, a vase), `h-1/4 w-3/5` for a bowl, `h-1/6 w-3/4` for a plate or a platter. In a tile the picture has `aria-hidden="true"` in place of `role="img"`. A picture that stands on its own (not in a tile or a basket line) has `rounded-xl border` and is a `<figure>` with a `<figcaption>` under it with `mt-2`, a label in `text-muted-foreground`.
- **Description lists:** details and hours are a `<dl>`. Each row is a `<div>` with a 1px top border and `py-2`, `grid grid-cols-3 items-baseline gap-x-4`: the `<dt>` is a label in `text-muted-foreground`, the `<dd>` is `col-span-2` in `text-body`. The last row also has a 1px bottom border.
- **Avatars:** `size-10 rounded-full border bg-card`, with the person's initials centered in it in `font-heading text-body`, and `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Icons:** from `lucide-react`, with `size-4` and `strokeWidth={1.5}`, only where this brief or these notes name one. An icon beside a word has `aria-hidden="true"`; an icon that stands alone sits in a button with an `aria-label`.
- **Corners:** `rounded-sm` (2px) for badges and state marks, `rounded-lg` (4px, the stock corner) for buttons, inputs and selects, `rounded-xl` (10px) for cards, tiles and pictures, `rounded-full` only for avatars. A drawn piece has square corners. No pills.
- **Primary color:** terracotta is never for any fill but the default button, nor for a heading, a price, an icon or a border. The focus color (`ring`) is terracotta too: the focus outline and a field's stock `focus-visible:border-ring` stay.
- **Surfaces:** flat. No shadows, no blur and no gradients in the code you write. A stock component that floats keeps its one short shadow.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, the only transition is color. Nothing moves on scroll or is sticky. Stock components keep the motion they ship with.
