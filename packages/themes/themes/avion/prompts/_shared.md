### How Avion pages are laid out

- **Page and panels:** the page is `bg-background`, a cool grey. Content sits on it in panels. A panel is a plain element with `rounded-xl border bg-card p-5`; do not use the shadcn `Card`, which ships its own padding and a ring. Never put a panel inside a panel: divide a panel's parts with 1px borders instead.
- **Top bar:** the first thing on the page is a `<header>` that runs the full width, `border-b bg-card`, with one row inside the container, `flex items-center py-3`: the product's name on the left, a link to `/` in `font-heading text-h2`; then, `gap-8` from it, a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Main" holding the nav links as labels with `gap-5`; then the bar's actions at the right end (`ml-auto`). For the sample product, the nav links, in this order, are Overview (`/`), Shipments (`/shipments`), Depots (`/depots`), Reports (`/reports`) and Settings (`/settings`); this brief says which of them the page shows and which is current. The link for the current page keeps its address and is `text-foreground` with `aria-current="page"`; the others are `text-muted-foreground hover:text-foreground`.
- **Container:** the top bar's row and the main content sit in a centered container, `mx-auto max-w-[1280px]`, with `px-3`, and `px-5` from `md` up. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed, unless this brief names another. Main content has `py-5`.
- **Grid:** main content is a 12-column grid with `gap-5`, a single column below `md`. The page header and every panel are items of this grid: the page header spans all twelve columns, and each panel spans the columns this brief gives it. Panels in one row stretch to the same height, unless this brief says otherwise. "Full width below `lg`" means `md:col-span-12` from `md` (the single column below `md` needs no class), so a panel of three columns from `lg` is `md:col-span-12 lg:col-span-3`. Where a grid item is a column of several panels, the column is `flex flex-col gap-5` and its last panel has `flex-1`, so that the columns of a row end on one line.
- **Page header:** main content opens with a row that is not a panel: `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-3`, with the `<h1>` in `text-h1` on the left and the page's actions on the right, `gap-2` between the actions. The row wraps where it has to; it has no breakpoint of its own.
- **Panel heading:** every panel opens with a heading row, unless this brief says a panel has none: `flex min-h-7 flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-3`. It holds a short label heading, an `<h2>` in `font-mono text-label uppercase text-muted-foreground`, and at its right end any control that belongs to the whole panel (a filter, a badge, a count). Every panel's heading row is at least `min-h-7` tall, with a control or without, so that headings and content line up from panel to panel; this brief may give one row a larger minimum. Content starts `mt-3` under the row.
- **Panels with rows:** a panel that holds a table or a list of rows has no padding of its own and `overflow-hidden`. Its heading row sits in a wrapper with `px-5 pt-5 pb-3` and a 1px bottom border; the padding goes on the wrapper, because a minimum height on a padded row would count the padding. The table or the first row starts directly under the wrapper's border, with no `mt-3`. Each row of a list has `px-5 py-3` and a 1px top border, except the first. A table's cells follow the Tables note instead.
- **Vertical rhythm:** `gap-1` between a label and its value, `gap-2` between a field's label and the field and between controls that belong together, `gap-3` between a heading and what is under it, `gap-5` between panels, between form fields and between groups inside a panel.
- **Labels:** panel headings, table headers, field labels, nav links, and button and badge text are labels: `font-mono text-label uppercase`, a short fragment and never a sentence.
- **Small figures:** a time, a date, an ID, a count with its unit, a person's name, an email address or an address on the web that this brief calls a small figure is `font-mono text-label tabular-nums` in `text-muted-foreground`, without `uppercase`, a name or an email address included (`tabular-nums` leaves letters as they are). Where this brief gives a small figure or a label another color, that color replaces `text-muted-foreground`; do not write both. What someone typed, and the name of a person or a place, is never uppercased. Units stay as they are written (`kg`, `%`).
- **Numbers:** a figure, an ID, a date or a time that stands on its own (in a table cell, as a value, as a small figure) is `font-mono tabular-nums`. This does not apply to a number inside a sentence or an address, to an ID that opens a sentence as a link, or to a value that is a phrase with a number in it (a service such as "Next day, before 18:00"). In a table, numbers are right-aligned, and so is their column header. A key number, the one figure a panel is about, is `font-heading text-h1 tabular-nums`. A clock time or a date that stands on its own is a `<time>` with a `dateTime`.
- **Dates and times:** a date is written `11 Nov`, a date with a time `11 Nov, 14:05`, and a time alone `14:05`, on the 24-hour clock; add the year (`3 Dec 2026`) only where this brief asks for it. Each one that stands on its own (a small figure, a table cell, a value) is a `<time>`; one inside a sentence is not. A duration (`38 min`) is a small figure but not a `<time>`.
- **Text color:** headings, body text and values are `text-foreground`. Labels, small figures and helper text are `text-muted-foreground`.
- **Titles inside a panel:** where this brief asks for a title in `text-body font-medium`, on an `<h3>` or any other element, add `font-sans`: it is body text in a heavier weight, not a heading in the heading font.
- **Measure:** a paragraph is at most `max-w-md` wide (448px, about 65 characters a line in this font).
- **State:** a state is a status dot and a word, side by side: `inline-flex items-center gap-2`. The dot is `size-2 shrink-0 rounded-full` with `aria-hidden="true"`, in `bg-success` for good, `bg-destructive` for failed or late, `bg-tertiary` for caution and `bg-muted-foreground` for neutral or under way; the word is `text-body text-foreground`. Where a state stands on a line of its own under something, a `<div>` around it carries the margin, not the `inline-flex` state. Never show a state by color alone, and never color the word amber: amber fails contrast as text. Use `text-success` and `text-destructive` only for a short change or error message that also has an icon or a word saying what it is.
- **Links:** a text link is `text-primary underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-foreground` and inherits its size. Links get `transition-colors duration-150`.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="px-5 font-mono text-label uppercase"`. It keeps its stock height and corners. The default variant (teal) is for the main action of the page or of a panel, and a screen shows at most one enabled default button at a time unless this brief says otherwise. Other actions are `variant="secondary"` (blue-black) or, for quiet ones, `variant="outline"`. In `components/ui/button.tsx`, replace the `secondary` variant's stock hover class, `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]`, with `hover:bg-secondary/80`, once, and add no hover class beside it or in a `className`: the stock hover is a 5% mix of the resting color, too close to see. This gives every `secondary` button, link styled as one and chosen button of a button group the new hover. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. A button that its own click disables while focus stays on it (it saves, or marks something as done, or is Previous or Next at the end of a list) gets `focusableWhenDisabled`, so that keyboard focus stays on it. A button whose click moves focus somewhere else, or removes the button, does not need it; this brief names the buttons that get it. Base UI then sets `aria-disabled` and `data-disabled` in place of the `disabled` attribute, so the stock `disabled:` classes no longer apply: on a page that uses the prop, and only there, add `data-disabled:pointer-events-none data-disabled:opacity-50` beside them in `components/ui/button.tsx`, once. A call to action that goes to another page is an `<a>` with the same classes: write `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "…")`, because `buttonVariants` alone does not merge classes. A button that holds only an icon is `size="icon"` with an `aria-label`.
- **Button groups:** a set of buttons where one is chosen (a period, a status filter) is a `role="group"` with `flex flex-wrap gap-1`. Its name is an `aria-label`; only where this brief asks for a visible label above the group is it `aria-labelledby` pointing at that label. The chosen button has `aria-pressed="true"` and `variant="secondary"`; the rest have `aria-pressed="false"` and `variant="outline"`.
- **Badges:** the shadcn `Badge` with `className="h-auto rounded-sm px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label uppercase"`, in the `outline` variant. A caution badge keeps the `outline` variant and adds `border-transparent bg-tertiary text-tertiary-foreground`, which replace the variant's border and text colors through `cn`, and is used only where something needs attention. One or two words.
- **Inputs:** the shadcn `Input` and `Textarea` with `className="text-body md:text-body"`. They keep the fill they ship with, in dark mode too. Every field has a visible `<label>` above it, styled as a label in `text-muted-foreground` and tied to it with `htmlFor`; an `aria-label` alone is enough only where this brief says so. Helper text under a field is one short sentence with `mt-2`, set in the small figure's look (`font-mono text-label text-muted-foreground`, not uppercased) though it is a sentence; the field names it in its `aria-describedby`.
- **Selects:** a choice from a short list is a native `<select>` with the classes `h-8 w-full rounded-lg border border-input bg-card px-2 text-body`. Set a width on an element around it, not on the `<select>`. It gets the same label and focus outline as any field.
- **Errors:** an error is a line with `mt-2`: `flex items-start gap-2 font-mono text-label text-destructive`, not uppercased, holding the `CircleAlert` icon and a sentence that says what to do. It sits under its field; where a field and a button share a row, it sits under the row. The field gets `aria-invalid` and points to the line with `aria-describedby`. Fields are checked in code when the form is sent, with `noValidate` on the `<form>`, and focus moves to the first field with an error. An error line is removed when its own field changes (on `input` or `change`); it does not go away on its own otherwise, and comes back at the next send if the value is still wrong.
- **Confirmations:** a message that appears after an action goes into an element with `role="status"` that is on the page from the start, empty: a status element that arrives together with its text is often not announced. It stands where the message will show and is never hidden (no `hidden` attribute, no `empty:hidden`). Where a message replaces a form, the two share one wrapper that carries the top margin. Where the message replaces the control that had focus, give it `tabIndex={-1}` and move focus to it.
- **Tables:** the shadcn `Table` with `text-body`. Header cells are labels in `text-muted-foreground`; they get `px-5` and keep their stock `h-10` and `whitespace-nowrap`, so a header is one short line. A unit in a header keeps its case: put it in a `<span>` with `normal-case`. Body and footer cells get `px-5 py-2`; the stock `[&:has([role=checkbox])]:pr-0` on a cell stays. Rows are divided by 1px borders and keep their stock hover fill, also on a table whose rows do nothing: no zebra stripes, and no extra padding to fill space. A table's name is its panel's heading: the `label` prop below repeats that heading's words, and there is no `<caption>`, which would scroll away with the table. Stock cells do not wrap, so a table with many columns is wider than a phone and often wider than a laptop: it scrolls sideways in its own box, which is reachable with the keyboard: `tabIndex={0}`, `role="region"` and an `aria-label`. Edit `components/ui/table.tsx`: put these on its scroll box, with the focus outline drawn inside it (`focus-visible:-outline-offset-2`, since a table here always fills a panel that clips), and add a required `label` prop for the name. A table that only screen readers get is a plain `<table>`, not this component.
- **Icons:** from the project's icon set, with `size-4` and `strokeWidth={1.5}`. An icon that carries meaning has a word beside it; an icon beside a word has `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Corners:** `rounded-sm` for badges, checkboxes and markers, `rounded-lg` for buttons, inputs and selects (it is what they ship with), `rounded-xl` for panels, `rounded-full` only for status dots.
- **Surfaces:** flat. No shadows, no blur and no gradients in the code you write. A stock component that floats over the page keeps the one short shadow it ships with.
- **Charts:** drawn with plain elements. Bars and lines are `bg-secondary`; at most one, the current value, is `bg-primary`, and it is also named in words. Grid lines are 1px `border-border`. Axis values are small figures. A bar's height in percent counts against a box of fixed height, not a minimum one: this brief says which box that is. Every chart has a text alternative.
- **Menus on small screens:** below `md` the nav links go behind a "Menu" button (`variant="outline"`) with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, which sits at the right end of the row; anything else at the right end of the bar is hidden below `md` unless this brief says it stays. The button shows the links as a list inside the `<header>`, under its row: a second `<nav>` with the same `aria-label`, shown only below `md`, which carries the `id` that `aria-controls` names and has the `hidden` attribute while closed. It has a 1px top border across the full width, and inside the container, with the container's side padding, `py-2`; each link is a block with `py-2`. Do not use a `Sheet` or a `Dialog`. Whether the menu is open is page state, and a page that has no other state still has this one.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, the only transition is color. Nothing moves on scroll. Stock components keep the motion they ship with.
