### How Sorbet pages are laid out

- **Phone first:** build every part for a 375px phone first, then widen it from `md` up. At 375px every control is at least 44px tall and nothing sits side by side that this brief does not put side by side.
- **Page:** one `<div>` wraps the whole page with `min-h-screen bg-background text-body text-foreground`. Inside it sit the `<header>`, a `<main>` and the `<footer>`.
- **Container:** the header's row, the main content and the footer's row each sit in a centered container: `mx-auto max-w-[1168px] px-4`, with `px-6` from `md` up, so the content is at most 1120px wide, as `DESIGN.md` gives the grid. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed.
- **Grid:** where this brief puts parts side by side, their wrapper is `grid grid-cols-1 gap-10 md:grid-cols-12 md:gap-x-6`, and each item gets the `md:col-span-*` this brief gives it. A row of cards of the same kind (key figures, plans, features) is `grid grid-cols-1 gap-6 md:grid-cols-12`: `gap-6` in both directions, with no `md:gap-x-6`. Items in one row stretch to the same height unless this brief says otherwise.
- **Vertical rhythm:** written as gaps on stacks, never as top margins and never as `space-y-*`. Every element that holds more than one thing in a column is `flex flex-col` with one of these gaps: `gap-2` between a label and what it labels, between a field's label, the field, its helper text and its error, and between a heading and a label or small figure under it (inside a card too: a label and its line get their own `flex flex-col gap-2`, and the card's `gap-4` sits between such groups); `gap-3` between the cards of one list; `gap-4` between a heading and its paragraph (one sentence in `text-body` counts as a paragraph), and between the parts inside a card; `gap-6` between the fields of a form, between a part's heading and its content, and between cards of the same kind; `gap-10` between groups on an app screen; `gap-16` between sections on the Welcome and Plans pages. The brief says which of the last two the `<main>` uses. A row of things side by side is `flex flex-wrap items-center` with `gap-3`, or `gap-2` for things that belong together (an icon and its word). Where this brief gives no gap, use the one that fits.
- **Main:** the `<main>` element itself carries the container classes, `flex flex-col`, the gap the brief names, and `pt-10 pb-16`.
- **Header:** the first thing on the page is a `<header>` with `border-b border-border`, running the full width. Inside it, one `<div>` carries both the container classes and the row's classes, `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-x-6 gap-y-2 py-3`: the app's name on the left, a link in `inline-flex h-11 items-center font-heading text-h2 text-foreground`; then the `<nav>`, the page's only one, with the `aria-label` "Main" and `w-full md:w-auto`. From `md` up the nav sits right after the name, `gap-x-6` from it, not pushed to the row's right end. The `<nav>` holds four links as a `<ul>` with `flex gap-1`, in this order: "Today" to `/today`, "Progress" to `/progress`, "Plans" to `/plans`, "Profile" to `/profile`. Welcome is not among them; the brief says which link is the current page. Each link is `inline-flex h-11 items-center rounded-lg px-3 font-mono text-label`: the current page's link has `aria-current="page"` and `bg-secondary text-secondary-foreground`; the others are `text-foreground hover:underline underline-offset-4`. Below `md` the nav takes a line of its own under the name; the four links fit on that line at 375px, so there is no "Menu" button. Anything at the row's right end has `ml-auto`.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` with `border-t border-border`, running the full width. Inside it, one `<div>` carries both the container classes and the row's classes, `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-3 py-10`: the app's name in `font-heading text-h2`, and a line in `font-mono text-label text-muted-foreground` with the copyright year as plain text: `Mossy · Made for small, daily steps · 2026`.
- **Page header:** on an app screen the `<main>` opens with a `flex flex-col gap-2`: a small figure above (the date, or a short line this brief gives), then the `<h1>` in `text-h1`. The `<h1>` is `text-display` only where this brief says so, and then only from `md` up (`text-h1 md:text-display`).
- **Headings:** an `<h2>` is `text-h2`. An `<h3>` is `text-h2` too, unless this brief gives it another size. Headings get the heading font on their own; any other element in `text-display`, `text-h1` or `text-h2` adds `font-heading`. Sentence case, no full stop.
- **Sections:** where this brief calls something a section, it is a `<section>` with `aria-labelledby` pointing at its heading, `flex flex-col gap-6`, with the heading (or a heading row) first and the content after it. Without a visible heading it gets an `aria-label`, and where it then holds one element (a grid, a card, a quote) it has no `flex flex-col gap-6`. Where a card is the section, the card carries `<section>`, and its own `gap-4` replaces the `gap-6`. The header, the page header and the footer are not sections.
- **Labels:** field labels, nav links, button and badge text, captions and the names of small groups are labels: `font-mono text-label`, in sentence case. Nothing is uppercased, in the text or with a class. A label is a short fragment. Helper text and error lines use the label style too and may be one short sentence; any longer text is body text.
- **Small figures:** a count, a time, a date, a percentage or a price note that this brief calls a small figure is `font-mono text-label tabular-nums text-muted-foreground`. The classes go on the element that holds the whole figure: a `<time>` that is the whole figure carries them itself; a `<time>` inside a longer figure (`Every day · Done at 08:12`) inherits them from its `<p>`.
- **Key figures:** the one number a card is about (a streak, a count, a price) is `font-heading text-display tabular-nums`, with its unit beside it as a small figure in a row with `flex flex-wrap items-baseline gap-2`. Where this brief asks for a smaller one, it is `font-heading text-h1 tabular-nums`.
- **Text color:** headings, body text, key figures and the names of things are `text-foreground`. Labels, small figures and helper text are `text-muted-foreground`. Paragraphs are never muted.
- **Writing:** address the reader as "you", in short sentences. An error says what happened and what to do.
- **Measure:** a paragraph of body text is at most `max-w-lg` wide.
- **Addresses:** the pages live at `/` (Welcome), `/today`, `/progress`, `/plans` and `/profile`. The app's name in the header links to `/` on Welcome and to `/today` on the other pages; each nav link points to its page's address, the current page's own included. A link whose destination this brief does not name points to `#`.
- **Dates:** "today" is the fixed sample date, Wednesday, 11 November 2026; only a time someone records (a habit marked done) and the greeting on Today come from the clock. A period such as "the last 7 days" includes today. A date is written day first with the month in full, `11 November`; the weekday goes before it (`Wednesday, 11 November`) and the year after it (`2 September 2026`) only where the brief shows them. Only a chart's axis labels shorten (`Thu`, `4 Nov`). A clock time is 24-hour, `08:12`. A date or time the brief marks as a `<time>` gets a `dateTime` (`2026-11-11`, `08:12`); one inside an `aria-hidden` drawing is plain text.
- **Links:** a text link is `text-primary underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-foreground` with `transition-colors duration-150`, and inherits its size.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="h-11 px-6 font-mono text-label"`. The stock `Button` is 32px tall and touch targets are 44px: every button and every link styled as one gets `h-11`. Button text is sentence case, two or three words; a choice in a button group may be one. The default variant (raspberry) is for the one main action of a view; no view shows two. Other actions are `variant="secondary"`, or `variant="outline"` for quiet ones. In `components/ui/button.tsx`, once: in the `secondary` variant, replace `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]` with `hover:bg-secondary/80` (the stock hover cannot be told from the resting color); in the `outline` variant, replace `bg-background` with `bg-card` and take out `dark:bg-input/30`: the card's white in both modes, its hover classes unchanged. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. A button that is disabled until something is typed or that its own click disables gets `focusableWhenDisabled`, so that keyboard focus stays on it; in this app that is only "Delete my account" on Profile. A button whose click moves focus somewhere else does not need it. With it, Base UI sets `aria-disabled="true"` and `data-disabled` in place of the `disabled` attribute, so the stock `disabled:` classes do nothing: on a page that uses the prop, and only there, add `data-disabled:pointer-events-none data-disabled:opacity-50` beside them in `components/ui/button.tsx`. A call to action that goes to another page or jumps within this one is an `<a>` with the same classes: write `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "h-11 px-6 font-mono text-label")`, because `buttonVariants` alone does not merge classes. An icon inside a button sits before the text.
- **Button groups:** a set of buttons where one is chosen (a period, a billing switch) is a `role="group"` with `flex flex-wrap gap-2` and an `aria-label`. The chosen button has `aria-pressed="true"` and `variant="secondary"`; the rest have `aria-pressed="false"` and `variant="outline"`. Not `Tabs`: there are no tab panels.
- **Inputs:** the shadcn `Input` with `className="h-11 px-4 text-body md:text-body"`. Fields only ever sit in cards, and a field and a select look the same there: in `components/ui/input.tsx`, take out the stock `dark:bg-input/30` (that class only: `bg-transparent`, `file:bg-transparent`, `disabled:bg-input/50` and `dark:disabled:bg-input/80` stay), so the input is transparent on the card in both modes, as the trigger of a `Select` is (see Selects). Every field has a visible `<label>` above it with `block`, as a label in `text-muted-foreground`, tied to the field with `htmlFor`. The label, the field, helper text and the error sit in a `flex flex-col gap-2`. Helper text is a small figure, and the field names it in `aria-describedby`.
- **Selects:** a choice from a short list is the shadcn `Select`; add it with `npx shadcn@latest add select`. Never a native `<select>`: the browser draws that one and its open list, and no control here keeps the browser's look. Write `<Select items={…} value={…} onValueChange={…}>` around `<SelectTrigger><SelectValue /></SelectTrigger>` and a `<SelectContent>` that holds one `<SelectItem value="…">` per choice directly, with no `SelectGroup`, `SelectLabel` or `SelectSeparator`. `items` is the list of the choices as `{ value, label }`: without it the trigger shows the value, not the label, where the two differ. `onValueChange` gets the new value, not an event, and its type allows `null`: ignore a `null`. The trigger fills its parent: a width the brief gives goes on a `<div>` around the `Select` alone, inside the field's stack, not on the trigger. It gets the same label and wrapper as any field: the `<label>`'s `htmlFor` is the `id` on the `SelectTrigger`, which also takes the field's `aria-describedby` and, with an error, `aria-invalid` (its red border ships with it). A select that is off gets `disabled` on the `Select`; the trigger's stock classes dim it. Edit `components/ui/select.tsx`, once, changing the classes in the file, so that the trigger is the same pill as an `Input` and the open list is a soft sheet in the card's color under it:
  - `SelectTrigger`: replace `w-fit` with `w-full`, `data-[size=default]:h-8` with `data-[size=default]:h-11` (a `h-11` in `className` does not win over it), its two stock gaps (its own and the one in `*:data-[slot=select-value]:`) with `gap-2`, its stock paddings (`py-2` and the two side paddings after it) with `pr-3 pl-4`, its small text size with `text-body`, its `focus-visible:ring-*` classes with the outline classes of the Focus rule, and its `aria-invalid:ring-*` classes, the `dark:` one included, with `aria-invalid:ring-0`. Take out `dark:bg-input/30` and `dark:hover:bg-input/50`, so the trigger is transparent on its card in both modes, like the input. Give the `ChevronDownIcon` in it `strokeWidth={1.5}`. `pr-3` is meant: the chevron is drawn 4px inside its 16px box, so its tip ends 16px from the border, as far as the text starts from the left one, and the text starts on the same line as the text of an `Input` above or below.
  - `SelectContent`: change the default `sideOffset = 4` to `sideOffset = 8`, `align = "center"` to `align = "start"` and `alignItemWithTrigger = true` to `alignItemWithTrigger = false`, so that the list opens 8px under the trigger, not over it. On the popup, replace `w-(--anchor-width) min-w-36` with `max-w-(--available-width) min-w-(--anchor-width)`, which makes the list as wide as its trigger and wider only where a choice needs it, and replace `ring-1 ring-foreground/10` with `border border-border p-2`: a solid 1px border, and 8px around the rows. Its `rounded-lg` (20px, the trigger's corner), `bg-popover` and `shadow-md` stay: a list that floats is the one thing `DESIGN.md` gives a short, soft shadow.
  - `SelectItem`: add `min-h-11`, so every row is a 44px touch target; replace its stock gap with `gap-2`, its `rounded-md` with `rounded-sm`, its vertical padding with `py-2`, its right padding with `pr-10`, its left padding with `pl-2` and its small text size with `text-body`; delete the one class that starts with `not-data-[variant=destructive]:focus:`, which would turn the check from raspberry to the text color on a highlighted row; on the `<span>` around the `CheckIcon`, replace `right-2` with `right-1` and add `text-primary`; give the `CheckIcon` `strokeWidth={1.5}`. A row is then a rounded tile inside the sheet with its text under the trigger's text; the row under the pointer or the arrow keys keeps the stock fill (`focus:bg-accent`), and the chosen row has a raspberry check under the trigger's chevron, so the two can be told apart and the choice is not marked by color alone.
- **Errors:** an error is a line under its field, inside the field's stack: `flex items-start gap-2 font-mono text-label text-destructive`, holding the `CircleAlert` icon (`size-4 shrink-0`, `aria-hidden="true"`) and one sentence that says what to do. The field gets `aria-invalid` and adds the line's `id` to its `aria-describedby`. Fields are checked in code on submit, with `noValidate` on the `<form>`; focus moves to the first field with an error. An email is well formed when, trimmed, it matches `/^[^\s@]+@[^\s@]+\.[^\s@]+$/`. An error line is removed when its own field changes (on `input` or `change`); every other error stays until the next submit.
- **Confirmations:** a message that appears after an action goes into an element with `role="status"` that is on the page from the start, empty: one that arrives with its text is often not announced. It is never hidden (no `hidden` attribute, no `empty:hidden`), and it has no classes that draw or take space: the classes, the margin included, go on the message inside it, which is rendered only while there is a message. So that an empty one adds no gap, the status element is never an item of a `gap-*` stack or row; the brief says where it sits. A short confirmation is a line `flex w-fit items-center gap-2 font-mono text-label text-success` with the `CircleCheck` icon (`size-4`, `aria-hidden="true"`) and a word or two. Where a message replaces a form, the form and the status element share one wrapper; the message's first element gets `tabIndex={-1}` and focus moves to it, because the button that had focus is gone.
- **Cards:** a card is a plain element with `rounded-xl border border-border bg-card p-6` (28px corners, 24px padding, a 1px border), and `flex flex-col gap-4` inside unless this brief says otherwise. Not the shadcn `Card`, which ships its own padding and a ring. Never put a card inside a card, and never a bordered box inside a card: divide a card's parts with 1px `border-t border-border` lines and `pt-4`.
- **Badges:** the shadcn `Badge` with `className="h-auto gap-1 rounded-sm px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label"`. A good-news badge (a streak, all done, a saving) adds `border-transparent bg-tertiary text-tertiary-foreground`: butter yellow with dark text, in both modes, and for good news only. Any other badge is `variant="secondary"`. One to four words. An icon in a badge comes before the words with `aria-hidden="true"` and no size class: the stock `Badge` sets its icons to 12px. `bg-tertiary` is a fill only: never use butter yellow as a text color, a border or a chart color.
- **State:** never shown by color alone. `text-success` and `text-destructive` are only for a short message that also has an icon.
- **Meters:** a bar that shows a share (a habit's completion) is a track `h-6 w-full overflow-hidden rounded-sm border border-border bg-background` holding a fill `h-full bg-secondary`, whose width is set with an inline `style`. The track has `aria-hidden="true"`, which hides the fill too; the number beside it says the share.
- **Charts:** drawn with plain elements. Bars are `bg-secondary` with square corners, on 1px `border-border` grid lines; axis values are small figures, in a column as tall as the plot: the top value hangs under the top line (`absolute top-0`), the bottom value stands on the bottom line (`absolute bottom-0`). No primary, yellow or success fills in a chart. A chart's drawing has `aria-hidden="true"` and a text alternative.
- **Pictures:** no image files. A picture is drawn from plain elements in a card with `role="img"` and an `aria-label`.
- **Avatars:** a circle with the person's initials: `flex size-11 shrink-0 items-center justify-center rounded-full bg-secondary font-mono text-label text-secondary-foreground`, `aria-hidden="true"` beside the written name.
- **Icons:** from `lucide-react`, with `size-4` (`size-6` where this brief says so; none inside a `Badge`) and `strokeWidth={1.5}`. An icon beside a word has `aria-hidden="true"`. Beside a line of body text in an `items-start` row, the icon gets `mt-1` to sit on the first line. Never an emoji. Only where named.
- **Corners:** `rounded-sm` (10px) for badges and small tiles (the drawn check boxes in the preview, meters, the rows in a select's list), never on a shape less than 20px tall; `rounded-lg` (20px, what buttons, inputs and selects ship with) for buttons, inputs, selects, a select's open list and nav links; `rounded-xl` (28px) for cards; `rounded-full` only for avatars and the stock `Switch`.
- **Surfaces:** flat: no shadows, blur or gradients in the code you write. The open list of a `Select` keeps the one short shadow it ships with.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, the only transition is color. Nothing moves on scroll, and nothing bounces when a habit is done. Stock components keep the motion they ship with.
