### How Primer pages are laid out

- **Page:** cream paper with white cards on it. The outermost element is a `<div>` with `flex min-h-svh flex-col bg-background text-body text-foreground`, holding the `<header>`, a `<main>` (`flex-1`) and the `<footer>`.
- **Container:** the header's row, the content of `<main>` and the footer's row each sit in a centered container `<div>`: `mx-auto w-full max-w-[1240px] px-5`, so the content is at most 1200px wide, as `DESIGN.md` gives the grid, with 20px at the page's edge at every width. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed.
- **Sample organisation:** "Rowlark", an invented online school that teaches practical data skills in three topics: spreadsheets, SQL and charts. It has nine courses, 102 lessons in all, and three teachers: Samir Okonjo (spreadsheets), Odile Brandt (SQL) and Lena Farrow (charts). The learner is Tessa Marlow. Prices are in US dollars. The same courses, lessons, lengths and prices appear on every page; this brief gives the ones it needs.
- **Today:** the sample's today is Sunday, 14 March 2027, a fixed date in code, never the device's clock.
- **Dates and lengths:** a date is written day first with the month in full, `14 March 2027`, and the year only where this brief shows it. A date that stands on its own (a small figure, a row of a list) is a `<time>` with a `dateTime` (`2027-03-14`); inside a sentence it is plain text. A length is hours and minutes with a space before each unit, `3 h 10 min`, and minutes alone under an hour, `16 min`. A count carries its noun: `12 lessons`.
- **Addresses:** the pages live at `/catalogue`, `/course`, `/lesson`, `/progress` and `/plans`. The school's name in the header links to `/catalogue`. A link whose destination this brief does not name points to `#`.
- **Grid:** columns stand 32px apart and cards 20px, as `DESIGN.md` gives them. Where this brief puts parts side by side, their wrapper is `grid grid-cols-1 gap-8 lg:grid-cols-12`, a single column below `lg`, and each item gets the `lg:col-span-*` this brief gives it. A row of cards of the same kind is `grid grid-cols-1 gap-5` with the columns this brief names. Items in one row stretch to the same height unless this brief says otherwise.
- **Vertical rhythm:** written as gaps on stacks, never as top margins and never as `space-y-*`. Every element that holds more than one thing in a column is `flex flex-col` with one of these gaps: `gap-1` between a name and the small figure under it; `gap-2` between a heading and a small figure above or under it, between a field's label, the field, its helper text and its error, and between the items of a list of short lines; `gap-3` between a heading and its paragraph and between paragraphs; `gap-5` between the parts inside a card, between a section's heading and its content, and between cards; `gap-8` between the groups of a page and between columns; `gap-20` between sections, the 80px of `DESIGN.md`. A row of things side by side is `flex flex-wrap items-center` with `gap-3`, or `gap-2` for things that belong together (an icon and its word). The scale has no 16px and no 24px step: where a habit asks for one, use `gap-3` or `gap-5`. Where this brief gives no gap, use the one that fits.
- **Main:** the container inside `<main>` is `flex flex-col` with `pt-8 pb-20` and the gap this brief names: `gap-20` on a page made of sections (Catalogue, Course, Plans), `gap-8` on a page that is one piece of work (Lesson, Progress).
- **Header:** the first thing on the page is a `<header>` with `border-b border-border`, running the full width. Its container is also the row, `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-x-8 gap-y-1 py-3`: the school's name, "Rowlark", a link in `inline-flex h-10 items-center font-heading text-h2 text-foreground`; then the `<nav>`, the page's only one named "Main" (`aria-label`), with `w-full md:w-auto`. It holds a `<ul>` with `flex gap-5` of three links, in this order: "Courses" to `/catalogue`, "My progress" to `/progress`, "Plans" to `/plans`. Each link is `inline-flex h-10 items-center font-sans text-body font-semibold text-foreground underline-offset-8`: the current page's link has `aria-current="page"` and `underline decoration-2`; the others have `hover:underline`. Below `md` the nav takes a line of its own under the name; the three links fit on it at 375px, so there is no "Menu" button and the header has no state. This brief says which link is current.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` with `border-t border-border`, running the full width. Its container is also the row, `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-3 py-8`: the school's name in `font-heading text-h2`, as a `<p>`, and a small figure: `Rowlark · Practical data skills, one short lesson at a time · 2027`.
- **Breadcrumb:** where this brief asks for one, it is a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Breadcrumb" holding an `<ol>` with `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-2`, in `font-mono text-label`: each step but the last a link in `text-muted-foreground underline underline-offset-4 hover:text-foreground`, a `/` in `text-muted-foreground` with `aria-hidden="true"` between steps, and the last step in `text-foreground` with `aria-current="page"`, not a link. Because the breadcrumb then says `page`, the header's "Courses" link has `aria-current="true"` in place of `page`, with the same look.
- **Headings:** Lexend, sentence case, no full stop, at the weight its type class sets: do not add a weight class to a heading. The `<h1>` is `text-h1`; it is `text-display` only where this brief says so, and then only from `md` up (`text-h1 md:text-display`). An `<h2>` is `text-h2`. An `<h3>` is `text-h2` too, unless this brief gives it another size. A small heading inside a card that this brief calls a name is `font-sans text-body font-semibold`: body text at weight 600, not the heading font. Headings get the heading font on their own; any other element in `text-display`, `text-h1` or `text-h2` adds `font-heading`.
- **Sections:** where this brief calls something a section, it is a `<section>` with `aria-labelledby` pointing at its heading, `flex flex-col gap-5`, with the heading (or a heading row) first and the content after it. Without a visible heading it gets an `aria-label`, and where it then holds one element it has no stack classes. Where a card is the section, the card carries `<section>`, and its own `gap-5` is the section's. A heading row is `flex flex-wrap items-end justify-between gap-3`: the heading on the left, and on the right what belongs to the whole section, a small figure or a control. The header, the footer and a page's opening are not sections.
- **Labels:** Fira Code sets what is counted, measured or typed: levels, lengths, counts, scores, percentages, dates, code, and also field labels, table headers, breadcrumbs, badge text and captions. A label is `font-mono text-label`, in sentence case, never `uppercase`, a short fragment. Buttons and nav links are not labels: they are in the body font.
- **Small figures:** a length, a count, a date, a percentage or a short note that this brief calls a small figure is `font-mono text-label tabular-nums text-muted-foreground`. The classes go on the element that holds the whole figure: a `<p>` where it stands on a line of its own, a `<span>` inside a row or a line, unless this brief names the element. Parts of one figure are joined with ` · `. A `text-foreground` in this brief replaces the muted color: write the two through `cn`, so that one replaces the other. A small figure with an icon is `inline-flex items-center gap-2` with the icon first.
- **Key figures:** the one number a card is about (a count, a price) is `font-heading text-display tabular-nums`, with its unit beside it as a small figure, in a row with `flex flex-wrap items-baseline gap-2`: a `<p>` holding the number and the unit as two `<span>`s, unless this brief names the elements. Where this brief asks for a smaller one, it is `font-heading text-h1 tabular-nums`.
- **Text color:** headings, body text, names, key figures and the figures of a chart are `text-foreground`. Labels, small figures and helper text are `text-muted-foreground`, and only on the page or a card, never on a fill. Paragraphs are never muted.
- **Writing:** address the reader as "you", in short, plain sentences, the way a patient teacher talks. No exclamation marks.
- **Measure:** a paragraph of body text is at most `max-w-2xl` wide.
- **Links:** a text link is the text color, underlined: `text-foreground underline underline-offset-4 hover:decoration-2`, and it inherits its size. A link that is a card's or a row's title has no underline until hover: `text-foreground underline-offset-4 hover:underline`. The class lists in these notes and in this brief leave out the focus outline: add the outline classes from the rules to every link and to every other element you write that takes focus.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="h-10 px-5 font-sans text-body font-semibold"`: the body font at weight 600, in sentence case, two to four words. The stock `Button` ships a smaller size and `font-medium`; these classes replace both, and its stock corner (`rounded-lg`, 10px) stays. The default variant is for the one main action of a view, deep blue with white text, yellow with ink text in dark mode: no view shows two. Every other button is `variant="secondary"`, yellow with ink text, a lighter blue with white text in dark mode. `variant="outline"` is only for the buttons of a button group that are not chosen. No `ghost`, `link` or `destructive` buttons. In `components/ui/button.tsx`, once, on every page: in the `secondary` variant, replace `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]` with `hover:bg-secondary/80` (the stock hover cannot be told from the resting color); on a page with a button group, in the `outline` variant, replace `bg-background` with `bg-card` and take out `dark:bg-input/30`, so the button is the card's white on the cream page, its hover classes unchanged. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. No button on these pages is ever disabled, so none gets `focusableWhenDisabled`, and `button.tsx` needs no `data-disabled:` classes. A call to action that goes to another page is an `<a>` with the same classes: write `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "h-10 px-5 font-sans text-body font-semibold")`, because `buttonVariants` alone does not merge classes. An icon in a button is `size-4` with `aria-hidden="true"` and no `data-icon`, so the button keeps its `px-5`, and sits where this brief puts it.
- **Button groups:** a set of buttons where one is chosen (a level, a week) is a `<div>` with `role="group"`, `flex flex-wrap gap-2` and an `aria-label`. The chosen button has `aria-pressed="true"` and `variant="secondary"`; the rest have `aria-pressed="false"` and `variant="outline"`. Not `Tabs`: there are no tab panels. Pressing one moves no focus.
- **Inputs:** the shadcn `Input` with `className="h-10 px-3 text-body md:text-body"`, its stock corner. In `components/ui/input.tsx`, replace the class `bg-transparent` and the class `dark:bg-input/30` with `bg-card`, so a field is white on the cream page in light mode and the card's color in dark mode; `file:bg-transparent` and the `disabled:` fills stay. A field is a `flex flex-col gap-2`: a visible `<label>` with `block`, a label in `text-muted-foreground`, tied to the field with `htmlFor`; then the field; then any helper text, a small figure that the field names in `aria-describedby`.
- **Errors:** an error is one line under what it is about: `flex items-start gap-2 font-mono text-label text-destructive`, holding the `CircleAlert` icon (`size-4 shrink-0`, `aria-hidden="true"`) and one sentence that says what to do. A field gets `aria-invalid` and names the line's `id` in its `aria-describedby`; a `RadioGroup` names the line in its `aria-describedby` and takes `aria-invalid` too, as the Forms rule says. A form is checked in code on submit, with `noValidate` on the `<form>`. The error line is removed when its control changes. The error color is for this line only: a wrong answer to a question is not an error and is told in words in the text color.
- **Confirmations:** a message that appears or changes after an action goes into an element with `role="status"` that is on the page from the start: one that arrives with its text is often not announced. Where it starts empty it is never hidden, has no classes that draw or take space, and is never an item of a `gap-*` stack or row: it shares a plain `<div>` with the element above it, and the message inside it, rendered only while there is one, carries the look and the space, such as `mt-5`. A status element that always holds text (a count of results) may be an item of a stack like any other.
- **Cards:** a card is a plain element with `rounded-xl border border-border bg-card p-5` (16px corners, 20px padding, a 1px border, no shadow), and `flex flex-col gap-5` inside unless this brief says otherwise. Not the shadcn `Card`, which ships its own padding and a ring. Never put a card inside a card, and never a bordered box inside a card: divide a card's parts with 1px rules, `border-t border-border` with `pt-5`.
- **Rows with rules:** a list whose rows are divided by rules (lessons, a legend, recent activity) is a `<ul>` or an `<ol>` with `flex flex-col` and no gap. Each row has `py-3 first:pt-0 last:pb-0 border-border not-first:border-t`: a rule above every row but the first.
- **Badges:** the shadcn `Badge` (`npx shadcn@latest add badge`) with `className="h-auto rounded-sm border-transparent bg-tertiary px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label text-tertiary-foreground"`: green with ink text in both modes, one or two words, no icon. The corner must be in this `className`, or the stock pill wins. There is one kind of badge. Green is a fill only: never write `text-tertiary`.
- **Chart colors:** four fills, the same in both modes, used in this order: `bg-azure`, `bg-tertiary` (green), `bg-sun` (yellow), `bg-berry`. A chart with one series is azure; a chart with more takes them in this order. Never `bg-secondary`, `bg-primary` or a `chart-*` class in a chart: `bg-secondary` and `bg-primary` change with the mode, and the yellow of a chart is always `bg-sun`. Each topic keeps one color on every page: Spreadsheets azure, SQL green, Charts berry. Sun is for the time spent on practice files, which belongs to no topic. No text is ever set on azure, sun or berry, and no chart color is a text color. Sun is a pale fill on a white card, so, as with every chart color, it is never the only way to read something: the word or the figure is always beside it.
- **Topic marks and legend dots:** a topic is shown as `inline-flex items-center gap-2`: a dot, `size-2 shrink-0 rounded-full` with the topic's fill and `aria-hidden="true"`, then the topic's name, a label in `text-muted-foreground` unless this brief gives another style. The same dot is the dot of a chart's legend.
- **Progress bars:** a row, `flex items-center gap-3`: a track, `h-2 min-w-0 flex-1 overflow-hidden rounded-full bg-border`, holding a fill, `h-full rounded-full bg-tertiary`, whose width is a percentage in an inline `style`; then the percentage, `25%`, as a small figure in `text-foreground` with `w-10 shrink-0 text-right`. At 0% there is no fill. The track has `aria-hidden="true"`, which hides the fill too; the percentage beside it and the count this brief puts near it (`3 of 12 lessons done`) say the same in words. Percentages are whole numbers.
- **Charts:** drawn with plain elements, flat: a bar is a `<div>` with one fill, square corners and no border, whose height or width is a percentage in an inline `style`, worked out in code from the data. Every bar has its figure written beside or above it in `font-mono text-label tabular-nums text-foreground`. Grid lines are 1px `border-border`; axis values are small figures. A chart's drawing has `aria-hidden="true"` and the text alternative this brief names.
- **Avatars:** a circle with a person's initials: `flex size-10 shrink-0 items-center justify-center rounded-full bg-secondary font-mono text-label text-secondary-foreground`, with `aria-hidden="true"`, beside the written name. Where this brief asks for a larger one it is `size-12`, never more.
- **Lesson rows:** a lesson in a list has its number, its title and its length. The title is `text-body` and the length a small figure; the number is a small figure in a column of its own, or leads the title in the title's own style (`4. Filtering rows with WHERE`), as this brief says. Where a lesson has a state (done, next, locked), the state is an icon and a word, never a color.
- **Accordions:** the shadcn `Accordion` (`npx shadcn@latest add accordion`), inside a card that has `px-5 py-0` in place of the card's padding and no stack classes, so each trigger's own padding is the card's. The stock `Accordion` is off the type scale. In `components/ui/accordion.tsx`: on the trigger, replace `items-start` with `items-center`, its stock vertical padding with `py-3`, and its small text size and `font-medium` with `font-sans text-body font-semibold`, and add `min-h-10 gap-3`; on the content, replace the small text size with `text-body`; on the content's inner element, replace the stock bottom padding with `pb-5` (`pt-0` stays) and delete the class that starts with `[&_p:not(:last-child)]`, which gives paragraphs a bottom margin off the scale. The items keep the stock 1px rule between them. Each trigger sits in an `<h3>` that the component renders, so an accordion always follows an `<h2>`. Give each `AccordionItem` a `value`.
- **Icons:** from `lucide-react`, with `size-4` (`size-5` where this brief says so) and the stroke they ship with, 2px with round caps: do not set `strokeWidth`. An icon beside a word has `aria-hidden="true"`. Beside a line of body text in an `items-start` row, the icon sits on the first line: `mt-1` for a `size-4` icon, `mt-0.5` for a `size-5` one. Only where named. Never an emoji.
- **Corners:** `rounded-sm` (6px) for badges and the stacked bar of a chart; `rounded-lg` (10px, what buttons and inputs ship with) for buttons, inputs and a code block; `rounded-xl` (16px) for cards; `rounded-full` only for avatars, progress bars, legend dots, the stock `Switch` and a radio button, none of them over 48px. A chart's bars have square corners.
- **Surfaces:** flat. No shadows, blur or gradients in the code you write.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, the only transition is color: `transition-colors duration-150` on a link whose color changes on hover, which on these pages is a breadcrumb's link; a link that only gains an underline or a thicker one gets none. A progress bar does not animate, and nothing moves on scroll. Stock components keep the motion they ship with, and so does a link written with `buttonVariants`.
