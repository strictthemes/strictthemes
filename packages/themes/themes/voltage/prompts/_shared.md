### How Voltage pages are laid out

- **Page:** the outermost element of the page is a `<div>` with `min-h-screen bg-background font-sans text-body text-foreground`. The page carries the color: paint nothing else yellow, hover colors included.
- **Container:** `mx-auto max-w-[1200px] px-3 md:px-5`. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed. Sections fill its full width.
- **Grid:** where a section has panels side by side, it is a grid with `grid grid-cols-1 gap-5 md:grid-cols-12`, and each item spans the columns this brief gives it (`md:col-span-4` and so on). Below `md` every item takes the full width, in source order. Panels in one row stretch to the same height.
- **Vertical rhythm:** every vertical distance is a `gap-*` on a `flex flex-col` stack or on a grid, or a padding this brief names. No `mt-*`, `mb-*` or `space-y-*`, except the icon margin in the Icons note. The steps: `gap-1` between a value and the small line under it; `gap-2` between a label and the thing it labels, between a field and its error line, and between the buttons of a group; `gap-3` between a heading and the text under it, between paragraphs, between the items of a list and between buttons side by side; `gap-5` between blocks inside a panel, between form fields and between panels; `gap-8` between a section's heading row and its content; `gap-16` between sections.
- **Main:** the `<main>` element carries the container and the stack: `mx-auto max-w-[1200px] px-3 md:px-5 flex flex-col gap-16 py-8 md:py-16`. Each section is a `<section>` with `flex flex-col gap-8` and no padding, whatever its children. No background bands and no borders around sections.
- **Links and addresses:** the pages live at `/` (the event page), `/line-up`, `/tickets`, `/info` and `/artists/<slug>` for an act (`/artists/ossa-bloom`). A link this brief names without an address goes to the page it names; any other link with no destination is `#`.
- **Header:** a `<header>` that runs the full width of the window with `border-b border-border`. Inside it, a `<div>` carries the container and the row: `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-x-8 gap-y-3 py-3`. On the left the festival's name, "Tremor Days", a link to `/` in `font-heading text-h2 text-foreground` with no underline at rest and `hover:underline underline-offset-4`; then a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Main" and `hidden md:flex gap-5`, holding three links as labels, in this order and with no `<ul>`: Line-up (`/line-up`), Tickets (`/tickets`) and Info (`/info`). Then at the right end (`ml-auto`) the festival's dates as a label, `4–5 Dec 2026`, `hidden md:block`. A nav link is `text-foreground hover:underline underline-offset-4`. The current page's link has `aria-current="page"` and `underline decoration-2 underline-offset-4`; this brief says which one it is, if any. The header holds no button besides "Menu".
- **Menus on small screens:** below `md` a "Menu" button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` sits at the right end of the row, with `ml-auto md:hidden`: the dates that carry the `ml-auto` from `md` up are hidden below it. It is a quiet button from the Buttons note. It shows the same three links, stacked, inside the `<header>`, under its row: a second `<nav>` with the same `aria-label` "Main", `md:hidden`, which carries the `id` that `aria-controls` names and has the `hidden` attribute while closed. The second nav has a 1px `border-t border-border` across the full width, and inside it a `<div>` with the container and `py-2` that holds the links directly, with no `<ul>`; each link is `block py-2`. It closes on Escape, which returns focus to the button. Do not use a `Sheet` or a `Dialog`.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` that runs the full width with `border-t border-border`. Inside it, a `<div>` carries the container and the stack, `flex flex-col gap-3 py-8`: the festival's name as plain text in `font-heading text-h2`, one sentence in `text-body` saying what it is, and a label line with the place, the dates and the copyright (`Varsby Docks · 4–5 Dec 2026 · © 2026 Tremor Days`).
- **Headings:** Headings stay short: five words or fewer, sentence case, no full stop, and no single word longer than nine letters in an `<h1>`. The page's `<h1>` is `text-h1 md:text-display`: 38px on a phone, 64px from `md` up. It is the only `text-display` text on the page, unless this brief names another element that uses it. A section's `<h2>` is `text-h2 md:text-h1`. A heading inside a panel is an `<h2>` or `<h3>`, whichever keeps the order, in `text-h2`.
- **Section heading row:** a section opens with a row, `flex flex-wrap items-end justify-between gap-3`, holding its `<h2>` and, where this brief gives one, a link at the right end.
- **Labels:** dates, times, prices, stage names, field labels, nav links, captions, table headers, and button and badge text are labels: `font-mono text-label font-bold uppercase`, in `text-foreground`. The `font-bold` holds the weight inside stock components. A label is a short fragment. Inside a label line, stage names (Crane Stage) and the festival's name are uppercased with the rest; in the header's and the footer's name, a heading and a sentence they keep their case. Only the name of a person or an act keeps its case in a label line: put it in a `<span>` with `normal-case`.
- **Small print:** helper text, the note under a total, a count such as `3 of 10 sets`: `font-mono text-label text-muted-foreground`, never uppercased, fragment or sentence. A count in small print adds `tabular-nums`. A caption is a label in `text-muted-foreground`. Paragraphs are never muted, and nothing inside a button or a field is muted.
- **Numbers and dates:** a date, a time, a price or a count that stands on its own is `font-mono tabular-nums`. A large number, the one figure a panel or a block is about, is `font-heading text-h1 tabular-nums`. A range is two `<time>` elements joined by an en dash: `<time dateTime="2026-12-04">4</time>–<time dateTime="2026-12-05">5 Dec 2026</time>`, and `17:30–18:15` the same way with full date-times. Every date and clock time that stands on its own or sits in a label, a heading or a table is a `<time>` with a `dateTime` (`2026-12-04T16:00+01:00`, or `2026-12-04` for a day), the dates in the header and the footer included; a year alone (the copyright) and a button's text stay plain. A number in running text (a sentence, or a line of a list in `text-body`) is plain text in that text's font; a date or a time there is a `<time>` only where this brief says so. Dates come day first, with no leading zero: in a label the short form (`Fri 4 Dec`, `4–5 Dec 2026`), in a heading, a sentence or a table cell the long form (`Friday 4 December`). Times are 24-hour (`16:00`). Nothing on these pages depends on today's date except the countdown, which reads the clock.
- **Text on the page and on panels:** short text may sit straight on the yellow page: headings, labels, one line of facts, and at most two sentences of body text. Anything longer goes on a panel. A paragraph is at most `max-w-xl` wide.
- **Panels:** a panel is a plain element with `flex flex-col gap-3 rounded-xl border border-border bg-card p-5`. Where this brief gives a panel another gap, it replaces `gap-3`. Do not use the shadcn `Card`. Never put a panel inside a panel: divide a panel's parts with a 1px `border-t border-border` and the panel's gap.
- **Panels with rows:** a panel that holds a list of rows edge to edge has no padding of its own, `gap-0` and `overflow-hidden`. Its heading row sits in a wrapper with `px-5 pt-5 pb-3` and a 1px `border-b border-border`. Each row has `px-5 py-3` and a 1px `border-t border-border`, except the first.
- **Links:** a text link is `text-foreground underline underline-offset-4 hover:decoration-2` and inherits its size. No link changes color, so a link has no transition. Links are never blue or pink.
- **Focus:** a link you write also gets the outline from the Focus rule, which the class lists here leave out; a stock component, the `SelectTrigger` included, gets it in its own file.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="h-auto px-5 py-2 font-mono text-label font-bold uppercase"`, keeping its stock `rounded-lg` (14px). The default variant (black with yellow text) is for the one main action, and a screen shows at most one. The second action is `variant="secondary"` (electric blue, white text). In `components/ui/button.tsx`, in the secondary variant, replace the class `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]` with `hover:bg-secondary/80`, since the stock hover cannot be told from the resting color; add no hover class where you use the variant. Quiet actions, the "Menu" button included, are `variant="outline"`: white with a black outline on the page and on a panel. In the same file, in the outline variant, replace `bg-background` (the page's yellow) with `bg-card`; replace `hover:bg-muted`, `aria-expanded:bg-muted` and `dark:hover:bg-input/50` with `hover:bg-accent`, `aria-expanded:bg-accent` and `dark:hover:bg-accent` (`bg-muted` is yellow too); and delete `dark:bg-input/30`, which would beat `bg-card` in dark mode and leave the button see-through. Keep `dark:border-input`. A button that holds only an icon is `size="icon"`, quiet, with an `aria-label`. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. A button that keeps the focus while its own click disables it gets `focusableWhenDisabled`; this brief names each one. Base UI then sets `data-disabled` and not `disabled`, so on a page that uses the prop, and only there, add `data-disabled:pointer-events-none data-disabled:opacity-50` beside the stock `disabled:` classes in `components/ui/button.tsx`. A call to action that goes to another page is an `<a>` with the same classes: write `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "…")`, because `buttonVariants` alone does not merge classes. A button or a link with `inline-flex` that is a direct child of a `flex-col` stack stretches to the stack's width: give it `self-start` unless this brief makes it full width.
- **Button groups:** a set of buttons where one is chosen (a day) is a `role="group"` with `flex flex-wrap gap-2`, named by an `aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby` where this brief gives it a visible label. The chosen button has `aria-pressed="true"`, `variant="secondary"` and the `Check` icon before its text, so the fill is not the only sign; the rest have `aria-pressed="false"` and are quiet buttons. Not `Tabs`: there are no tab panels.
- **Badges:** the shadcn `Badge` with `className="h-auto rounded-sm border-transparent bg-tertiary px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label font-bold uppercase text-tertiary-foreground"`: hot pink with black text, one or two words, no icon. At most one badge on a page. Any other state is a word.
- **Inputs:** the shadcn `Input` with `className="h-auto px-3 py-2 text-body md:text-body"`. It keeps its stock `rounded-lg` and black `border-input` outline; `px-3` puts its text on the same line as the text of a `Select` above or below. Give it the same fill as a select: in `components/ui/input.tsx`, replace the class `bg-transparent` with `bg-card` and delete `dark:bg-input/30`; `file:bg-transparent`, `aria-invalid:border-destructive` and `dark:aria-invalid:border-destructive/50` stay. Fields sit on panels. Every field has a visible `<label>` above it with `block`, tied to it with `htmlFor`, styled as a label; a field and its label are a stack with `gap-2`. No placeholder text.
- **Selects:** a choice from a short list is the shadcn `Select`; add it with `npx shadcn@latest add select`. Never a native `<select>`: the browser draws that one and its open list, and no control here keeps the browser's look. Write `<Select items={…} value={…} onValueChange={…}>` around `<SelectTrigger><SelectValue /></SelectTrigger>` and a `<SelectContent>` that holds one `<SelectItem value="…">` per choice directly, with no `SelectGroup`. `items` is the list of the choices as `{ value, label }`: without it the trigger shows the value, not the label, where the two differ. `onValueChange` gets the new value, not an event, and its type allows `null`: ignore a `null`. The trigger fills its parent: set a width on an element around it, not on the trigger. It gets a label and an error line like any field: the `<label>`'s `htmlFor` is the `id` on the `SelectTrigger`, which also takes `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby` and a `ref` for moving focus to it. Edit `components/ui/select.tsx`, once, changing the classes in the file, so that the trigger looks like an `Input` and the open list is the theme's:
  - `SelectTrigger`: replace `w-fit` with `w-full`, `bg-transparent` with `bg-card`, its two stock gaps (its own and the one in `*:data-[slot=select-value]:`) with `gap-2`, its left padding with `pl-3` (`py-2 pr-2` stay), its small text size with `text-body`, its `focus-visible:ring-*` classes with the outline classes of the Focus rule, and its `aria-invalid:ring-*` classes, the `dark:` one included, with `aria-invalid:ring-0`. Delete the three classes that start with `data-[size=`, which fix its height and give the small size its own corner, and `dark:bg-input/30` and `dark:hover:bg-input/50`, which would beat `bg-card` in dark mode. The trigger is then as tall as an `Input`, from the same padding and line. On the `ChevronDownIcon` in it, replace `text-muted-foreground` with `text-foreground`: nothing inside a field is muted. `pr-2` is meant: the chevron is drawn 4px inside its 16px box, so its tip ends 12px from the border, as far as the text starts from the left one.
  - `SelectContent`: change the default `align = "center"` to `align = "start"` and `alignItemWithTrigger = true` to `alignItemWithTrigger = false`, so that the list opens under the trigger, not over it. On the popup, replace `w-(--anchor-width) min-w-36` with `max-w-(--available-width) min-w-(--anchor-width)`, which makes the list as wide as its trigger and wider only where a choice needs it, and replace `ring-1 ring-foreground/10` with `border border-border p-1`: a black outline, like every panel and field. Its `rounded-lg`, `bg-popover` and `shadow-md` stay: the Surfaces note lets a stock menu keep its short shadow.
  - `SelectItem`: replace its stock gap with `gap-2`, its vertical padding with `py-2`, its left padding with `pl-2` (`pr-8` stays), its stock corner with `rounded-sm` and its small text size with `text-body`. Replace `focus:bg-accent` and the class after it, which sets the highlighted row's text color, with `focus:bg-foreground focus:text-background`, and delete the one class that starts with `not-data-[variant=destructive]:focus:`, which would turn the row's text and its check back to the ink color, black on black. On the `<span>` around the `CheckIcon`, replace `right-2` with `right-1`. A row is then a rounded bar inside the list, 4px from its outline, with its text under the trigger's text. The row under the pointer or the arrow keys is filled with ink, like a picture: black with yellow text in light mode, cream with black text in dark mode. The chosen row has a check under the trigger's chevron, in the row's text color.
- **Errors:** an error is a line under its field, in the field's stack: `flex items-start gap-2 font-mono text-label text-destructive`, not uppercased, holding the `CircleAlert` icon (`shrink-0`) and a sentence that says what to do. The field gets `aria-invalid` and points to the line with `aria-describedby`. Fields are checked in code when the form is sent, with `noValidate` on the `<form>`, and focus moves to the first field with an error. An error line is removed when its own field changes (on `input` or `change`); an error that depends on a second field is also removed when that field changes. Every other error stays until the next submit.
- **Confirmations:** a message that replaces a form goes into an element with `role="status"` that is on the page from the start, empty; one that arrives with its text is often not announced. The status element has no classes that draw or take space, and is never hidden. The form, the status element and any button that follows the message share one wrapper, a plain `<div>` with no gap, so the empty status element adds no space. Inside the status element, rendered only with the message, an element carries the message's layout; its first element has `tabIndex={-1}` and gets focus, because the button that had focus is gone. A button after the message sits in the wrapper, outside the status element, in a `<div>` with `pt-3` rendered only with the message. The theme has no success color: a confirmation is `text-foreground`, with the `Check` icon and words that say what happened.
- **Tables:** the shadcn `Table` with `text-body` on it. Header cells are labels in `text-muted-foreground`, with `px-3` and their stock height. Body cells get `px-3 py-2`. Add `first:pl-0 last:pr-0` to both, since a table here sits in a padded panel. Rows are divided by 1px borders; in `components/ui/table.tsx` remove the row's yellow fills: `hover:bg-muted/50`, `has-aria-expanded:bg-muted/50` and `data-[state=selected]:bg-muted`. The table scrolls sideways in its own box: in the same file give the scroll box `tabIndex={0}`, `role="region"` and an `aria-label`, and add a required `label` prop for the name. The visible name is the heading above the table's panel; no `<caption>`.
- **Pictures:** there are no image files. A picture is a plain element with an aspect ratio (`aspect-square` or `aspect-video`), `rounded-xl border border-border bg-foreground`, `role="img"` and an `aria-label` that says what it shows. Anything drawn inside it is `bg-background`, with no corners, or text in `text-background`. A picture with a caption is a `<figure>` with `flex flex-col gap-2`, its `<figcaption>` a caption.
- **Avatars:** a circle, `size-12 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-foreground`, holding the act's initials in `font-mono text-label font-bold text-background`, centered with `flex items-center justify-center`, with `aria-hidden="true"`, since the name stands beside it.
- **Icons:** from `lucide-react`, with `size-4` and the default 2px stroke. Use them only where this brief or these notes name one. An icon beside a word has `aria-hidden="true"`. In an `items-start` row beside `text-body`, an icon gets `mt-1` so it lines up with the first line; beside a label it needs none.
- **Corners:** `rounded-sm` (6px) for badges, checkboxes and the rows of a select's list, `rounded-lg` (14px) for buttons, inputs, selects and a select's open list, `rounded-xl` (22px, the `lg` corner in `DESIGN.md`) for panels and pictures, `rounded-full` only for avatars and countdown dots.
- **Colors that are not text:** `bg-secondary` (blue) fills buttons only. `bg-tertiary` (pink) fills the one badge only. Never use `text-secondary`, `text-tertiary` or `text-primary`: text is `text-foreground`, `text-muted-foreground`, `text-destructive`, or the foreground class of the fill it sits on.
- **Surfaces:** flat. No shadows in the code you write, offset "sticker" shadows included, no glow, no blur and no gradients. Every line is 1px `border-border`. A stock menu or dialog keeps the short shadow it ships with.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, the only transition is color. Nothing moves on scroll, nothing pulses or blinks, and a number that changes just shows its new value. Stock components keep the motion they ship with, such as the `Button`'s 1px nudge while pressed.
