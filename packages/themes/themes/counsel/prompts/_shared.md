### How Counsel pages are laid out

- **Container:** one `<div>` wraps the whole page and is the container: `mx-auto max-w-[1200px] px-4 md:px-8`, plus `text-body`. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed. Inside it sit the `<header>`, a `<main>` holding everything between the two, and the `<footer>`.
- **Grid:** a section's content sits in a grid: `grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-12 md:gap-x-8`. The grid sets columns only, never `gap-y-*`: every vertical distance comes from the rhythm rule. Below `md` every grid item takes the full width. Leave the columns this brief does not fill empty; an empty column is part of the design, so center nothing to fill it. From `md` up every grid item gets `md:col-span-*`, because an item without one takes a single column: "columns 1–12" is `md:col-span-12`. It also gets `md:col-start-*` when it starts after column 1, and `md:col-start-1` when it must start a new row under an item that leaves room beside it; or the grid puts it in that room. An item whose columns overlap those of the item before it always starts a new row. Where a brief puts "a section's content in columns 5–12", the `<section>` is the grid and the content is one `<div>` with those column classes. Where a list "is itself the grid", the `<section>` is not a grid: the heading above the list sits directly in it at the full width.
- **Text and note:** running text sits in seven of the twelve columns (`md:col-span-7`, or the columns this brief gives). Beside it, where this brief asks for one, sits one short note in columns 9–12 (`md:col-start-9 md:col-span-4`), with `mt-14 md:mt-0` on a phone, so it comes after the text. Nothing else stands beside the text.
- **Vertical rhythm:** written as a top margin on the lower element (`mt-*`), never as `space-y-*`, `gap-y-*`, a bottom margin or padding, except the padding a section gets around its hairline (see Sections) and the padding of a card or a list entry, which these notes give. Between a label and the one thing it names (a form label and its field, a name and the role under it, a term and its value when they stack): `mt-2`. Between a heading and the text under it, and between paragraphs: `mt-4`. A label line above a heading, and a label heading above its text, its `<address>` or its `<dl>`, count as headings: `mt-4` to what follows. Between blocks inside a section (a paragraph and a button, a heading and a list, a card and the text after it, two parts of a side column): `mt-8`. Between two parts of a section that each have their own `<h2>`: `mt-14`. Where two grid items share a row from `md` up, the second has its phone margin and `md:mt-0`, unless this brief gives a margin from `md` up. Inside a flex row (the header, a label line with a badge, a group of buttons) the space is the row's `gap-*` as this brief states it. Where this brief gives an element its own margin, it replaces the one from this list.
- **Sections:** `<main>` holds a stack of `<section>` elements. The first has `mt-14 md:mt-28` and no border: the header's hairline is above it. Every other section is divided from the one before it by one hairline, with white space on both sides of it: `mt-8 border-t border-border pt-8 md:mt-14 md:pt-14`, which is 112px from section to section on a large screen and 64px on a phone, with the line in the middle. That is the whole gap and the only padding a section has; the section's first element takes no top margin of its own.
- **Header:** the `<header>` is itself the row, with `border-b border-border py-8`: `flex flex-col items-start gap-4 md:flex-row md:items-baseline md:justify-between`. On the left the firm's name, `Harrow & Vane`, a link to `/` in `font-heading text-h2`, styled as a header link and never marked as the current page. It is not a heading element, and it has no weight class. On the right a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Main" and no classes, holding a `<ul>` with `flex flex-wrap gap-x-4 gap-y-2 md:gap-x-8` of four links as labels, in this order: "Practice" (`/practice/commercial-disputes`), "People" (`/people/ruth-calloway`), "Insights" (`/insights/notice-clauses`) and "Contact" (`/contact`). Each brief names the current one, if any. Below `md` the links sit under the name and may wrap onto a second line; there is no "Menu" button.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` with `mt-14 md:mt-28`, a 1px `border-border` top border and `py-8`. Inside it a grid like the one above, with three parts:
  - Columns 1–4 (`md:col-span-4`): the firm's full name as plain text in `font-heading text-h2`, `Harrow & Vane LLP`, then with `mt-2` the office's address: one `<address>` with `not-italic font-mono text-label uppercase text-muted-foreground` holding two `<p>` lines with no margin between them, `4 Chancery Row` and `Wexcombe WX1 2LN`.
  - Columns 5–8 (`md:col-start-5 md:col-span-4`, with `mt-8 md:mt-0`): two `<p>` label lines, the second with `mt-2`: the telephone number `01632 960 214` as a `tel:01632960214` link, and the email address `enquiries@harrowvane.example` as a `mailto:` link in `normal-case`. Both styled as footer links.
  - Columns 9–12 (`md:col-start-9 md:col-span-4`, with `mt-8 md:mt-0`): a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Footer" holding a `<ul>` of the header's four links, in the same order, as labels, `mt-2` between items. The current page is marked here too, with `aria-current="page"` and `underline`.
  - Under the grid, with `mt-8`, a label line in `text-muted-foreground`: `© 2026 Harrow & Vane LLP · Registered in England and Wales`.
- **Headings:** the `<h1>` is `text-h1`, and `text-display` from `md` up where this brief says so (`text-h1 md:text-display`). An `<h2>` in the heading font is `text-h2`, and so is an `<h3>`. Nothing in the heading font is smaller than `text-h2`: a smaller heading is a label heading. Headings keep the regular weight their type class gives them: add no weight class, never `font-bold` or `font-semibold`. Sentence case, no full stop.
- **No bold, no italics:** Marcellus has one weight and no italic, and nothing on these pages fakes either. No `<em>`, `<i>`, `<strong>`, `<b>` or `<cite>`, and no `italic` or bold class, in headings or in running text. An `<address>` is italic by default: give every `<address>` `not-italic`.
- **Labels:** nav links, dates, matter numbers, the names of practice areas in a label line, roles under a name, form labels, terms in a `<dl>`, and button and badge text are labels: `font-mono text-label uppercase`. The wide tracking comes with `text-label`; do not add a `tracking-*` class. Write a label as a short fragment, not a sentence. Error lines and helper text use the label's font and size but are sentences, and are not uppercased. Join the parts of a label line with ` · `. A label on its own line is a block element such as a `<p>`. Email addresses and what someone typed are never uppercased: put an email address inside a label in a `<span>` (or give its link) `normal-case`.
- **Label headings:** a heading that is styled as a label (Address, Hours, Contact) is an `<h2>` or `<h3>`, whichever keeps the heading order, with `font-mono text-label uppercase text-foreground`. Keep the `font-mono`: a heading element gets the heading font on its own.
- **Text color:** headings, label headings, body text, nav links, names and form labels are `text-foreground`. A button's or a badge's text color comes from its variant or its classes. Dates, matter numbers, roles under a name, `<dt>` terms, caption lines and helper text are `text-muted-foreground`. Muted text sits only on `bg-background` or `bg-card`. Paragraphs are never muted. No other text colors: gold (`tertiary`) and navy (`primary`) are never text colors.
- **Measure:** a paragraph of body text is at most `max-w-xl` wide (576px, about 70 characters in Jost at this size), however many columns it sits in. A heading may run wider.
- **Figures:** a sum, a count or a year that stands on its own in a label is `font-mono tabular-nums`. A large figure in a row of figures is `font-heading text-h1`, where this brief says so. Sums are written with the pound sign before the number: `£6.2m`, `£48m`, `£1,480`. A date or a year that stands on its own (in a label line, a `<dd>`, a list entry) is a `<time>` with a `dateTime`. A date or a year inside a sentence (running text, a confirmation message, an error line) is plain text, not a `<time>`, unless this brief says otherwise. A date is written `10 November 2026` (`dateTime="2026-11-10"`), a time `08:30`, a year `2026`. The copyright year is plain text.
- **Telephone numbers** are written in groups, `01632 960 214`; a `tel:` link's `href` has the digits with no spaces (`tel:01632960214`).
- **Links:** a link in running text is `text-foreground underline decoration-1 underline-offset-4 hover:text-muted-foreground`, and inherits its size and font. A link in the header or the footer, and a name or a title that is a link, is `text-foreground decoration-1 underline-offset-4 hover:underline`; the link for the current page has `aria-current="page"` and `underline`. Mark one only where this brief names the current page. Links are never colored, and get `transition-colors duration-150`. A link whose destination this brief does not give points to `#`; the firm's name always points to `/`.
- **Onward links:** a link that stands alone at the end of a part ("All partners", "All briefings") is a `<p>` holding the link (also where a brief puts it in a heading's row), as a label (`font-mono text-label uppercase`) styled as a link in running text, in `inline-flex items-center gap-2`, followed by the `ArrowRight` icon.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="h-auto px-8 py-2 font-mono text-label font-normal uppercase"`: `font-normal` because the labels are regular. The default variant (navy, gold in dark mode) is for the page's one main action: one on a page, unless this brief gives a second. Any other button is `variant="secondary"` (black, near-white in dark mode). In `components/ui/button.tsx`, replace that variant's stock `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]` with `hover:bg-secondary/80`, once, not beside it: the 5% mix cannot be told from the resting color. Make this change even on a page with no secondary button, so every page's `button.tsx` is the same. Do not use the `outline`, `ghost`, `link` or `destructive` variants, or the `xs`, `sm` or icon sizes. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. A call to action that goes to another page, or a `mailto:` or `tel:` link styled as a button, is an `<a>` with the same classes: write `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "…")`, because `buttonVariants` alone does not merge classes. Button text is at most four words.
- **Inputs:** the shadcn `Input` and `Textarea` (`npx shadcn@latest add input textarea`) with `className="h-auto border-muted-foreground px-4 py-2 text-body md:text-body"`. The stock `border-input` is too faint against the page and the card; `border-muted-foreground` reaches 3:1 in both modes. They keep their stock 2px corner, the fill they ship with (in dark mode too), the stock `focus-visible:border-ring` and the stock `aria-invalid:border-destructive`. Every field has a visible `<label>` above it with `block`, tied to it with `htmlFor` and styled as a label in `text-foreground`; the field has `mt-2`. Fields follow each other with `mt-8`, on the wrapper `<div>` that holds a label, its field and its error. Optional fields say so in the label: `Telephone (optional)`.
- **Selects:** a choice from a short list is a native `<select>` with `h-auto w-full rounded-md border border-muted-foreground bg-transparent px-4 py-2 text-body text-foreground dark:bg-input/30 aria-invalid:border-destructive dark:aria-invalid:border-destructive/50 focus-visible:border-ring` and the four `focus-visible:outline-*` classes from the rules: the fill, border and error classes are the ones the stock `Input` ships, so the two look alike in both modes; these two `dark:` classes are the one exception to the rules' "no `dark:` color overrides". It takes the same label, margins and error line as any field, and shows an error with `aria-invalid` and its red border, like an `Input`. Its first option is a prompt with an empty value.
- **Checkboxes:** the shadcn `Checkbox` (`npx shadcn@latest add checkbox`). In `components/ui/checkbox.tsx`, change its `rounded-[4px]` to `rounded-md` and its `border-input` to `border-muted-foreground`, once, so it has the inputs' corner and border (where the file ships another corner or border class, change that one). Its stock size, check icon and checked fill (`bg-primary`) stay. Its row is `flex items-start gap-4`, with `mt-1` on the checkbox so it lines up with the first line of its label, and the `<label>` in `text-body text-foreground`, not uppercased: it is a sentence, not a label. Give the `Checkbox` an `id` and the `<label>` `htmlFor` with it, so a click on the words ticks the box; give the `<label>` an `id` too, and the `Checkbox` `aria-labelledby` naming it, because in a Base UI project the `Checkbox`'s `id` lands on a hidden input and the visible box would otherwise have no name.
- **Errors:** an error is a line under its field with `mt-2`: a `<p>` with an `id`, `flex items-start gap-2 font-mono text-label text-destructive`, not uppercased, holding the `CircleAlert` icon (`shrink-0`, no margin) and a sentence that says what to do. The field gets `aria-invalid` and points to the line with `aria-describedby` (beside any helper line it already names). Fields are checked in code when the form is sent, with `noValidate` on the `<form>`, and focus moves to the first field with an error. Render the error lines first and move focus after that render, in an effect or a `requestAnimationFrame`, so the field's description is there when it is read. An error line and its field's `aria-invalid` are removed when that field changes (on `input` or `change`; for the checkbox, on `onCheckedChange`). Every other error stays until the next send.
- **Forms:** a form sits on the pale grey: the `<form>` is `rounded-xl bg-card p-8`, with no border and no shadow. The send button sits under the last field with `mt-8`.
- **Confirmations:** a message that replaces a form goes into an element with `role="status"` that is on the page from the start, empty. The form and the status element share one wrapper, and the wrapper carries the top margin and the column classes, so the message stands where the form stood. The status element has no classes and no margin, so empty it takes no space; never hide it. Inside it, rendered only once there is a message, a `<div>` with `rounded-xl bg-card p-8`, like the form: an `<h2>` or `<h3>` in `text-h2` (whichever keeps the heading order), then with `mt-4` one or two sentences in `text-body`, then whatever this brief adds. A button that follows the message sits after the status element in the same wrapper, not inside it, with `mt-8`. Give the heading `tabIndex={-1}` and move focus to it, because the button that had focus is gone.
- **Badges:** exactly one badge on a page, where this brief puts it, and it is the page's one use of gold: the shadcn `Badge` (`npx shadcn@latest add badge`) with `className="h-auto rounded-sm border-transparent bg-tertiary px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label font-normal uppercase text-tertiary-foreground"`. One or two words, no icon. Nothing else on the page is `bg-tertiary`. In dark mode the gold moves to the main button and the badge turns pale grey, as `DESIGN.md` says; that needs no code.
- **Cards:** a card is a plain element with `rounded-xl bg-card p-8`: pale grey, no border, no shadow. Not the shadcn `Card`, which ships its own padding and a ring. Inside it, a label comes first, with no top margin. Use one only where this brief asks for it, never a card inside a card, and nothing with a border or a fill inside a card except the fields of a form.
- **Portraits:** there are no image files. A portrait is a rectangle, never a circle: a plain `<div>` with `aspect-3/4 w-full rounded-xl border border-border bg-card`, `role="img"` and an `aria-label` that describes the photograph in a sentence ("Ruth Calloway in a dark suit, at a window of the firm's library"). It is drawn as a monogram plate: the `<div>` also has `flex items-end p-4` (`p-8` for a portrait this brief calls large), and holds one `<span>` with `aria-hidden="true"`, the person's initials in `font-heading text-h1 text-muted-foreground` (`text-display` for a large portrait). Nothing else is drawn in it: no icon, no gradient, no shape. A portrait never sits inside a card.
- **Lists of people and matters:** a list where this brief gives each item a 1px top border (people, matters, briefings, steps) is a `<ul>` or `<ol>` with `list-none`, no padding, and no space between its items; each item has `border-t border-border py-8`, unless this brief gives `py-4`, and the list a `border-b border-border` that closes it. In each item a name or a title is in the heading font (an `<h3>` in `text-h2`, or the element this brief names), and the role, the date or the matter number is a label line in `text-muted-foreground`, `mt-2` under the name or above the title as this brief says. Where this brief lays an entry out in columns, the `<li>` itself carries the grid classes beside these, and its parts are the grid items.
- **Description lists:** a `<dl>` is a list of rows. Each row is a `<div>` with `grid grid-cols-3 items-baseline gap-x-4 border-t border-border py-4`: the `<dt>` is a label in `text-muted-foreground`, the `<dd>` takes the other two columns (`col-span-2`) in `text-body`. The `<dl>` has `border-b border-border`, so the last row is closed too. This holds at 375px as well, unless this brief stacks the rows.
- **Rules:** every line is 1px and `border-border`. Hairlines, not boxes, divide a page. No shadows, no blur, no gradients, no colored backgrounds behind sections.
- **Corners:** `rounded-sm` (1px) for the badge, `rounded-md` (2px) for the select and the checkbox, the stock 2px of the `Button`, `Input` and `Textarea` (their `rounded-lg`, which this theme sets to 2px), and `rounded-xl` (4px, the `lg` corner in `DESIGN.md`) for cards, forms and portraits. Nothing is a pill or a circle: no portrait, no badge, no dot and no switch is round.
- **Icons:** from the project's icon set, `lucide-react`, with `size-4`, `strokeWidth={1.5}` and `aria-hidden="true"`. Use them only where this brief or these notes name one. Never emoji.
- **Surfaces:** flat. A stock component that floats over the page (a menu, a dialog) keeps the one short shadow it ships with; these pages use none.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, the only transition is color, and nothing moves on scroll. Stock components keep the motion they ship with, the `Button`'s included.
