### How Ember pages are laid out

- **Page:** one `<div>` wraps the whole page: `min-h-screen bg-background text-body text-foreground`. Inside it sit the `<header>`, the `<main>` holding everything between the two, and the `<footer>`. Ember lives in dark mode: build and look at every part in dark mode first, then in light mode, and keep both right.
- **Container:** `mx-auto max-w-[1280px] px-3 md:px-6`. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed. The `<main>` element is itself a container and also carries `pt-12`. The header and the footer run the full width of the window, with their lines; the container is a `<div>` inside each. Every other line stays inside the container.
- **Grid:** where this brief gives columns, the element is `grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-12 md:gap-x-6` (24px gutters) and each item says its span, such as `md:col-span-7`. The grid sets columns only, never vertical space. Below `md` items stack in source order.
- **Vertical rhythm:** written as a top margin on the later element, never as `space-y-*` and never as a gap on a column of mixed content. A heading under a label has `mt-2`. A paragraph under a heading, and a paragraph under a paragraph, has `mt-3`. A block under text (a row of buttons, a list, a table, a meter, a row of cards) has `mt-6`. A new group inside a section has `mt-12`. A `<section>` inside `<main>` has `mt-20` (80px), except the first, which has none: the `pt-12` on `<main>` is the space above it. Where two grid items share a row from `md` up and stack below it, the second gets `mt-6 md:mt-0` when it follows on from the first (a picture beside its text) and `mt-12 md:mt-0` when it is a separate group (a card, a column with its own heading); each brief writes which. Gaps are only for items side by side in a row (`flex`, `flex-wrap`, a grid of cards), where this brief or these notes name one. Inside a list of rows, a row has its own top border and padding and no margin. Where a part of a brief gives a margin that differs from this note, the brief's part wins.
- **Headings:** headings are Oxanium, which `<h1>` to `<h6>` get on their own; no full stop. Every heading set at `text-h1` or `text-display`, at any width, is `uppercase`: the page's big title and every section `<h2>`. Write their text in sentence case in the code; the class sets the capitals. A heading in `text-h2` alone (in a card, a row, a tile) stays in sentence case. The page's big title is `text-h1 md:text-display`, the only `text-display` text on the page unless a brief gives a big figure that size. A section's `<h2>` is `text-h2 md:text-h1 uppercase`. Keep every word of an uppercase heading to nine letters or fewer, so nothing runs out of the screen at 375px. Titles wrap; never truncate one.
- **Labels:** versions, dates, times, ranks, regions, prices, eyebrows, captions, nav links, table headers and button and badge text are labels: `font-mono text-label uppercase`. Write their text in sentence case ("Patch 4.2 · Tue 10 Nov"); the `uppercase` class sets the capitals. `text-label` already carries the tracking; add no `tracking-*` class. A label is a short fragment; anything that is a sentence is body text. A table's body cells are not labels: they are `text-body`, with the Figures classes where they hold a number, unless a brief gives a column label classes. Parts of a label line are joined with ` · `. A label on its own line is a block element such as a `<p>`. Martian Mono is wide: a label of more than about 30 characters will not fit a phone's line, so keep them short.
- **Figures:** every number that stands on its own (a rank, a rating, a score, a price, a count, a version, a date or a time in a label, a table cell or a big figure) gets `font-mono tabular-nums`. A **big figure** is a number set large: `font-mono text-h1 tabular-nums`, or `font-mono text-h2 tabular-nums` where a brief says so; it is never uppercased and has no label classes. Where a big figure and a label share one `<p>` (`61 days left`), the `<p>` has no classes but its margin, the figure is a `<span>` with the big figure classes, and the label is a `<span>` with `ml-2`. A heading that holds a number (`Patch 4.2`) stays in Oxanium and adds only `tabular-nums`. Thousands take a comma (`2,418`), a change takes a sign (`+21`, and `−17` with the minus sign `−`, not a hyphen), a rate takes one decimal (`55.4%`). A number inside a sentence is plain text. A label that holds any digit (a date, a time, a count, a price, a year, `5v5`, `Season 4`) adds `tabular-nums` to its label classes, on the label's own element, even where a brief lists only `font-mono text-label uppercase`.
- **Dates:** "today" is always the sample date, Thursday 12 November 2026, never the clock; nothing on these pages reads the time. In a label a date is short, `Tue 10 Nov`, with the year only where a brief writes one (`Tue 10 Nov 2026`); in a sentence it is written in full, `Tuesday 10 November`. Times are 24-hour (`09:40`). A date or a time that stands on its own is a `<time>` with a `dateTime` (`2026-11-10`, or `2026-11-12T09:40` with a time). A date range in a label (`6 Oct – 12 Jan 2027`) is two `<time>` elements joined by an en dash in plain text.
- **Text color:** headings, body text, nav links, table body cells, big figures and labels that name a thing (a player, an item) are `text-foreground`. Other labels, captions and helper text are `text-muted-foreground`, and muted text sits only on `bg-background` or `bg-card`. Running text (the sentences under a heading, a summary, a description) is never muted. A one-line note that a brief or the Footer note gives in `text-muted-foreground` (the footer's line, a note under a table) is a caption, not running text, and stays muted. A button's text color comes from its variant. `text-primary` is never used: red is not a text color in this theme.
- **Red:** `bg-primary` is ember red, brighter in dark mode, for the screen's one main action: a default `Button` or a link styled as one. Each brief names it or says the page has none, and there is never a second. It is never a text color, a border, a band or the fill of anything else. The focus outline from the rules (`outline-ring`) is red too, and stays.
- **Amber:** `bg-tertiary` is for one badge on a page, always with `text-tertiary-foreground` (blue-black in both modes). It is never a text color, a border or a fill of anything else.
- **Heat:** `bg-heat-low` (deep red), `bg-heat-mid` (orange) and `bg-heat-high` (amber) are the theme's own three-step scale, the same in both modes. They are fills only: for meters, rank marks, the viewer's row in a table, chart bars and the shapes in a drawn picture. Never a text color, a border, a button, or a fill with text on it. `bg-heat-low` sits close to the page in dark mode and `bg-heat-high` close to it in light mode, so a heat fill never carries meaning alone: the value or the step is always written next to it in words or figures.
- **Meters:** a meter is a `<div>` with `role="meter"`, `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax`, `aria-valuenow`, an `aria-valuetext` in words and an `aria-labelledby` that points to its label. Its track is `h-3 w-full border border-border bg-muted`, with square corners, and holds one fill, `h-full` with one heat class and its width as an inline `style` (`style={{ width: "61%" }}`). A meter always has its value written beside or above it as a label or a big figure. Each brief says which value takes which of the three steps.
- **Rank marks:** a small square, `size-3 shrink-0 rounded-sm` with one heat class and `aria-hidden="true"`, always followed by the step's name in words in a row `flex items-center gap-2`. The tiers of Ranked Clash are Inferno (rating 2,800 and up, `bg-heat-high`), Blaze (2,600 to 2,799, `bg-heat-mid`) and Cinder (under 2,600, `bg-heat-low`).
- **Measure:** a paragraph of running text is at most `max-w-xl` wide.
- **Links:** a text link is `text-foreground underline decoration-1 underline-offset-4 hover:decoration-2`: it is the text color, underlined, and its line thickens under the pointer. A link that holds a title (inside a heading, and the game's name in the header) is `text-foreground decoration-1 underline-offset-4 hover:underline`. A nav link is a label in `text-foreground` with `underline-offset-4 hover:underline`; the link for the current page has `aria-current="page"` and `underline decoration-2`. Links get `transition-colors duration-150`.
- **Link destinations:** the five pages are `/` (Game), `/patch`, `/leaderboard`, `/store` and `/player`; where the project has another router layout, use its paths. A link whose destination this brief does not name points to `#`.
- **Header:** a `<header>` with `border-b border-border`. Its container `<div>` is also the row: `flex items-center justify-between gap-6 py-3`. On the left the game's name, "Ashfall", a link to `/` in `font-heading text-h2 uppercase`, never marked as the current page. On the right, a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Main" and `hidden md:block`, holding a `<ul>` with `flex gap-6` of five label links, in this order: Game (`/`), Patch notes (`/patch`), Leaderboard (`/leaderboard`), Store (`/store`), Profile (`/player`). The brief names the current one.
- **Menu on small screens:** below `md` the `<nav>` is hidden and a "Menu" button (`variant="outline"`, with the button classes below) stands at the right end of the row, with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. It shows the links as a list inside the `<header>`, under its row: a second `<nav>` with the same `aria-label` and `md:hidden`, which carries the `id` that `aria-controls` names and has the `hidden` attribute while closed. It has a 1px top border across the full width (`border-t border-border`, on the `<nav>` itself, with the container `<div>` inside it) and, inside the container, `py-2`; each link is a block with `py-2`. It closes on Escape, pressed while focus is on the Menu button or in the menu (one `onKeyDown` handler on the `<header>`, acting only while the menu is open), and focus then goes to the Menu button; it also closes when a link in it is chosen. Do not use a `Sheet` or a `Dialog`.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` with `mt-20`, `border-t border-border` and `py-12`. Inside it, the container `<div>` holds a grid (`md:grid-cols-12`): columns 1–6 hold the game's name as plain text in `font-heading text-h2 uppercase` and, with `mt-2`, a `<p>` in `text-body text-muted-foreground`: "A team arena game by Lowlight Games."; columns 7–12 hold a `<ul>` (not a `<nav>`) of the same five links as the header, as text links in `text-body`, with `flex flex-wrap gap-x-6 gap-y-2`, and `mt-6 md:mt-0`. These links take no `aria-current`: only the header marks the current page. Under the grid, with `mt-12`, a label line in `text-muted-foreground`: `© 2026 Lowlight Games`.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="h-auto px-6 py-2 font-mono text-label uppercase"`. It keeps its stock `rounded-lg` corner (3px). The default variant (red) is the screen's one main action, as the Red note says. Every other action is `variant="secondary"` (blue-black, warm white in dark mode), or `variant="outline"` for quiet ones. The `secondary` variant's stock hover is the same color as its fill: in `components/ui/button.tsx`, replace its `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]` with `hover:bg-secondary/80`, once, on every page, whether or not the page has a secondary button, and add no hover class where you use it. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. A link that looks like a button (to another page, or to a part of this one) is an `<a>` with the same classes: write `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "…")`, because `buttonVariants` alone does not merge classes. Such a link is `inline-flex`, so a top margin on it works: it needs no wrapper. If the project has no `Button` yet, add it with `npx shadcn@latest add button`.
- **Button groups:** a set of buttons where one is chosen (a region, a class) is a `role="group"` with an `aria-label` and `flex flex-wrap gap-2`. The chosen button has `aria-pressed="true"` and `variant="secondary"`; the others have `aria-pressed="false"` and `variant="outline"`. Every button in it has the Buttons classes. Pressing the chosen button again changes nothing. Not `Tabs`: there are no tab panels.
- **Badges:** the shadcn `Badge` (add it with `npx shadcn@latest add badge`) with `variant="secondary"` and `className="h-auto rounded-sm border-transparent bg-tertiary px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label uppercase text-tertiary-foreground"`: the variant is named only because its classes hold no red, and these classes replace its fill and text color. One or two words, and at most one badge on a page.
- **Status dots:** the only full round in this theme: `size-2 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-foreground`, `aria-hidden="true"`, always beside a word that says the state. It does not pulse or blink. Only where a brief names one.
- **Announcements:** a message that tells a screen reader what changed goes into an element that is on the page from the start, empty or with its first text: one that arrives together with its text is often not announced. It is never hidden with `hidden` or `empty:hidden`. Each brief says whether it is a visible line with `aria-live="polite"` or a `<p>` with `role="status"` and `sr-only`.
- **Cards:** a card is a plain element with `rounded-xl border border-border bg-card p-6` (6px corners, 24px padding, a 1px border). Inside it, a label comes first where it has one, and the rest follows the rhythm above. Never put a card inside a card, and never a box with a border on all four sides inside a card: divide a card's parts with `border-t border-border` and `pt-3`. Do not use the shadcn `Card`.
- **Pictures:** there are no image files. A picture is a plain element in a fixed aspect ratio, `bg-muted`, with `role="img"` and an `aria-label` that says what it shows, and the shapes in it are plain `<div>`s with heat fills or `bg-foreground`, square corners, no border and no text, placed with `flex` or with `absolute` and fraction classes (`left-1/4`, `bottom-1/3`, `w-1/12`, `h-1/2`) as each brief gives them. A picture that stands on the page has `relative overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-border`. A picture at the top of a tile has no corners of its own and only a bottom border (`border-b border-border`): the tile is `overflow-hidden` and clips it, so it never reads as a card inside a card. Nothing glows: no gradient, no blur, no shadow, no opacity trick.
- **Lists of rows:** a list of news or entries is an `<ol>` or `<ul>` with no markers and no padding. Each row has `border-t border-border py-6`, and the list has `border-b border-border` so the last row closes.
- **Tables:** the shadcn `Table` (add it with `npx shadcn@latest add table`) with `text-body`. Edit `components/ui/table.tsx`: its scroll box, the `<div>` around the `<table>`, gets `tabIndex={0}`, `role="region"`, an `aria-label` from a new required `label` prop, and the focus outline. Header cells are labels in `text-muted-foreground` with `px-3`; they keep the stock `h-10`. Body cells have `px-3 py-2`. Add `first:pl-0 last:pr-0` to every cell, unless a brief says otherwise. Numbers are `font-mono tabular-nums` and right-aligned (`text-right`), and so is their column header. Rows are divided by 1px borders, with no zebra stripes. The stock `TableBody` drops its last row's border: take `[&_tr:last-child]:border-0` out of it. The rows do nothing, so take `hover:bg-muted/50` out of the stock `TableRow`. Every cell keeps the stock `whitespace-nowrap`. The table's name is the heading above it and the `label`; it has no `<caption>`. The `Table`'s `className` reaches the `<table>` inside the scroll box, so a margin a brief gives the table goes on a plain `<div>` around it (`<div className="mt-6">`). Where the table sits in a grid column, that column gets `min-w-0`; a table straight in a `<section>` needs none. A table that a brief says scrolls on a phone gets the `min-w-*` class the brief gives, so it scrolls in its box and the page does not.
- **Icons:** from `lucide-react`, with `size-4`, `strokeWidth={1.5}` and `aria-hidden="true"`, and only where a brief names one. An icon takes the color of the text beside it (`text-foreground`): never red, even beside an issue or an error. Never emoji.
- **Corners:** hard. `rounded-sm` (1px) for badges and rank marks, `rounded-lg` (3px, the stock corner) for buttons, `rounded-md` (3px as well) for a player's mark, `rounded-xl` (6px) for cards, tiles and pictures. Meter tracks and the shapes in a picture are square. `rounded-full` is for the status dot only, 8px across. No pills, no circles around a person: a player's mark is a square.
- **Surfaces:** flat and dark: no shadow, glow, blur or gradient in the code you write. A card is `bg-card`, one step lighter than the page. A stock component that floats keeps its one short shadow.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, the only transition is color. Nothing moves on scroll, and nothing animates in when it is added to the page. The stock `Button` keeps its `transition-all` and its 1px press nudge, as the rules say.
