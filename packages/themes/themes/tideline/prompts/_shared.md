### How Tideline pages are laid out

- **Color blocks:** a large block of color is `bg-lagoon text-lagoon-foreground` or `bg-coral text-coral-foreground`, the pair always together on the block itself. The two colors and their ink are the same in both modes, so the ink class keeps the text on a block deep-sea ink (`#12262D`) in dark mode too, where the page's own text turns to sand. A block is `rounded-xl p-6 md:p-10` with the fill and the ink, `flex flex-col gap-6` inside (the gap between its groups; a block with one group holds just that group's stack), no border and no shadow. Everything inside it inherits the ink: no element in a block gets a text color class of its own, so never `text-foreground`, `text-muted-foreground` or `text-primary` there, also not on the labels, small figures and small print that the notes below make muted elsewhere. A block holds words and figures only: a heading, a sentence or two, a few facts. Nothing in it takes focus or carries a color of its own: no link, button, field, error line, badge, avatar, icon or card. In dark mode the main button and the focus outline are sun yellow, which is lost on lagoon and coral; the action that goes with a block stands on the sand right after it. A line drawn on a block is 1px in the block's ink: `border-lagoon-foreground` on lagoon, `border-coral-foreground` on coral. One color per block, sand between blocks: two blocks never touch or stand side by side, and a block is never inside a card. Where a block is a whole section, the `<section>` itself carries the block's classes. Where a line or an action follows a block in the same section, the section is a full-width section (below), the block is a `<div>` first in it, and what follows stands on the sand under it.
- **Page:** one `<div>` wraps the whole page with `min-h-svh bg-background text-body text-foreground`. Inside it sit the `<header>`, a `<main>` and the `<footer>`, in this order.
- **Container:** the header's row, the `<main>` and the footer's row each sit in a centered container, `mx-auto max-w-[1248px] px-6`, so the content is at most 1200px wide, the grid in `DESIGN.md`, with 24px at each side at every width. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed.
- **Main:** the `<main>` element itself carries the container classes, `flex flex-col gap-24` (96px between sections) and `pt-10 pb-24`. Its items are the page's sections, in the order the brief gives. The first is always the opening: a page opens on one block of color that runs the width of the container.
- **Sections:** where this brief calls something a section, it is a `<section>` with `aria-labelledby` pointing at its heading; without a visible heading it gets an `aria-label`. There are two kinds, and the brief says which:
  - A full-width section is `flex flex-col gap-6`: the heading (or a heading group) first, the content after it. Everything starts at the left edge of the grid; nothing on these pages is centered.
  - A section of parts side by side is itself the grid: `grid grid-cols-1 gap-10 md:grid-cols-12 md:gap-x-6 md:items-start`, and each part gets the `md:col-span-*` (and `md:col-start-*`) the brief gives it. Below `md` the parts stack in their order, 40px apart.
- **Heading groups:** where a label stands over a heading, the two sit in a `flex flex-col gap-2`, the label first. Where a sentence follows a heading, the heading (or its group) and the sentence sit in a `flex flex-col gap-4`.
- **Rows of three:** three cards or three items side by side are a `grid grid-cols-1 gap-6 lg:grid-cols-3`: one column up to `lg`, three from there, `gap-6` in both directions. Cards in one row stretch to the same height. Two cards side by side are `grid grid-cols-1 gap-6 md:grid-cols-2`.
- **Vertical rhythm:** written as gaps on stacks, never as top margins and never as `space-y-*`. Every element that holds more than one thing in a column is `flex flex-col` with one of these gaps: `gap-2` between a label and what it labels, between a figure and the note under it, and between a field's label, the field, its helper text and its error; `gap-4` between a heading and its paragraph, between paragraphs, and between the parts inside a card; `gap-6` between the fields of a form, between a section's heading and its content, between cards of the same kind, and between the groups of a color block; `gap-10` between the parts of a long read; `gap-24` between sections, on the `<main>`. A row of things side by side is `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-4`, or `gap-2` for things that belong together (an icon and its word, a figure and its unit). Where this brief gives no gap, use the one of these that fits. The only margins these pages use are `mt-auto`, to push the last part of a card to its foot, and the `mt-1` that sets an icon on the first line of its text.
- **Header:** the first thing on the page is a `<header>` with `border-b border-border`, running the full width. Inside it, one `<div>` carries both the container classes and the row's classes, `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-x-6 py-2`. On the left the school's short name, "Sandhopper", a link to `/` in `inline-flex h-11 items-center font-heading text-h2 text-foreground`, never marked as the current page. Then the `<nav>`, the page's only one, with the `aria-label` "Main" and `w-full md:ml-auto md:w-auto`, so from `md` up it sits at the row's right end and below `md` it takes a line of its own under the name. It holds a `<ul>` with `flex flex-wrap gap-x-6` and four links, in this order: "Lessons" to `/lessons`, "Forecast" to `/forecast`, "Guide" to `/guide`, "Book" to `/booking`. Each link is `inline-flex h-11 items-center font-mono text-label uppercase text-foreground underline-offset-4 hover:underline`. The current page's link, where the brief names one, keeps its address and adds `aria-current="page"` and `underline`. The four fit on one line at 375px, so there is no "Menu" button.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` with `border-t border-border`, running the full width. Inside it, one `<div>` carries both the container classes and `flex flex-col gap-6 py-10 md:flex-row md:items-start md:justify-between`. First a `flex flex-col gap-2`: the name as a `<p>` in `font-heading text-h2`, "Sandhopper Surf School" (not a heading and not a link), and a `<p>` in `text-body`: "Surf lessons and kit hire at Pentarrow Sands since 2009." - **Headings:** the `<h1>` is `text-h1`, and `text-display` from `md` up where this brief says so (`text-h1 md:text-display`). An `<h2>` is `text-h2`. An `<h3>` is `text-h2` too, unless this brief gives it `text-body font-bold`. Headings get Epilogue on their own; any other element in `text-display`, `text-h1` or `text-h2` adds `font-heading`. The type class sets the weight (800, 800, 700): add no weight class to text in those three sizes. Short, in sentence case, no full stop.
- **Labels:** nav links, field labels, button and badge text, table headers, the label over a heading or a figure, units, and short lines of facts (a length, a group size, an age, a distance) are labels, except the value in a row of a `<dl>`, which is body text as the Rows note says: `font-mono text-label uppercase`. Write them in sentence case in the code; the class sets the capitals. A label is a short fragment, never a sentence. Parts of a label line are joined with ` · `. A label on its own line is a block element such as a `<p>`.
- **Small print:** helper text under a field, an error line and a caption that is a whole sentence are small print: `font-mono text-label`, without `uppercase`, because a sentence in capitals is hard to read.
- **Large figures:** a figure a visitor plans by (a tide time, a wave height, a wind speed, a price that is the point of a card, a total) is the number in `font-heading tabular-nums` at the size this brief gives (`text-h1` or `text-h2`), and its unit beside it as a label, each a `<span>` in one element with `flex flex-wrap items-baseline gap-x-2` (a `<p>`, or the `<dd>` in a set of conditions): `3` and `ft`, `8` and `mph`, `15` and `°C`, `13:34` and `low`. A price carries its pound sign in the number (`£42`), and what it is for is the label beside it (`a person`). On sand and on a card the number is `text-foreground` and the unit `text-muted-foreground`; on a color block neither has a color class.
- **Conditions:** a set of conditions is a `<dl>` on a color block: `grid grid-cols-2 gap-6 md:grid-cols-4`. Each fact is a `<div>` with `flex flex-col gap-2 border-t pt-4` and the block's ink for the line (`border-lagoon-foreground`), holding a `<dt>` (a label: `Tide`, `Swell`, `Wind`, `Water`), a `<dd>` with the large figure and its unit, and a second `<dd>` with the fact's note, a label like the first (`9 s · From the west`). Nothing in it has a text color class: it stands on a block.
- **Units:** tide heights in metres (`1.5 m`), swell in feet (`3 ft`), wind in miles an hour (`8 mph`), the water in degrees Celsius (`15 °C`), the time between waves in seconds (`9 s`), a length of time as `2 h` or `2 h 30 min`. A space stands between the number and its unit.
- **Small figures:** a time, a date, a height, a distance or a count that this brief calls a small figure is `font-mono text-label uppercase tabular-nums text-muted-foreground`, on the element that holds the whole figure.
- **Text color:** on sand and on cards, headings, body text, large figures, prices, field labels and the names of things are `text-foreground`. Other labels, small figures, captions and helper text are `text-muted-foreground`. Paragraphs are never muted. Muted text stands only on sand or on a card, never on a fill. Lagoon, coral, sun yellow and orange are fills, never text colors. On a color block every text inherits the block's ink, as the first note says.
- **Writing:** plain and friendly, in short sentences, addressing the visitor as "you" and written by the school as "we". Give the facts a visitor plans by: time, place, price, what to bring. No exclamation marks. An error says what to do.
- **Measure:** a paragraph of body text is at most `max-w-xl` wide.
- **Dates and times:** a date or a clock time is a `<time>` with a `dateTime` wherever it appears, in a sentence, a label, a figure, a table cell or the trigger of a date field too: `<time dateTime="2027-06-17">Thursday 17 June 2027</time>`, `<time dateTime="13:34">13:34</time>`. A date is written with the weekday, the day, the month in words and the year, with no comma (`Thursday 17 June 2027`), or shorter only where this brief shows it (`Thu 17 Jun`, `15 May`). A clock time is 24-hour with two-digit hours, `06:40`, `13:34`, and takes `tabular-nums`. A span of hours is two `<time>`s joined by an en dash: `08:30–18:00`. A length of time (`2 h 30 min`) is not a `<time>`. A copyright year is plain text.
- **Prices:** whole pounds with the pound sign and no pence: `£42`, `£5`. Never a price without its currency.
- **Links:** links are the text color, underlined: a link in running text is `text-foreground underline decoration-1 underline-offset-4 hover:decoration-2`, and inherits its size. A link whose destination this brief does not name points to `#`. Links stand on sand or on a card, never on a color block.
- **Added components:** a component you add with `npx shadcn@latest add` during the build (an `Input`, a `Textarea`, a `Select`, a `Popover`, a `Calendar`, a `Badge`, a `Table`) gets the `cn` import, the focus outline only if it takes focus on the page, and the edits these notes give its file.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="h-11 rounded-md px-6 font-mono text-label uppercase"`: 44px tall, 12px corners, an uppercase mono label of two to four words, written in sentence case in the code. The default variant (deep-sea charcoal, sun yellow in dark mode) is for the page's one main action; the brief names it, and no page has two. Every other button is `variant="secondary"`, sun yellow with ink (sand with ink in dark mode). Its stock hover mixes in the foreground and can hardly be told from its resting color: in `components/ui/button.tsx`, replace the `secondary` variant's `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]` with `hover:bg-secondary/80`, once, on every page whether or not it shows a secondary button. Quiet buttons (the unchosen ones in a group, a stepper's minus and plus) are `variant="outline"`; on a page that uses one, in the `outline` variant replace `bg-background` with `bg-card` and take out `dark:bg-input/30`, so it is the card's color in both modes, on sand and on a card, its hover classes unchanged. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. A button that its own click disables gets `focusableWhenDisabled`, so keyboard focus stays on it: only a stepper's minus and plus. A button below `md` is as wide as its column where this brief says so (`w-full md:w-auto`). Buttons stand on sand or on a card, never on a color block.
- **Button groups:** a set of buttons where one is chosen is a `role="group"` with `flex flex-wrap gap-2`, named by its visible label (`aria-labelledby` pointing at the label's `id`). The chosen button has `aria-pressed="true"` and `variant="secondary"`; the others have `aria-pressed="false"` and `variant="outline"`. Choosing moves no focus. Not `Tabs`: there are no tab panels.
- **Steppers:** a number set with minus and plus buttons is a `role="group"` named by its visible label (`aria-labelledby`), with `flex items-center gap-2`: a minus `Button`, the value, a plus `Button`. Each button is `variant="outline" size="icon"` with `className="size-11 rounded-md"` (not the text button's classes) and an `aria-label` that says what it does, holding the `Minus` or `Plus` icon. The value is a `<span>` in `inline-flex min-w-24 justify-center font-mono text-label uppercase text-foreground tabular-nums` with `aria-live="polite"`, written with its noun (`2 people`). At the lowest and highest value the matching button is `disabled` with `focusableWhenDisabled`.
- **Inputs:** the shadcn `Input` with `className="h-11 rounded-md border-muted-foreground px-4 text-body md:text-body"`, and the `Textarea` with `className="min-h-32 rounded-md border-muted-foreground px-4 py-2 text-body md:text-body"`. The stock `border-input` is the card's own pale border, too faint to find a field by; `border-muted-foreground` is clear in both modes. The stock `focus-visible:border-ring` and `aria-invalid:border-destructive` stay. Every field has a visible `<label>` above it with `block`, a label in `text-foreground`, tied to it with `htmlFor`. The label, the field, helper text and the error sit in a `flex flex-col gap-2`. Helper text is small print in `text-muted-foreground`, and the field names it in `aria-describedby`. A field that may be left empty says so in its label: `A note (optional)`. A date is never an `Input` of type `date`: it is the date field below.

- **Forms:** a form sits in a card, the card is the wrapper for the form and the confirmation that replaces it, and the form's heading sits above the card, in the part's stack, not inside it. In place of the card's column stack, such a card is a plain block (`rounded-xl border border-border bg-card p-6` with no `flex`), holding the `<form>` and, after it, the status element. The `<form>` has `noValidate`, an `aria-label` and `flex flex-col gap-6`; its last item is the submit button, full width below `md` (`w-full md:w-auto md:self-start`).
- **Errors:** an error is a line under its field, inside the field's stack, after any helper text: a `<p>` with an `id` and `flex items-start gap-2 font-mono text-label text-destructive`, holding the `CircleAlert` icon (`size-4 shrink-0`, `aria-hidden="true"`; `items-start` sets it on the first line) and one sentence that says what to do. The field gets `aria-invalid` and adds the line's `id` to its `aria-describedby`, beside the helper text it already names. Fields are checked in code when the form is sent, and focus moves to the first field with an error; before the first send no error shows. To focus a field in the same step that adds its error line, set the errors in state and move the focus in an effect that runs after that render (or wrap the state change in `flushSync`), so the line is in the page when focus lands. An email is well formed when, trimmed, it matches `/^[^\s@]+@[^\s@]+\.[^\s@]+$/`. An error line and its field's `aria-invalid` are removed when that field changes (on `input` or `change`); every other error stays until the next send.
- **Confirmations:** a confirmation is plain text in `text-foreground`, no colored box: a heading at the level this brief gives and a sentence or two. It goes into an element with `role="status"` that is in the card from the start, empty, so that its message is announced. Give the message's heading `tabIndex={-1}` and move focus to it, because the button that had focus is gone.
- **Cards:** a card is a plain element with `rounded-xl border border-border bg-card p-6` (white on sand, 20px corners, 24px padding, a 1px border, no shadow) and `flex flex-col gap-4` inside, unless this brief says otherwise. Not the shadcn `Card`, which ships its own padding and a ring. Never a card inside a card, never a bordered box inside a card, and never a color block inside a card: divide a card's parts with a 1px `border-t border-border` and `pt-4`.
- **Rows:** a list of terms and values (a summary, the facts about a place) is a `<dl>` with `divide-y divide-border`, so a 1px rule falls between rows and none above the first or below the last. Each row is a `<div>` with `flex items-baseline justify-between gap-6 py-4 first:pt-0 last:pb-0`: the term, a `<dt>`, is a label in `text-muted-foreground` with `shrink-0`; the value, a `<dd>`, is `min-w-0 text-right text-body text-foreground`, so a long value wraps inside its own cell at 375px. Where this brief says the rows are stacked, each row is `flex flex-col gap-2 py-4 first:pt-0 last:pb-0` in place of that, the term over the value, the term has no `shrink-0` and the value has no `text-right`.
- **Tables:** the shadcn `Table` (`npx shadcn@latest add table`) with `className="text-body"`, in a card that has no padding and `overflow-hidden` (`overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-border bg-card`, with no `flex` and no `gap-4`), so the table touches the card's edges. Header cells are labels in `text-muted-foreground` with `h-auto px-6 py-4` and `scope="col"`; body cells have `px-6 py-4` and `text-foreground`, and a cell that holds a figure (a time, a height, a speed, a price) adds `tabular-nums`. Figures in a table stay in the body font, with their unit: `1.5 m`, `£10`. Rows do not change on hover: `hover:bg-transparent` on every `TableRow`, the header's too. Stock cells do not wrap, so a wide table scrolls sideways in its own box. In `components/ui/table.tsx`, give that box `tabIndex={0}`, `role="region"`, an `aria-label` and the outline classes of the Focus rule, written with `focus-visible:-outline-offset-2` in place of `focus-visible:outline-offset-2` so the outline is drawn inside the box, which fills a card that clips (the stock box has no focus classes), and add a required `label` prop for the name.
