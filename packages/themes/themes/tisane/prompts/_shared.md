### How Tisane pages are laid out

- **Container:** one `<div>` wraps the whole page and is the container: `mx-auto max-w-[1120px]` with `px-4`, and `px-6` from `md` up, plus `text-body`. This width is the one arbitrary value allowed. Inside it sit the `<header>`, a `<main>` holding everything between the two, and the `<footer>`.
- **Grid:** where this brief puts things side by side, they are items of a grid: `grid gap-6 md:grid-cols-12`, a single column below `md`. "Columns 1–6" is `md:col-span-6`; "columns 8–12" is `md:col-span-5 md:col-start-8`, and so on. The grid's `gap-6` is the space between its items in both directions, also when they stack, so a grid item never has a top margin. Where this brief says a section is "a grid", or "in columns 1–8 of a grid", the `<section>` itself carries the grid classes and its `mt-18`; in the second case one `<div>` in it spans those columns and holds the content. Add `md:items-start` to a grid of two or more items, unless this brief says they line up; a grid of one item needs none.
- **Measure:** a paragraph of body text is at most `max-w-xl` wide (576px).
- **Vertical rhythm:** written as a top margin on each element, never as `space-y-*` and never as a gap on a stack of text. These are the only distances:
  - Between sections: every `<section>` that is not inside another `<section>` has `mt-18` (72px), except the first, which has `pt-10` and no top margin. A `<section>` nested inside one of them takes the margin this brief gives it, never `mt-18`. The footer has `mt-18`.
  - A heading under a label, and a label under the thing it labels (a caption under a picture): `mt-2`.
  - Text under a heading, and a paragraph after a paragraph: `mt-4`.
  - A block after text or after another block (a grid, a list, a table, a card, a form, a picture, a button on its own line): `mt-6`.
  - An `<h2>` or `<h3>` that opens a new part inside a section, after content: `mt-10`.
  - The first element inside any box (a section, a grid item, a card, a list item, a form) has no top margin.
  - A link or a button on a line of its own sits in a `<p>` or a `<div>`, which carries the margin.
  These margins rely on normal block flow. Inside a grid item or a card, do not make the content `flex flex-col` with a gap.
- **Header:** the `<header>` is itself the row, with `py-6` and no border: `flex flex-col items-start gap-2 md:flex-row md:items-baseline md:justify-between`. On the left the tea room's name, a link to `/` in `font-heading text-h2`, styled as a header link and never marked as the current page. On the right a `<nav>` with the `aria-label` "Main", the page's only `<nav>`, holding a `<ul>` with `flex flex-wrap gap-x-6 gap-y-2` and four `<li>`s, each one link in `text-body`: Menu (`/menu`), Book a table (`/booking`), Journal (`/journal`), Visit (`/visit`). Every link to these places uses these addresses. Below `md` the links wrap onto a second line; they fit at 375px, so there is no "Menu" button. Mark one link as current only where this brief names it: it keeps its address and gets `aria-current="page"`.
- **Footer:** a `<footer>` with `mt-18`. Inside it one `<div>` with `border-t py-10`, which is not the grid but holds two things. First a grid `<div>` (`grid gap-6 md:grid-cols-12 md:items-start`): in columns 1–6 a `<div>` with the tea room's name as a `<p>` in `font-heading text-h2` (not a heading) and, with `mt-2`, one sentence about it; in columns 8–12 a `<div>` with an `<address className="not-italic">` holding three block `<span>`s, `14 Mill Lane`, `Ashby Wold`, `AW3 7LN`, then with `mt-4` a `<p>` label, `Tuesday to Sunday · Closed Mondays`. Then, under the grid with `mt-6`, a `<p>` label: `© 2026 The Linden Room`.
- **Headings:** the `<h1>` is `text-h1`, and `text-display` from `md` up only where this brief says so (`text-h1 md:text-display`). An `<h2>` is `text-h2`. An `<h3>` is `text-body`, in the heading font it gets on its own, unless this brief gives it `text-h2`. Young Serif has one weight and no italic: never put `font-bold`, `font-semibold` or `font-medium` on a heading or on any text in `font-heading`, and never slant it. Sentence case, no full stop.
- **Labels:** prices, times, dates, weights and measures, dietary marks, captions, table headers, form labels, and button and badge text are labels: `font-mono text-label`, in sentence case. A label is a short fragment, except helper text under a field and any line this brief writes as a sentence. Nothing is uppercased. A label on its own line is a block element such as a `<p>`. Parts of a label line are joined with ` · `.
- **Dates and times:** a date or a clock time is a `<time>` with a `dateTime` wherever it appears, in a sentence or a summary too: `<time dateTime="2026-11-14">14 November 2026</time>`, `<time dateTime="11:30">11:30</time>`. A `dateTime` always has two-digit hours, while the text has none: `<time dateTime="09:00">9:00</time>`. A date is written day, month in words, year (`14 November 2026`), with the weekday in front (`Saturday 14 November 2026`) only where this brief shows one. In a label or a table, a span of hours is two `<time>` elements joined by an en dash: `9:00–17:00`; in a sentence it reads "from 11:30 to 14:30", each time a `<time>`. A copyright year is plain text. "Today" is always the visitor's own clock, never a sample date.
- **Prices and amounts:** a price, an amount in a recipe or a time in a list is a label in `text-foreground` with `tabular-nums`; in a list of rows it stands at the right end of its row. Prices have the pound sign and two decimals: `£4.20`.
- **Text color:** headings, body text, prices, amounts and form labels are `text-foreground`, and so is any label this brief gives `text-foreground`. Every other label is `text-muted-foreground`: captions, dates, dietary marks, helper text, the label above a heading or at the top of a card, and the footer's hours and © lines. A button's text color comes from its variant. Paragraphs are never muted. `text-primary` (olive) is only for links and for the word that marks today in a table of hours.
- **Links:** a link with no destination named in this brief or these notes points to `#`. A link in running text is `text-primary underline decoration-1 underline-offset-4 hover:text-foreground` and inherits its size. A link in the header or the footer is `text-foreground decoration-1 underline-offset-4 hover:underline`; the current page's link also has `underline`. Links get `transition-colors duration-150`.
- **Added components:** a component you add with `npx shadcn@latest add` during the build (an `Input`, a `Table`, a `Badge`) gets the `cn` import, the focus outline only if it takes focus on the page, and the edits these notes give its file.
- **Buttons:** the shadcn `Button` with `className="h-auto rounded-md px-6 py-2 font-mono text-label"`. The default variant (olive) is for the page's one main action. Any other button is `variant="secondary"` (olive-black, cream in dark mode). Its stock hover mixes in the foreground, the same color here: in `components/ui/button.tsx` replace the `secondary` variant's `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_oklch,var(--secondary),var(--foreground)_5%)]` with `hover:bg-secondary/80`, once, and add nothing to the button's `className`. Quiet buttons (the unchosen ones in a group, a stepper's plus and minus) are `variant="outline"`. A button that sends a form needs `type="submit"`: this `Button` is `type="button"` unless told otherwise. A button that its own click disables gets `focusableWhenDisabled`, so focus stays on it: only a stepper's minus and plus. Base UI then sets `aria-disabled="true"` and `data-disabled` instead of `disabled`, so the stock `disabled:` classes do nothing: on a page that uses the prop, and only there, add `data-disabled:pointer-events-none data-disabled:opacity-50` beside them in `components/ui/button.tsx`. A call to action that goes to another page is an `<a>` with the same classes: write `cn(buttonVariants({ variant }), "…")`. Button text is two or three words; a filter's choices are one or two.
- **Button groups:** a set of buttons where one is chosen (a filter) is a `role="group"` with an `aria-label` and `flex flex-wrap gap-2`. The chosen button has `aria-pressed="true"` and `variant="secondary"`; the rest have `aria-pressed="false"` and `variant="outline"`.
- **Steppers:** a number set with minus and plus buttons is a `role="group"` named by its visible label (`aria-labelledby` pointing at the label's `id`), with `flex items-center gap-2`: a minus `Button`, the value, a plus `Button`. Each button is `variant="outline" size="icon"` (32px, as it ships: above the 24px WCAG target) with only `rounded-md` added (not the text button's classes) and an `aria-label` that says what it does ("Fewer people"), holding the `Minus` or `Plus` icon. The value is a `<span>` in `inline-flex min-w-24 justify-center font-mono text-label text-foreground tabular-nums` with `aria-live="polite"`, written with its unit (`3 people`), centered between the buttons. At the lowest and highest value the matching button is `disabled` with `focusableWhenDisabled`.
- **Inputs:** the shadcn `Input` and `Textarea` with `className="h-auto rounded-md px-4 py-2 text-body md:text-body"`. Their fill is `bg-card`, the select's: in `components/ui/input.tsx` and `components/ui/textarea.tsx`, replace the stock `bg-transparent` and `dark:bg-input/30` with `bg-card` (in `input.tsx`, `file:bg-transparent` stays), and delete `dark:aria-invalid:border-destructive/50`, so a field's error border matches the select's in dark mode. Every field has a visible `<label>` above it with `block`, a label in `text-foreground`, tied to it with `htmlFor`, and the field has `mt-2`. A field that may be left empty says so: `Note (optional)`. A date field is an `Input` of type `date`.
- **Selects:** a choice from a short list is a native `<select>` with `h-auto w-full rounded-md border border-input bg-card px-4 py-2 text-body text-foreground aria-invalid:border-destructive`. Its first option is an empty choice in words ("Choose a time"). It gets the same label, `mt-2`, focus outline and error handling as any field: with an error, `aria-invalid` turns its border brick red, as the stock `Input` does.
- **Forms:** a form sits in a card, so its fields and select stand on cream. Each field sits in its own `<div>` with its label and the lines under it; these `<div>`s are `mt-6` apart (the first has none), and the button that sends the form sits in a `<div>` with `mt-6`.
- **Errors:** an error is a line under its field with `mt-2`: `flex items-start gap-2 font-mono text-label text-destructive`, holding the `CircleAlert` icon (`shrink-0`; `items-start` sets it at the top of the first line) and a sentence that says what to do. Where a field and a button share a row, it sits under the row. The field gets `aria-invalid` and points to the line with `aria-describedby`. Fields are checked in code when the form is sent, with `noValidate` on the `<form>`, and focus moves to the first field with an error. Before the first send no error shows. An error line is removed when its own field changes (on `input` or `change`); every other error stays until the next send.
- **Confirmations:** a confirmation is plain text in `text-foreground`: a heading at the level this brief gives and a sentence or two, no colored box. It goes into an element with `role="status"` that is on the page from the start, empty, so that its message is announced. The form and the status element share one wrapper, the card, so the message stands where the form stood. The status element has no classes and no margin, sits after the form in the card's block flow, and is never hidden (no `hidden`, no `empty:hidden`), so empty it takes no space. The message in it is a `<div>` rendered only when there is a message. Where text stays above it in the card, the message `<div>` has `mt-6`, the margin the form had; where it is the card's only content, it has none. A link or a button that follows a confirmation sits beside the status element in the card, not inside it. Give the message's heading `tabIndex={-1}` and move focus to it.
- **Badges:** the shadcn `Badge` with `className="h-auto rounded-sm border-transparent bg-tertiary px-2 py-1 font-mono text-label text-tertiary-foreground"`. Peach is a fill only: never `text-tertiary` or a peach border. One or two words, no icon. At most one badge on a page.
- **Cards:** a card is a plain element with `rounded-xl border bg-card p-6`, not the shadcn `Card`, which ships its own padding and ring. Inside a card, a label comes first where there is one, and the text follows with `mt-2`. Never nest cards. A card with a picture at its top is `overflow-hidden rounded-xl border bg-card` with no padding: the picture is the plain `<div>` alone, with no `<figure>`, no caption and no corners or border of its own, and fills the card's top edge. The text under it sits in a `<div>` with `p-6`.
- **Pictures:** there are no image files. A picture is a `<figure>` holding a plain `<div>` with an aspect ratio (`aspect-4/3`, `aspect-3/2` or `aspect-square`, as this brief says), `rounded-xl bg-muted`, `role="img"` and an `aria-label` that says what it shows, and a `<figcaption>` under it, a label in `text-muted-foreground` with `mt-2`. The `<figcaption>` is the figure's last child: anything else about the picture goes after the `<figure>`. Draw nothing inside unless this brief asks. A picture in a card is the exception given under Cards.
- **Rows:** a list where each row has a name on the left and a mono value on the right (a menu, ingredients, hours) is a `<ul>` with no markers and no padding, and `divide-y`, so a 1px rule falls between rows and none above the first or below the last. Each `<li>` is `flex items-baseline justify-between gap-4 py-4`, unless this brief gives `py-2`; the value has `shrink-0`. A list in running text is `list-disc` or `list-decimal` with `pl-6`, and `mt-2` between its items.
- **Tables:** the shadcn `Table` with `className="text-body"`. Header cells are labels in `text-muted-foreground` with `h-auto px-4 py-2`; body cells have `px-4 py-2 whitespace-normal`; the first and last cell of a row add `first:pl-0 last:pr-0`. Rows do not change on hover: `hover:bg-transparent` on every row. The stock `Table` wraps the `<table>` in a box that scrolls sideways; make it reachable with the keyboard even where the table fits: in `components/ui/table.tsx`, give that box `tabIndex={0}`, `role="region"` and an `aria-label`, and add a required `label` prop for the name. `label` and a visually hidden `<caption>` (`sr-only`) repeat the words of the heading above the table.
- **Rules:** every line is 1px and `border-border`.
- **Corners:** `rounded-sm` (6px) for badges and small tags, `rounded-md` (12px) for buttons, inputs and selects, `rounded-xl` (20px, the spec's `lg`) for cards and pictures. `rounded-lg` is 12px too; write `rounded-md`. No `rounded-full`.
- **Surfaces:** flat and matte. No shadows, blur, gradients or glows in the code you write. A stock component that floats over the page keeps the one soft shadow it ships with.
- **Icons:** from `lucide-react`, with `size-4`, `strokeWidth={1.5}` and `aria-hidden="true"`, only where this brief or these notes name one. Never emoji.
- **Motion:** in the code you write, the only transition is color. Nothing moves on scroll. Stock components keep the motion they ship with.
