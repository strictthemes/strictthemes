# Code of conduct

This project is a place to work on themes and tools, and everyone who takes
part in it should be able to do that without being made unwelcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Talk about the work: the code, the design, the wording. Not about the
  person.
- Accept that people disagree, and that a maintainer has the last word on
  what is merged.
- Say so when you were wrong. Everyone is, often.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, mockery or attacks on a person or a group.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private details without their permission.

## Where this applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and
anywhere else someone speaks for the project.

## Reporting

To report behaviour that breaks this code, write to the maintainers in
private: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a
vulnerability**. It is the one private channel the project has for now, and
only the maintainers read what is sent there. Say what happened and where.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who
do not follow this code, for a time or for good.
